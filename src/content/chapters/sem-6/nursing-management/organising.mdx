## In simple words

Organising is arranging tasks, people and equipment so that the plan can be carried out. Every hospital has an organisational chart showing who reports to whom.

## Steps in organising

1. Identify and group the activities to be done
2. Assign activities to people and departments
3. Define authority and responsibility
4. Establish relationships and communication channels
5. Obtain and arrange equipment and supplies

## Hospital departments

| Department | Work |
|---|---|
| Medical | Consultation and nursing care |
| Surgical | Operation theatres and post-operative care |
| Laboratory and imaging | Diagnostics |
| Pharmacy | Medicines and stores |
| Nursing | Inpatient and outpatient care |
| Support | Dietary, laundry, housekeeping, records |
| Administration | HR, finance, legal |

## Nursing department hierarchy

**Nursing superintendent → deputy nursing superintendent → ward in-charge / charge nurse → staff nurse → nursing assistant**

<Callout type="must" title="Empowerment, not overload">
  Duty that is allocated must be **supported with knowledge, information and equipment**. Unclear authority causes overload, error and complaints.
</Callout>
