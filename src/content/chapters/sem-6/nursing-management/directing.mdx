## In simple words

Directing is telling staff what to do and supporting them to do it well. Supervision is checking that care is being given safely — kindly, specifically and with feedback.

## Elements of directing

- Clear instructions and priorities
- Communication in both directions
- Motivation and recognition
- Delegation with support
- Fair discipline
- Role modelling

## Supervision rounds

- Check care quality: patient, notes, dressing, feeding, medication rounds
- Watch practice: hand hygiene, infection control, drug safety
- Supervise documentation: accuracy and completeness
- Look at environment: lights, ventilation, safety, cleanliness
- Record observations and corrections, then review

## Motivation

- Maslow’s hierarchy: physiological, safety, social, esteem, self-actualisation
- Herzberg: hygiene factors prevent dissatisfaction; motivators create satisfaction
- Recognise work; provide training and career growth; involve staff in decisions

<Callout type="must" title="Praise in public, correct in private">
  Correction given in front of colleagues damages morale, while private, specific feedback protects both **fairness and staff dignity**.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Direct: duty, support, motivation, corrective action.
- Supervise: rounds, observation, records, protocol and environment.
- Maslow and Herzberg explain staff motivation.
- Feedback rules: praise publicly, correct privately.
- Discipline follows policy: fair, progressive, documented.
