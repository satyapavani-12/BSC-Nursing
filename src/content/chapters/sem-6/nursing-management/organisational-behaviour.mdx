## In simple words

Organisational behaviour studies how people behave in organisations. In nursing it explains why some teams work well and others conflict, and how the nurse administrator can support good relations.

## Elements of organisational behaviour

| Element | Focus |
|---|---|
| Individual | Personality, perception, learning, motivation |
| Group | Role, norms, leadership, group decisions |
| Organisational | Structure, culture, power, change |

## Groups in nursing

- **Types:** formal wards, informal peer groups, committees
- **Norms:** unwritten rules for acceptable behaviour
- **Roles:** task roles and support roles; balance is needed
- **Group cohesion** improves safety; fragmented groups miss information

## Communication

- Formal channel: duty lists, records, reports
- Informal channel: personal conversations
- Barriers: withholding facts, hierarchy, overload, cultural differences
- Good communication reduces errors and improves morale

<Callout type="must" title="Human relations are a management tool">
  Warm, fair, clear communication reduces conflict, burnout and turnover. **A unit in which staff can raise concerns without fear is a safer unit.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Individual: personality, perception, learning, motivation.
- Group: roles, norms, cohesion, decisions.
- Communication: formal records and informal talk; avoid barriers.
- Conflict: talk, facts, compromise, fairness.
- Support and debrief staff to prevent burnout.
