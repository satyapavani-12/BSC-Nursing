## In simple words

Leadership is influencing a group to work towards a common goal. Managers keep the system running; leaders change it and inspire people to follow.

## Qualities of a nursing leader

- Clear vision and goals
- Communication and listening
- Decisiveness with consultation
- Role model for professional values
- Supports, teaches and empowers staff
- Delegates and supervises
- Handles conflict calmly
- Accountable and fair

## Leadership styles

| Style | Approach | Best used |
|---|---|---|
| Autocratic | Leader decides, staff execute | Emergencies |
| Democratic | Leader consults, shares decisions | Team improvement, regular care |
| Laissez-faire | Leader gives freedom, minimal direction | Expert, self-directed teams |
| Transformational | Inspires change | Innovation and quality programmes |
| Transactional | Reward and accountability | Routine targets |

## Conflict management

- **Approach:** listen to both sides, look for facts, not fault
- **Techniques:** head-on talk, keeping calm, compromise, referral to policy
- **Avoid:** power games, favouritism, gossip

<Callout type="must" title="Empowerment beats authority">
  A leader builds power through **expertise, trust and fair action** rather than position or fear. Coercive power damages safety because staff stop reporting problems.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Leadership influences; management administers.
- Styles: autocratic, democratic, laissez-faire, transformational.
- Power: position, expertise, reward, coercion.
- Conflict: resolve by facts and compromise.
- Change: involve, teach and reinforce.
