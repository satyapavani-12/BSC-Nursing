## In simple words

Planning decides in advance what will be done, by whom, when, with what resources and how it will be checked. In a nursing unit this is the difference between a calm ward and chaos.

## Steps in planning nursing services

1. Establish objectives — how many patients, level of care
2. Formulate alternatives — staffing, schedules, equipment plans
3. Choose the best alternative
4. Implement the plan
5. Evaluate and revise

## Policy, procedure, protocol

| Item | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Policy | A standing rule that guides decisions |
| Procedure | The steps to be followed in order |
| Protocol | A set of rules for a specific clinical situation |
| Standing orders | Pre-approved orders for defined situations |

## Budgeting for a unit

- Prepare expenditure estimates — salaries, linen, equipment, medicines, training
- Explain variance when actual cost differs from estimate
- Ensure stock, indent and inventory records

<Callout type="must" title="A plan that is not evaluated is not a plan">
  Every plan needs a review point: patient outcomes, complaints, incident reports, consumption and budget variance. **Plan, do, check, act.**
</Callout>
