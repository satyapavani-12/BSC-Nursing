## In simple words

Quality care means doing the right thing, at the right time, in the right way, and getting a safe outcome for every patient.

## Pillars of quality

| Pillar | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Patient safety | No avoidable harm |
| Effectiveness | Evidence-based care |
| Patient-centredness | Care that respects the patient |
| Timeliness | Care without harmful delay |
| Efficiency | No waste of time or supplies |
| Equity | Same quality for the poor and the advantaged |

## Accreditation and standards

- **NABH** — National Accreditation Board for hospitals
- **NQAS** — National Quality Assurance Standards of India
- **Indian Healthcare Foundation (IHF)** — India quality standards
- Accreditation looks at clinical care, records, infection control, staff protection and integrity

## Patient safety programmes

- Medication safety: identify, read, dose, check, record
- Fall prevention: bipedal risk assessment and bed rails
- Infection control: hand hygiene, isolation, bundles
- Safe surgery checklist
- Bar-coding and feedback from incident reports

<Callout type="must" title="System problem, not blame">
  Most errors are system errors. **Correct the system, the procedure or the design of the workflow** rather than blaming one person. A strong safety culture makes staff report near misses.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Quality pillars: safety, effectiveness, timeliness, efficiency, equity, patient-centredness.
- Accreditation: NABH, NQAS, audits.
- Safety programmes: medication, falls, infection, surgery checklists.
- Blame-free, system corrective responses to incidents.
- Care should be measured on outcomes and indicators.
