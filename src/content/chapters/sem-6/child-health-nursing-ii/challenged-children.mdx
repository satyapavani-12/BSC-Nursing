## In simple words

"Challenged child" is a child with a physical, sensory, intellectual or developmental disability that makes ordinary activity harder. Care aims at **maximum ability and inclusion**, not a cure label.

## Types of challenges

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Physical | Cerebral palsy, polio, spina bifida, limb deficiency, amputations |
| Sensory | Blindness and low vision, deafness and hardness of hearing |
| Intellectual | Intellectual impairment, down syndrome, learning disability |
| Developmental | Autism, speech delay, cerebral palsy |
| Chronic illness | Thalassaemia, renal failure, congenital heart disease requiring long-term care |
| Behavioural and emotional | ADHD, severe anxiety, conduct problems |

## Rehabilitation

- **Medical** — treat complications, epilepsy, contractures and pain
- **Physical** — physiotherapy, occupational therapy, orthoses, prostheses
- **Communication** — speech therapy, sign language, hearing aids
- **Psychosocial** — counselling, play therapy, vocational training
- **Educational** — special education and inclusive schools
- **Community-based** — home training and parent groups

## Law and rights

- **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016** — 21 disabilities, reservations in education, employment and accessibility
- **National Trust Act** — guardianship and welfare of persons with autism, cerebral palsy and mental retardation
- **Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan** and inclusive education
- **Adoption** is permitted for couples under the Hindu Adoption and Maintenance Act, 1956 and the Juvenile Justice Act

<Callout type="must" title="Ability first, disability second">
  Describe and treat the whole child. **Assess what the child can do and build from there.** Never let a physical disability be a reason for missing school, immunisation or play.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Types of challenges: physical, sensory, intellectual, developmental, chronic.
- Rehabilitation: medical, physical, communication, psychosocial, education.
- Family support, home training and respite.
- Inclusive education under RPwD Act, 2016.
- Assess ability, set realistic goals, promote independence.
