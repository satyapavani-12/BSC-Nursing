## In simple words

Child Health Nursing II deals with sick and challenged children. The commonest problems are orthopaedic, eye, ear, nose, throat and skin conditions — most are treatable if recognised early.

## Orthopaedic disorders

| Disorder | Key points |
|---|---|
| Club foot (talipes equinovarus) | Foot inverted and turned inward; manipulation, serial casting, Ponseti method |
| Hip dysplasia | Barlow and Ortolani tests; Pavlik harness |
| Scoliosis | Lateral curve; screening in adolescence |
| Cerebral palsy | Non-progressive motor disability; physiotherapy and family teaching |
| Rickets and bowing of legs | Vitamin D and calcium deficiency; confirm by X-ray |
| Osteomyelitis / septic arthritis | Fever, painful swollen limb; antibiotics, drainage |

## Eye disorders in children

- **Conjunctivitis** — redness and sticky discharge; hand hygiene, tetracycline eye ointment
- **Squint (strabismus)** — eyes not aligned; occlusion therapy and surgery
- **Cataract** — congenital or traumatic; early surgery delays blindness
- **Vitamin A deficiency** — night blindness, Bitot spots, keratomalacia
- **Refractive errors** — screen and correct with spectacles
- **School screening** — vision testing at school age

## ENT in children

- **Acute otitis media** — ear pain, fever; antibiotics and ear drop hygiene
- **Chronic otitis media with discharge** — keep ear dry; refer for surgery if persistent
- **Foreign body in ear or nose** — do not push deeper; refer for safe removal
- **Tonsillitis and pharyngitis** — sore throat, fever; symptomatic care
- **Hearing loss** — screen in newborns; early intervention protects speech

## Skin disorders

| Condition | Features | Care |
|---|---|---|
| Eczema | Dry, itchy, recurrent patches | Emollients, avoid triggers, topical steroids |
| Scabies | Itching at night, web spaces, family members | Permethrin cream; treat whole family |
| Impetigo | Honey-crusted sores | Antibiotic cream, hygiene |
| Pediculosis | Itching and nits in hair | Pediculicide, wash clothes |
| Candidiasis (nappy rash) | Red rash in the nappy area | Keep dry, frequent changes, antifungal cream |

<Callout type="must" title="Keep the ear dry">
  **Never put a cotton bud or stick in the ear canal** and never drop oil or water into a discharging ear. Recurrent ear discharge in a child needs referral — it can cause deafness.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Orthopaedic: club foot, hip dysplasia, rickets, cerebral palsy.
- Eye: conjunctivitis, squint, cataract, vitamin A deficiency, screening.
- ENT: otitis media, foreign bodies, tonsillitis, hearing loss.
- Skin: eczema, scabies, impetigo, pediculosis, nappy rash.
- Golden rules: never put anything in the ear; treat all contacts.
