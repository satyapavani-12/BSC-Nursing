## In simple words

Substance use disorders are the repeated use of alcohol or drugs despite harm. Dependence means the body has adapted and stopping causes **withdrawal**.

## Common substances

| Substance | Effects of intoxication | Withdrawal |
|---|---|---|
| Alcohol | Slurred speech, ataxia, sedation, disinhibition | Tremor, sweating, agitation, seizures, delirium tremens |
| Opioids (heroin, morphine) | Pinpoint pupils, drowsiness, slow breathing | Flu-like body ache, vomiting, anxiety, craving |
| Cannabis | Euphoria, tachycardia, anxiety | Irritability, insomnia, craving |
| Benzodiazepines | Sedation, ataxia | Anxiety, insomnia, seizures |
| Stimulants (cocaine, amphetamine) | Euphoria, agitation, psychosis | Fatigue, depression, craving |

## Dependence and problems

- **Psychological** — craving, loss of control, using despite harm
- **Physical** — tolerance and withdrawal symptoms
- **Complications** — liver damage, gastritis, neuropathy, malnutrition, accidents, family breakdown
- **Alcohol withdrawal delirium (delirium tremens)** — confusion, hallucinations, sweating, fever; a medical emergency

## Management

- **Detoxification** — replace thiamine, correct fluids and electrolytes
- **Medication** — disulfiram, acamprosate, naltrexone for alcohol; methadone maintenance for opioids; nicotine replacement for tobacco
- **Psychological** — motivational interviewing, counselling, CBT, group therapy
- **Family** — counselling and support groups such as AA
- **Prevention** — school and workplace programmes, legal restrictions

<Callout type="must" title="Watch for alcohol withdrawal seizures">
  **Tremor, sweating and agitation within 72 hours of stopping alcohol** may progress to seizures and delirium tremens. Monitor patients closely; give thiamine before glucose and benzodiazepines as prescribed.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Ward and emergency** — manage intoxication and withdrawal safely
- **Detox units** — monitor vital signs, nutrition, sleep and seizures
- **Rehabilitation** — group work, counselling, aftercare, relapse prevention
- **Community** — school health education, tobacco cessation, awareness

## 60-second revision

- Substances: alcohol, opioids, cannabis, stimulants, benzodiazepines.
- Dependence = tolerance and withdrawal.
- Withdrawal: tremor, sweating, seizures, delirium tremens within 72 hours.
- Treatment: detox, thiamine, disulfiram, acamprosate, naltrexone, NRT.
- Care: observe vital signs, nutrition, counselling, family support.
