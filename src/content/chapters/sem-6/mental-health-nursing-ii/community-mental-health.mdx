## In simple words

Community mental health nursing takes care to people in their own homes and villages, so that illness is found early, treatment continues and the person stays with the family rather than only in a hospital.

## Scope of community mental health

- Promote mental health and prevent relapse
- Identify early signs and crisis risk
- Support treatment adherence and follow-up
- Care for special groups: children, older people, disaster-affected, alcohol users
- Rehabilitate and integrate into family and work
- Educate the community and reduce stigma
- Train and supervise community workers
- Link to DMHP, NGO and referral services

## Community mental health services in India

- **District Mental Health Programme (DMHP)** — outpatient and outreach services in each district
- **Mobile mental health teams** visit villages
- **Day-care and halfway homes**
- **Occupational and sheltered work** units
- **NFAP (National Facility for Active Aging Programme)** and old-age homes
- **School-based** mental health programmes

## Family burden and support

- Family members carry most chronic psychiatric care and often feel exhausted
- Assess burden, health and support needs at each visit
- Provide respite, support groups and counselling
- Address physical care, nutrition and medicines
- Watch for abuse of the patient or neglect by the family

<Callout type="must" title="Keep the patient in the community wherever possible">
  Community care maintains **dignity, family life and work** better than long institutional care. Rehabilitation means restoring the person’s role, not only reducing symptoms.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Community care finds cases early, supports treatment and prevents relapse.
- DMHP and mobile teams provide outreach.
- Family support, respite and burden assessment.
- Rehabilitation: activity, work, integration.
- Stigma reduction through education and inclusion.
