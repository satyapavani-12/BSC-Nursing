## In simple words

Personality disorders are **enduring patterns of thinking, feeling and behaving** that differ sharply from the person’s culture and cause distress. Sexual disorders affect desire, arousal or behaviour.

## Personality disorders — cluster A (odd or eccentric)

| Disorder | Features |
|---|---|
| Paranoid | Suspicious, distrustful, reads hidden meaning |
| Schizoid | Detached, prefers to be alone, limited emotions |
| Schizotypal | Odd ideas, magical thinking, unusual speech |

## Cluster B (dramatic or emotional)

| Disorder | Features |
|---|---|
| Antisocial | Disregard for rights of others, no guilt, breaks rules |
| Borderline | Unstable relationships, self-image, emotions; self-harm |
| Histrionic | Attention-seeking, dramatic behaviour |
| Narcissistic | Grandiosity, need for admiration, lacks empathy |

## Cluster C (anxious or fearful)

| Disorder | Features |
|---|---|
| Avoidant | Social inhibition, fear of rejection, shy |
| Dependent | Submissive, unable to decide alone |
| Obsessive-compulsive | Perfectionism, order and control at cost of flexibility |

## Nursing management of personality disorders

- Set clear, consistent limits and expectations
- Avoid power struggles; keep calm and predictable
- Watch for self-harm and suicide risk (borderline)
- Deal with manipulation without confronting angrily
- Document behaviour objectively, not labels
- Individual therapy, DBT and group therapy as prescribed

## Sexual disorders

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Sexual desire | Low or absent desire, aversion |
| Arousal | Erectile dysfunction, lubrication disorder |
| Orgasm | Premature ejaculation, absent orgasm |
| Pain | Dyspareunia, vaginismus |
| Paraphilias | Exhibitionism, voyeurism, paedophilia |
| Gender dysphoria | Distress from mismatch of body and gender identity |

**Nursing:** privacy, non-judgemental history, confidentiality, sex education, referral to counsellor, support the couple.

<Callout type="must" title="Non-judgemental care">
  Sexual problems need **privacy, accurate information and no mockery or moral judgement**. Reassure that most sexual difficulties have effective treatment.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Personality disorders: three clusters and their features.
- Nursing: limits, calm, suicide watch, objective records, DBT.
- Sexual disorders: desire, arousal, orgasm, pain, paraphilia.
- Nursing: privacy, information, confidentiality, referral.
- Care is non-judgemental.
