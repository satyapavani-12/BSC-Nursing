## In simple words

Antenatal care protects the mother and baby before birth. The aim is to **prevent, detect and treat problems early** and to prepare the mother for a safe birth.

## Schedule of visits

| Period | Number of visits |
|---|---|
| First 6 months (to 32 weeks) | One visit each month |
| Seventh and eighth months (32-36 weeks) | One visit every 2 weeks |
| Ninth month (36-40 weeks) | Weekly |

At least **4 antenatal visits**; the WHO recommends **8 contacts**.

## Booking visit — history

- Name, age, height, weight, blood group
- Obstetric history: gravida, parity, previous births, abortions, stillbirths
- Menstrual history — LMP, cycle regularity
- Medical history — hypertension, diabetes, heart disease, previous surgery
- Family and social history
- Immunisation status

## Antenatal examination

- **Blood pressure** — 120/80 or above is a danger sign
- **Weight** — sudden gain indicates oedema and pre-eclampsia
- **Pallor** — anaemia
- **Fundal height** — correlates with weeks of gestation
- **Fetal heart rate** — 110-160 per minute
- **Fetal position, presentation** — after 36 weeks
- **Oedema** — feet, face and hands
- **Urine** — albumin and sugar

## Investigations

- **Blood:** Hb, blood group and Rh, VDRL, HIV, HBsAg, sugar tolerance, haemoglobin electrophoresis
- **Urine:** albumin and sugar and microscopy
- **Ultrasonography:** fetal growth, anomalies, position, placenta

## Antenatal danger signs

- Bleeding per vagina
- Severe or persistent headache
- Blurred vision
- Sudden swelling of face and hands
- Severe upper abdominal pain
- Reduced or absent fetal movement
- Fever with chills
- Gush of fluid (leaking membranes)

<Callout type="must" title="Reduce fetal movement is an emergency">
  **A mother who reports reduced fetal movements must be seen the same day.** Absent fetal movement, bleeding, high blood pressure with headache and blurred vision all need urgent referral.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- ANC: four to eight visits with increasing frequency.
- Book early, examine BP, weight, pallor, fundal height and fetal heart.
- Investigations: Hb, group, HIV, VDRL, urine, ultrasound.
- Danger signs: bleeding, headache, blurred vision, swelling, reduced fetal movement.
- Iron, folic acid, calcium, TT vaccine, birth planning.
