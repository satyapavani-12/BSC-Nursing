## In simple words

The uterus is the organ of pregnancy. Conception needs a healthy ovum, healthy sperms, ovulation timing, patent tubes and a receptive endometrium.

## Female reproductive organs

| Organ | Function |
|---|---|
| Ovaries | Produce ova and oestrogen/progesterone |
| Fallopian tubes | Transport ovum; site of fertilisation |
| Uterus | Muscular organ that carries the fetus |
| Cervix | Lower part; dilates in labour |
| Vagina | Birth canal |
| Vulva | External genitalia |

## Menstrual cycle (brief)

- Days 1-5: menstruation (shedding of the lining)
- Days 6-13: follicular phase — FSH grows a follicle, oestrogen rises
- Day 14: LH surge and ovulation
- Days 15-28: luteal phase — corpus luteum makes progesterone

## Conception to implantation

1. **Ovulation** — the ovum enters the tube
2. **Fertilisation** — sperm meets ovum in the tube; the zygote forms
3. **Division** — the zygote divides as it moves to the uterus
4. **Implantation** — the blastocyst embeds in the endometrium, about 6-7 days after fertilisation

## Placenta

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Foetal side | Chorionic villi bathe in maternal blood spaces |
| Maternal side | Decidua, attached to the uterine wall |
| Exchange | Oxygen, nutrients in; carbon dioxide and waste out |
| Hormones | hCG, oestrogen, progesterone |
| Fetal circulation | Two umbilical arteries carry deoxygenated blood; one vein carries oxygenated blood |

## Fetal development by weeks

| Period | Development |
|---|---|
| 4 weeks | Neural tube, heart begins to beat |
| 8 weeks | All organs present; embryo now called a fetus |
| 12 weeks | Limb buds; fingers form; sex identifiable |
| 16 weeks | Movement felt (quickening); heartbeat audible |
| 20 weeks | Vernix caseosa; fetus viability near the limit |
| 24 weeks | Lungs and eyelids developing; weigh about 700 g |
| 28 weeks | Eyes open; lungs almost mature; weigh about 1000 g |
| 32-36 weeks | Fattening; surfactant matures |
| 38-40 weeks | Term; ready for birth |

**Term is 37-41 weeks of gestation from the first day of the last menstrual period.** Pregnancy normally lasts about 280 days or 40 weeks (9 months and 10 days).

<Callout type="must" title="Gestational age is calculated from the last menstrual period">
  Naegele’s rule: due date = LMP + 9 months + 7 days. **Count from the first day of the last menstrual period, not from the day of conception.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Ovaries, tubes, uterus, cervix, vagina.
- Cycle: menstruation, follicular, ovulation, luteal.
- Fertilisation in the tube; implantation in the uterus.
- Placenta: exchange and hormones; cord 1 vein and 2 arteries.
- Fetal development by weeks; term 37-41 weeks; due date by Naegele’s rule.
