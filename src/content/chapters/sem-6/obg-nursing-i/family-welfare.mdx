## In simple words

Family welfare programmes help couples plan **the number and spacing** of children, according to their own choice. Contraception is the method used.

## Family welfare objectives

- Reduce maternal and child mortality
- Space pregnancies for the mother’s recovery
- Allow families to choose a small family size
- Reduce unsafe abortion
- Prevent teenage pregnancy

## Methods of contraception

| Method | Key points |
|---|---|
| Combined oral contraceptive pill | Regular daily; failure if missed; not for smokers over 35 or with history of clotting |
| Condom | Protects against infection as well |
| Copper-T IUCD | Long acting, 5-10 years; check threads; avoid if pelvic infection |
| PPIUCD | Inserted within 48 hours of delivery |
| Hormonal injection (DMPA) | Every 12 weeks |
| Emergency contraception | Within 72 hours |
| Natural methods | Calendar method, withdrawal, exclusive breastfeeding |
| Sterilisation | Female tubectomy and male vasectomy; permanence |
| Injectable contraceptive-antigonadotropin (Centchroman) | Weekly pill, non-hormonal option |

<Callout type="must" title="Couple choice is protected">
  The couple’s choice of contraceptive is respected and private. **Never pressure, label or mislead. Give information about all methods, including side effects and follow-up.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Family welfare, small healthy family, planned spacing.
- Methods: natural, barrier, hormonal, IUCD, permanent.
- Emergency pills within 72 hours.
- IUCD for the long term; check the thread monthly.
- Sterilisation as a permanent choice with informed consent.
- Counselling must be respectful.
