## In simple words

**Midwifery** is care of the woman during pregnancy, labour, birth, postnatal period and newborn. **Obstetrics** is the medical specialty for pregnancy and childbirth — midwifery is the nursing side of that care.

## Reproductive health

Reproductive health means complete physical, mental and social wellbeing in all matters of the reproductive system — not absence of disease only.

- Includes fertility, safe pregnancy and birth, contraception, prevention and treatment of infertility, and management of problems
- Sexual and reproductive rights of women must be respected

## Legal and policy basis in India

- **Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947** — midwifery education and registration
- **INC Curriculum** — prescribes midwifery training and clinical practice
- **MTP Act** — legal conditions of safe termination of pregnancy
- **PCPNDT Act** — prevents sex determination
- **Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques** protect women and prevent female foeticide
- **The Clinical Establishments Act** ensures standards of care

## Birth attendance: institutional vs home

Birth should be **institutional**. Institutions have equipment, skilled staff and referral. Home births need a trained attendant, referral plan, warm environment and infection prevention.

<Callout type="must" title="Skilled birth attendance saves lives">
  Most maternal deaths come from haemorrhage, sepsis, hypertensive disorders and obstructed labour. **A trained midwife with a referral plan prevents deaths.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Midwifery: antenatal, intrapartum, postpartum and newborn care.
- Obstetrics is the medical side; midwifery the nursing side.
- Legal: INC Act 1947, MTP, PCPNDT, Clinical Establishments Act.
- Aim: institutional birth with a trained midwife.
- Major causes of maternal death: haemorrhage, sepsis, hypertension, obstructed labour.
