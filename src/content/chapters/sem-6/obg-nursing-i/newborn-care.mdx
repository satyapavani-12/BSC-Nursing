## In simple words

The newborn is in a period of rapid adjustment. Good care means airway, warmth, cord and eye care, safe feeding and early identification of problems.

## Immediate care

- Airway: clear the secretions, dry and stimulate
- Warmth: skin-to-skin, keep the head covered, delay bath
- Cord: sterile clamp and cut, keep clean and dry
- Eyes: tetracycline ointment
- Vitamin K 1 mg IM
- APGAR scoring at 1 and 5 minutes
- Identification band and weight
- First feed within the first hour

## Newborn examination

- **Weight** — normal 2.5 to 3.5 kg; average loss of 5-10% in the first week, then regained
- **Head circumference** — about 35 cm
- **Chest circumference** — about 33 cm
- **Frontanelle** — soft diamond-shaped; closes at 18 months
- **Temperature** — 36.5 to 37.5 degrees C
- **Respirations** — 30 to 60 per minute
- **Heart rate** — 110 to 160 per minute
- **Skin** — slightly blue hands and feet (acrocyanosis) common; jaundice at day 2 to 3 peaks by day 4; physiological jaundice disappears by day 7 to 10

## Immunisation

| Vaccine | Schedule |
|---|---|
| BCG | At birth or up to 1 year |
| Hepatitis B | At birth, 6, 14 weeks |
| OPV and fIPV | At birth, then 6, 10, 14 weeks |
| Pentavalent (DPT, Hib, Hep B) | 6, 10, 14 weeks |
| Rotavirus | 6, 10, 14 weeks |
| MR (measles-rubella) | 9-12 months and 16-24 months |

<Callout type="must" title="Keep the baby warm and feed on demand">
  The newborn loses heat quickly. **Never give water, honey or sugar water.** Feed the baby on demand; the baby should have at least 6 wet nappies a day.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Immediate: airway, warmth, cord, eye drops, vitamin K, identify, APGAR.
- Normal values: weight, heart rate, respiratory rate and temperature.
- Immunisation: BCG, hepatitis B, OPV0, pentavalent, MR.
- Feed on demand; no water; 6+ wet nappies per day.
- Danger signs: poor feeding, fast breathing, cold or hot, jaundice day 1, convulsion.
