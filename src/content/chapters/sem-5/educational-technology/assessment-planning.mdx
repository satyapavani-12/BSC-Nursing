## In simple words

Teaching is planned backwards from the outcome. First decide **what the learner should be able to do**, then plan how to teach it, and finally check whether it happened.

## Three domains of objectives (Bloom)

| Domain | Deals with | Example verbs |
|---|---|---|
| Cognitive | Knowledge and thinking | Define, explain, apply, analyse |
| Affective | Attitudes and values | Value, accept, respect, cooperate |
| Psychomotor | Skills and doing | Measure, insert, demonstrate, perform |

## Writing a good objective

A complete objective has four parts — **audience, behaviour, conditions, degree** (ABCD).

"**The student** will **record the vital signs** of a patient **using a thermometer and sphygmomanometer** **within 5 minutes with no error**."

## Lesson plan

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| General information | Topic, date, time, group, duration, teacher |
| Specific objectives | Observable, measurable, in three domains |
| Content outline | Main points in logical order |
| Teaching-learning aids | Charts, models, specimens, videos |
| Introduction | Arouse interest; link to previous knowledge |
| Presentation | Methods used, step by step |
| Summary and evaluation | Recap and check |
| Assignment | Reinforcement of learning |

## Assessment types

- **Formative** — during learning: questions, observation, practice records
- **Summative** — after a unit or course: university examinations
- **Diagnostic** — before teaching: finds existing knowledge
- **Objective tests** — MCQ, short answers, true or false
- **Clinical evaluation** — clinical logs, OSCEs, checklists, OSCE checklists

<Callout type="must" title="Objectives must be observable">
  Weak: "The student will understand temperature." Strong: "**The student will record temperature using a thermometer and state the normal range accurately.**" If you cannot see it, you cannot assess it.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Plan backwards from observable objectives.
- Three domains: cognitive, affective, psychomotor.
- ABCD components of an objective.
- Lesson plan: information, objectives, content, aids, method, evaluation.
- Assessment: diagnostic, formative, summative.
