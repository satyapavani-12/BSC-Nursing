## In simple words

Guidance is helping a person choose and plan. **Counselling** is a deeper, private conversation that helps a person understand and cope with a problem and decide what to do.

Guidance is preventive and informative; counselling is healing and personal.

## Types of guidance

- Educational — choosing courses and study methods
- Vocational — choosing a career or job
- Personal and social — relationships, self-esteem, behaviour
- Health — adopting healthy behaviour
- Family — marriage, parenting and family planning

## Counselling steps

1. **Prepare** — private place, time, records
2. **Build rapport** — greet, explain confidentiality
3. **Listen** — let the person tell the whole story
4. **Identify** the problem and the person’s strengths
5. **Explore** options and consequences together
6. **Plan** small, achievable steps
7. **Follow up** — review and support

## Counselling skills

- Active listening and full attention
- Open questions
- Reflecting feelings ("you sound worried")
- Silence and patience
- Never judging, never imposing values
- Confidentiality and record keeping
- Referring when the problem needs specialist help

## 60-second revision

- Guidance: information and choice; counselling: coping and decision-making.
- Types: educational, vocational, personal, family, health.
- Steps: prepare, rapport, listen, identify, plan, follow up.
- Skills: listening, open questions, reflection, silence, confidentiality.
- Refer beyond competence; maintain confidentiality limits.
