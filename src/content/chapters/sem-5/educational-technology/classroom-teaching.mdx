## In simple words

Good teaching matches the method to the lesson: large groups get lectures, but skills need demonstration and practice, and problems need discussion.

## Classroom methods

| Method | When best | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Lecture | New information to a large group | Becomes passive; keep it short |
| Discussion | Attitudes and application | Needs preparation |
| Brainstorming | Many ideas quickly | No judgement |
| Role play | Sensitive situations and empathy | Debrief afterwards |
| Case study | Decision making | Needs prior knowledge |
| Team teaching | Combining expertise | Needs planning |
| Computer assisted learning | Self-paced revision | Needs facilities |
| Project | Independent learning | Needs clear goals |

## Micro-teaching

A short teaching session (5-10 minutes) with a small group, focused on one skill, followed by feedback.

1. **Plan** one specific skill
2. **Teach** for 5-10 minutes with peers observing
3. **Feedback** — strengths, areas to improve
4. **Reteach** the same skill after changes
5. Teachers repeat until the skill improves

## Skill laboratory practice

1. **Prepare** — objectives, station, equipment, checklist
2. **Demonstrate** — the teacher performs the skill step by step, with commentary
3. **Guided practice** — students practise with supervision
4. **Return demonstration** — each student performs back
5. **Evaluation** — record against a checklist; extra practice until safe

**Rules of demonstration:** speak audibly, face the group, go slowly through critical steps, avoid turning your back, and repeat the critical step.

<Callout type="must" title="Return demonstration is compulsory">
  A student who has only watched cannot perform safely. **Every student must give a return demonstration before clinical practice.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Match method to aim: lecture, discussion, role play, project.
- Micro-teaching: plan, teach, feedback, reteach.
- Skill lab: demonstrate, practise, return demonstration, evaluate.
- Demonstration rules: audible, visible, slow critical steps.
- Return demonstration before patient care.
