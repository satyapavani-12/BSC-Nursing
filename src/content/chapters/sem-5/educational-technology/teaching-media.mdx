## In simple words

Media are the aids a teacher uses to make a lesson visible, clear and memorable. Choose the medium that suits the audience, the setting and the message.

## Projected aids

| Aid | Best for |
|---|---|
| Chalkboard and whiteboard | Fast, low-cost teaching |
| Slides | Large groups and large diagrams |
| Overhead projector | Overlays and transparency sheets |
| Video and films | Procedures, animation, patient interviews |
| Computer with projector | Interactive learning and PowerPoint sessions |

## Non-projected aids

| Aid | Best for |
|---|---|
| Charts and posters | Low-literacy groups and clinics |
| Models and specimens | Anatomy and physiology teaching |
| Flash cards | Very small groups |
| Flip charts | Mobile community teaching |
| Puppets | Children as learners or as an audience |
| Printed handouts | Reinforcement of key points |
| Real objects | Familiarity (specimen of a food item) |

## Digital tools for nursing education

- **E-learning** — modules, discussion forums, quizzes
- **Webinars** — distance lectures across sites
- **Simulation and virtual reality labs** — safe practice for emergencies
- **Electronic records and hospital information system** — practice documentation
- **Telehealth** — patient counselling, triage and follow-up teaching
- **Chatbots and knowledge apps** — revision and quick answers

## Selecting media — rules

1. It must match the learning objective
2. The audience must be able to see and read it
3. It must work in the setting (power, room size, light)
4. It must be affordable and reusable
5. It must be prepared in advance and checked

<Callout type="must" title="Media support teaching, they do not replace it">
  **the teacher is the most important teaching aid.** Use media to make the point visible, then practice, question and discuss.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Projected aids: board, slides, video, computer projector.
- Non-projected aids: charts, models, flash cards, handouts.
- Digital: e-learning, webinars, simulation, telehealth.
- Media must aim, suit the audience, work in the setting and be affordable.
- The teacher remains the most important aid.
