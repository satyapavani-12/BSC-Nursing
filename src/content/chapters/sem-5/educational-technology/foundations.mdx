## In simple words

Education is drawing out the best in a person through knowledge, skill and attitude change. For nurses, education is both a **professional preparation** and a **service to patients and learners**.

## Aims of education

- Intellectual growth — knowledge and reasoning
- Psychomotor skill — safe procedures
- Affective growth — values, attitudes and professionalism
- Social growth — teamwork and communication
- Professional growth — accountability and lifelong learning

## Curriculum

The curriculum is the **planned learning experience** — objectives, content, teaching methods, and evaluation.

| Curriculum element | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Objectives | What should the learner become? |
| Content | What will be taught and learned? |
| Methods | How will it be taught? |
| Evaluation | How do we know learning happened? |
| Total experience | Cocurricular and clinical learning |

## Curriculum types

- **Subject-centred** — content leads the plan (most nursing curricula)
- **Learner-centred** — needs of the learner lead the plan
- **Competency-based** — safe, demonstrated skills define the outcome
- **Problem-based learning** — real problems drive learning
- **Integrated** — theory and clinical practice taught together

## The nurse educator

- Plans and delivers teaching in classroom and clinical area
- Assesses student competence fairly
- Models ethical and professional behaviour
- Counsels and mentors students
- Participates in curriculum and programme evaluation
- Keeps own knowledge current through continuing education

<Callout type="must" title="Adult learning">
  Nursing students are adults. Andragogy (Knowles) says adults learn best when they know **why** the lesson matters, participate in planning and learn by solving real problems.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Education draws out knowledge, skill and attitude.
- Curriculum: objectives, content, methods, evaluation.
- Types: subject, learner, competency, problem-based, integrated.
- Adults learn best when purpose is clear and problems are real.
- The educator plans, teaches, assesses, models and mentors.
