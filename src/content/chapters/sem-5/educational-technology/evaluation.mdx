## In simple words

Measurement collects facts (marks, counts). **Assessment** interprets those facts. **Evaluation** judges how far the objectives were achieved and improves the next teaching.

## Measurement, assessment and evaluation

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Measurement | Assigning numbers — 18 out of 20 |
| Assessment | Interpreting the numbers — the student measured temperature accurately |
| Evaluation | Judging the whole programme and improving it |

## Tools of assessment

| Tool | Type |
|---|---|
| Question paper | Written test |
| MCQ, true or false, short answer, essay | Objective and essay |
| Checklist and rating scale | Clinical and practical |
|  Assignment and project | Continuous assessment |
| Seminar and clinical presentation | Oral assessment |
| Notebook and clinical record | Continuous work |

## Question paper blueprint

A table that fixes **how many questions from each content area at each cognitive level (knowledge, understanding, application)**, so the paper represents the whole syllabus and not the teacher’s bias.

## Qualities of a good evaluation tool

- **Validity** — measures what it should
- **Reliability** — gives consistent results
- **Objectivity** — same result for any examiner
- **Discrimination** — separates strong from weak students
- **Feasibility** — can be done within time and resources
- **Comprehensiveness** — covers the whole curriculum

## Practical and clinical evaluation

- Internal assessment: practical marks, notebook, assignments, attendance
- External examination: university practical with internal and external examiners
- Students are assessed against a checklist for procedures
- University requirements: prescribed clinical hours and records

<Callout type="must" title="Evaluate the teaching too">
  Evaluation is not only of students. **Evaluate teachers, methods, clinical areas and curriculum** — student failures often reveal teaching gaps, not only student gaps.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Measure, assess, evaluate — increasing depth.
- Tools: question paper, MCQ, essay, checklist, rating, project.
- Blueprint balances content and cognitive level.
- Qualities: validity, reliability, objectivity, discrimination.
- Practical evaluation: checklists, records, internal and external examiners.
