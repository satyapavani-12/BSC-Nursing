## In simple words

**Mental health** is not just the absence of mental illness. It is a state of balance in which a person can use his abilities, cope with normal stresses, work productively and contribute to the community.

**Mental illness** is a disturbance of thinking, feeling, behaviour or perception that causes distress and interferes with daily life.

## Signs of positive mental health

- Accepts self, has realistic self-respect
- Forms warm and satisfying relationships
- Can work, play and be productive
- Copies with stress and frustration
- Feels secure and accepted
- Can make decisions and take responsibility

## A brief history of psychiatric nursing in India

- **1745 to 1800** — lunatic asylums with custodial care (London, Bombay, Calcutta)
- **1794 to 1858** — moral treatment began; restraint reduced
- **1858 onwards** — Lunacy Acts; Indian Lunacy Act 1912
- **1947** — Indian Psychiatric Society formed
- **1987** — Mental Health Act 1987
- **2017** — **Mental Healthcare Act 2017** — right to mental health care
- **Nurses** — trained psychiatric nursing introduced in 1950s; MSc Nursing in psychiatry

## Scope of mental health nursing

- Assess and monitor mental status and risk
- Give medicines and watch side effects
- Provide physical care, nutrition and hygiene
- Therapeutic communication and relationship building
- Counsel and teach the patient and family
- Crisis intervention and suicide prevention
- Rehabilitation and community integration
- Reduce stigma through education

<Callout type="must" title="Mental illness is not madness">
  Mental illness is a **health problem**, like diabetes or asthma. It is treatable, and most people recover and lead useful lives. Reject labels such as mad, possessed or dangerous.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Mental health = balance for productive living; illness = distressing disturbance.
- Signs of health: self-acceptance, relationships, work, coping.
- History: asylums → moral treatment → MHA 1987 → MHCA 2017.
- Nurse’s work: observe, medicate, comfort, counsel, rehabilitate.
- NMHP + DMHP take mental health to the community.
