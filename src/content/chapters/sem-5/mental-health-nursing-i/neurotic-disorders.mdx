## In simple words

Neurotic disorders are common, mild to moderate mental illnesses in which the patient stays in contact with reality and knows he is ill. Anxiety is the main symptom, and it appears in various forms.

## The anxiety disorders

| Disorder | Key features |
|---|---|
| Generalised anxiety disorder | Free-floating worry, restlessness, poor sleep, over 6 months |
| Panic disorder | Sudden attacks: palpitations, choking, fear of dying, peaks in 10 minutes |
| Phobia | Irrational fear of a specific object or situation, avoided |
| Social phobia | Fear of being judged, avoids eating or speaking in public |
| OCD | Obsessions (thoughts) and compulsions (acts) causing distress |
| PTSD | Re-experiencing after a traumatic event, nightmares, avoidance |

## Somatoform disorders

- **Somatisation** — many physical complaints in many systems, for years
- **Conversion disorder** — neurological symptom (paralysis, blindness, fit) with no physical cause; often after stress; la belle indifference
- **Hypochondriasis (illness anxiety)** — fixed fear of a serious illness despite reassurance
- **Somatoform pain** — persistent pain without cause

## Stress-related disorders

- **Acute stress reaction** — after a sudden shock; settles within days
- **Adjustment disorder** — emotional and behavioural reaction to a life change, within 3 months
- **PTSD** — follows severe trauma; re-experiencing, avoidance, hyperarousal; needs therapy and sometimes medicines

## Treatment and nursing care

- **Psychological:** CBT, exposure and response prevention, relaxation training, systematic desensitisation, psychoeducation
- **Medicines:** SSRIs, benzodiazepines briefly, beta-blockers for somatic anxiety
- **Nursing:** calm presence, controlled breathing, real reassurance, graded activity, avoid reinforcing sick role, family support
- **Assessment:** ask about trauma, avoid leading the patient into checking rituals

<Callout type="must" title="Do not dismiss somatoform complaints">
  The suffering is real even when the cause is psychological. **Say: "Your symptoms are real and we will help you" — never "there is nothing wrong with you."**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- GAD: worry over 6 months; panic: attacks peaking in 10 minutes; phobia: avoided fear.
- OCD: obsessions and compulsions with insight.
- Somatoform: real symptoms, no medical cause; do not dismiss.
- Conversion: neurological deficit after stress; la belle indifference.
- PTSD: flashbacks, avoidance, hyperarousal after trauma.
- Treatment: CBT, relaxation, SSRIs, family support.
