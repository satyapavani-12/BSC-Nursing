## In simple words

Mental illness is treated with medicines, psychological therapies, somatic therapies and social support. Most patients need a combination, and the nurse participates in all of them.

## Pharmacological therapy

| Drug group | Used for | Main nursing points |
|---|---|---|
| Antipsychotics (chlorpromazine, risperidone, olanzapine) | Schizophrenia, mania, psychosis | Watch: EPS, Akathisia, Dystonia, Parkinsonism, Tardive dyskinesia |
| Antidepressants (fluoxetine, sertraline, amitriptyline) | Depression, anxiety | Delayed effect 2-3 weeks; watch serotonin syndrome |
| Mood stabilisers (lithium, valproate) | Bipolar disorder | Lithium therapeutic level 0.6-1.2; watch tremor, toxicity |
| Anxiolytics (benzodiazepines) | Anxiety, insomnia | Short-term only; dependence |
| Anticholinergics (benztropine) | Drug-induced EPS | Dry mouth, constipation, blurred vision |

<Callout type="must" title="Neuroleptic malignant syndrome">
  **Fever, muscular rigidity and altered mind on antipsychotics = NMS.** Stop the drug, cool the patient, monitor, give supportive care. This is a medical emergency.
</Callout>

## Modified ECT

- Indications: severe depression with suicide risk, psychotic depression, catatonia, mania not responding
- Anaesthesia (short-acting) and muscle relaxant (succinylcholine) are used
- Electrode placement: bilateral or right unilateral
- Seizure of 15-120 seconds is therapeutic
- **Frequency:** 2-3 per week, total 6-12
- **Nursing:** consent, NPO 6-8 hours, remove dentures and jewellery, premedication, check ECG, reassure the patient; after: airway, orientation, headache complaints, recovery monitoring

## Psychotherapies

| Therapy | Focus |
|---|---|
| CBT | Identifies and changes faulty thinking and behaviour |
| Supportive therapy | Strengthens coping, encourages and supports |
| Behaviour therapy | Uses conditioning and reinforcement to change behaviour |
| Cognitive therapy | Corrects distortions such as hopelessness |
| Family therapy | Improves communication and reduces relapses |
| Group therapy | Patients share and learn from one another |
| Psychoanalysis | Explores unconscious conflicts; free association and dreams |

## Psychosocial rehabilitation

- Behavioural modification to build daily-living skills
- Vocational training and sheltered work
- Family counselling and support groups
- Self-help groups and community integration
- Reduce stigma; half-way homes and day-care centres

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Medication rounds** — five rights, monitor side effects, watch adherence
- **ECT suite** — consent, preparation, safety, recovery care
- **Ward** — reality orientation, limit setting, positive reinforcement
- **Rehab** — activity therapy, skills practice, family teaching

## 60-second revision

- Medicines: antipsychotics, antidepressants, mood stabilisers, anxiolytics.
- Antidepressants: delayed effect, serotonin syndrome, discontinuation syndrome.
- Lithium level 0.6-1.2; toxicity signs tremor, vomiting, confusion.
- ECT: modified, 6-12 sittings, seizure 15-120 seconds; nurse’s role in pre, during and post care.
- Psychotherapies: CBT, supportive, behaviour, family, group.
- Rehabilitation: skills, work, family, community integration.
