## In simple words

Mental health nursing rests on a few basic ideas: how the mind works, how people protect themselves from anxiety, why stress causes illness, how illness is classified, and how the nurse should behave in every contact.

## Mind and personality (Freud)

| Part | Principle | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Id | Pleasure | Instincts and urges; present from birth |
| Ego | Reality | Balances id and superego in the real world |
| Superego | Morality | Conscience and ideals learned from parents |

## Common defence mechanisms

| Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|
| Denial | Refusing to accept a cancer diagnosis |
| Repression | Forgetting an unpleasant memory |
| Projection | Blaming others for one’s own faults |
| Displacement | Shouting at a junior after being scolded |
| Rationalisation | Making excuses to justify failure |
| Regression | Behaving childishly under stress |
| Sublimation | Channelling aggression into sport |
| Reaction formation | Showing exaggerated love for a hated person |

## Models of mental illness

- **Biological (medical) model** — illness is due to brain chemistry or genes; treated with medicines
- **Psychological model** — due to conflicts, learning and thinking patterns; treated with therapy
- **Social model** — due to stress, family and environment; treated with social support
- **Behavioural model** — abnormal behaviour is learned; changed by conditioning
- **Nursing model** — holistic care of the whole person in his environment

## Classification

- **ICD-11 (WHO)** and **DSM-5 (APA)** are the main classifications
- **Neuroses** — milder, insight present (anxiety, depression, phobia)
- **Psychoses** — severe, insight absent (schizophrenia, mania with psychosis)

## Basic principles of psychiatric nursing

1. Accept the patient as he is — with respect, without judgement
2. Build a therapeutic relationship before anything else
3. Ensure safety first — of the patient, staff and others
4. Observe, record and report objectively
5. Protect privacy and confidentiality
6. Involve the family as partners in care
7. Promote independence and rehabilitation, not dependence
8. Care for yourself — reflect, debrief, seek supervision

<Callout type="must" title="Safety always comes first">
  Before any therapeutic talk, check for **suicide risk, violence risk and medical instability**. Safety precedes insight — a safe patient can be treated.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Mind: id (pleasure), ego (reality), superego (morality).
- Defence mechanisms protect the ego: denial, projection, repression, displacement.
- Neurosis vs psychosis: insight present vs absent.
- Models: biological, psychological, social, behavioural.
- Principles: acceptance, relationship, safety, observation, confidentiality, family, rehabilitation.
- Classification: ICD-11, DSM-5.
