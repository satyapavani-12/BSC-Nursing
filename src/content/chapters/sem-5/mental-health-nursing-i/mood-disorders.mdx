## In simple words

Mood disorders are illnesses in which mood is disturbed for weeks and interferes with life. **Depression** pulls the person down; **mania** pushes him up. When both occur, it is **bipolar disorder**.

## Depression — symptoms

- Low mood, most of the day, nearly every day
- Loss of interest and pleasure (anhedonia)
- Sleep changes — early morning waking (typical)
- Appetite and weight change
- Fatigue and tiredness
- Poor concentration; indecisiveness
- Worthlessness, guilt, helplessness
- Thoughts of death and suicide
- Low libido, constipation, psychomotor slowing

**Diagnosis:** symptoms for over 2 weeks with functional impairment.

## Mania — symptoms

- Elevated, expansive or irritable mood
- Increased energy with reduced need for sleep
- Grandiosity and inflated self-esteem
- Pressure of speech and flight of ideas
- Overspending and reckless behaviour
- Distractibility
- Increased sexual activity
- Psychotic features in severe mania

## Suicide

- **Warning signs:** previous attempt, hopelessness, giving away things, sudden calm after despair, sleep loss, alcohol abuse, isolation
- **Nursing:** ask directly, remove the means (poison, medicines, weapons), do not leave the patient alone, one-to-one observation, remove ligature points
- Never promise secrecy — safety over confidentiality
- Refer and support the family

<Callout type="must" title="Suicide precautions">
  **Remove every means of self-harm.** Keep the environment free of medicines, sharp objects, ropes and belts. Maintain continuous observation and never leave the patient alone.
</Callout>

## Nursing care

- **Depression:** listen, do not say "cheer up", encourage small activities, monitor sleep, food and medicines; take every threat seriously
- **Mania:** low-stimulation environment, firm limits, short sentences, redirect energy, prevent exhaustion and injury; firm but respectful
- **Mania:** channel energy into structured activity; never argue
- **Family:** educate about the illness, relapse signs and adherence

## 60-second revision

- Depression: low mood, anhedonia, sleep and energy loss, guilt, suicidal thoughts.
- Mania: elated or irritable mood, little sleep, grandiosity, overspending.
- Bipolar: both episodes; lithium and psychotherapy.
- Suicide risk: ask, remove means, constant observation.
- Nursing: listen and activate in depression; limit and protect in mania.
