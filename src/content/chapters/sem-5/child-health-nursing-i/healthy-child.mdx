## In simple words

Growth and development are the processes by which children grow in size and mature in function from birth to adolescence. Growth refers to physical changes (increase in size, weight, height), while development refers to maturation of skills and abilities (motor, language, social, emotional).

Understanding normal growth and development helps nurses assess children, identify delays early, and provide appropriate care.

## Principles of growth and development

- **Growth** is quantitative (increase in size, weight, height)
- **Development** is qualitative (maturation of skills and abilities)
- Development follows a predictable sequence (cephalocaudal: head to toe; proximodistal: center to periphery)
- Each child develops at their own rate
- Development is cumulative — new skills build on previous ones

## Developmental milestones

| Age | Gross motor | Fine motor | Language | Social |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 months | Lifts head briefly | Follows objects | Coos | Social smile |
| 4 months | Rolls front to back | Reaches for toys | Babbles | Laughs |
| 6 months | Sits with support | Transfers objects | Babbles syllables | Stranger anxiety |
| 9 months | Sits without support | Pincer grasp | Says "mama/dada" | Stranger anxiety |
| 12 months | Walks with support | Scribbles | Says 1-2 words | Waves bye-bye |
| 18 months | Walks alone | Scribbles, stacks blocks | Says several words | Tantrums |
| 2 years | Runs, climbs | Builds tower of blocks | 2-word phrases | Parallel play |
| 3 years | Runs well, jumps | Draws circle | 3-word sentences | Parallel play |
| 4 years | Hops on one foot | Draws person | Tells stories | Cooperative play |
| 5 years | Skips, jumps | Draws person with body parts | Tells stories | Cooperative play |

## Nursing care

- **Assess growth** — measure height, weight, head circumference at each visit; plot on growth charts
- **Assess development** — observe milestones; use developmental screening tools
- **Parent education** — teach about normal development, safety, nutrition, immunizations
- **Early identification** — refer for developmental delay if milestones are not met

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Well-child visits** — assess growth and development at each visit; plot on growth charts
- **Developmental screening** — use standard tools (Denver II, ASQ) to identify delays
- **Parent education** — teach about normal development, safety, nutrition, immunizations
- **Referral** — refer children with developmental delays to early intervention programs

## 60-second revision

- Growth = quantitative (size); development = qualitative (maturation).
- Cephalocaudal: head to toe; proximodistal: center to periphery.
- Milestones: motor, language, social, adaptive skills by age.
- Assess development at every well-child visit.
- Refer for developmental delays to early intervention.
