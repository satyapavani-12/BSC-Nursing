## In simple words

IMNCI (Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness) is the WHO strategy used all over India to manage sick children aged 2 months to 5 years. It treats the **whole child**, not one disease, and it works when no doctor is available — even at the health-worker level.

The child is assessed, classified into a **colour-coded plan** (red / yellow / green) and treated or referred.

## The IMNCI process

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Ask | History from the mother — cough, diarrhoea, fever, feeding, danger signs |
| Look | Breathing, colour, hydration, pallor, ear discharge, skin |
| Feel | Fever, cold, chest indrawing, stiffness, neck swelling |
| Classify | Red (severe — refer), Yellow (treat at home), Green (home care) |
| Treat | By plan; teach the mother |
| Follow up | Review at the stated day or when danger signs appear |

## General danger signs (any one = urgent referral)

- Not able to drink or breastfeed
- Vomits everything given
- Convulsions or fits
- Lethargic or unconscious
- Severe chest indrawing
- Stridor in a calm child
- Severe wasting or swollen feet (malnutrition)

## Colour-coded plans

| Colour | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Red** | Severe illness or very severe disease | Refer urgently to hospital; give first dose; keep warm |
| **Yellow** | Mild or moderate disease | Treat at home with medicines and fluids; review |
| **Green** | No signs of specific disease | Home care advice and follow-up |

## Common conditions managed

| Condition | Yellow plan | Red plan |
|---|---|---|
| Cough or fast breathing | Amoxycillin at home | Chest indrawing, stridor, danger sign → refer |
| Diarrhoea | ORS, zinc for 14 days | Severe dehydration, blood in stool → refer |
| Fever | Assess for malaria; antimalarial | Stiff neck, bulging fontanelle, very hot → refer |
| Ear problem | Ear drops, drying the ear | Mastoid swelling, high fever → refer |
| Malnutrition | Feeding advice, deworming | Severe wasting, oedema, anaemia → refer |

<Callout type="must" title="Mothers can save children">
  IMNCI trains the mother to **recognise danger signs** — not drinking, vomiting everything, fits, lethargy — and to return immediately. Teach every mother before discharge.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **OPD and emergency** — quick assessment of any sick child under 5
- **Ward** — classification drives treatment and review plans
- **Discharge** — home care advice, feeding, follow-up dates
- **Community** — teach mothers, ASHA and Anganwadi workers the danger signs

## 60-second revision

- IMNCI: whole-child assessment for 2 months to 5 years.
- Steps: assess (ask, look, feel) → classify → treat → follow up.
- Danger signs: cannot drink, vomits all, convulsions, lethargy, chest indrawing, stridor.
- Red = refer, yellow = home treatment, green = home care.
- Zinc for 14 days with ORS; first dose before referral.
- Teach the mother the danger signs before discharge.
