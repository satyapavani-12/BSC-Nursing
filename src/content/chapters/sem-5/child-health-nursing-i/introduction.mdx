## In simple words

Child health nursing is the care of infants, children and adolescents in health and illness. In India about **30% of the population is below 15 years**, so child health care is a national priority.

A child is not a small adult. Children differ in anatomy, physiology, immunity, fluid balance and psychology — and these differences change at every age.

## How children differ from adults

| Difference | Why it matters for nursing |
|---|---|
| Faster metabolism and growth | Higher fluid, calorie and drug needs; drug doses are weight-based |
| Immature immunity | Infections spread faster and become severe quickly |
| Higher body surface area | Greater fluid loss; burns more dangerous |
| Airway narrow and soft | Secretions block easily; choking risk |
| Communication is age-based | Assess by observation and parental report before speech |
| Fluid compartments shift | Dehydration develops and corrects faster |

## Child health programmes in India

| Programme | What it provides |
|---|---|
| RCH (Reproductive and Child Health) | Antenatal, intranatal and child care |
| IMNCI / IMNCI+ | Integrated management of neonatal and childhood illness |
| CSSM / Universal Immunisation Programme | Free vaccines for all children |
| ICDS (Anganwadi) | Nutrition, preschool education, growth monitoring |
| POSHAN Abhiyaan | Reducing malnutrition, stunting and anaemia |
| Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK) | Screening of children for birth defects and diseases |

## The nurse’s roles in child care

- **Caregiver** — gives medicines, feeds, fluids and comfort
- **Observer** — detects early signs of illness (danger signs)
- **Teacher** — teaches the mother nutrition, hygiene and immunisation
- **Advocate** — protects the child’s rights and interests
- **Play partner** — uses play to reduce fear and to assess development
- **Counselor** — supports the family, especially in chronic illness

<Callout type="must" title="Always think prevention">
  Most child deaths are from **preventable causes** — diarrhoea, pneumonia, measles, malnutrition. Immunisation, nutrition and early care of the sick child save more lives than any treatment.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Child health nursing covers health and illness from newborn to adolescence.
- Children differ from adults in metabolism, immunity, airway and fluid balance.
- Programmes: RCH, IMNCI, UIP, ICDS, POSHAN Abhiyaan, RBSK.
- Nurse roles: caregiver, observer, teacher, advocate, play partner, counselor.
- Prevention first: immunisation, nutrition, hygiene, early danger-sign care.
