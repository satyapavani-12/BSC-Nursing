## In simple words

Epidemiology is the study of **how disease spreads and how often it occurs in groups of people**. It answers: who is sick, where, when and why — and that guides control action.

## Common terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Incidence | Number of **new** cases in a period |
| Prevalence | Number of **all** cases at a time |
| Endemic | Constantly present in a place |
| Epidemic | Sudden increase above expected level |
| Pandemic | Epidemic in many countries |
| Sporadic | Scattered, occasional cases |
| Case fatality rate | Deaths among cases |
| Portal of entry | How the agent enters a new host |

## Chain of infection

1. **Infectious agent** — bacteria, virus, fungus, parasite
2. **Reservoir / source** — person, animal, soil, water
3. **Portal of exit** — respiratory, faeco-oral, blood, skin
4. **Mode of transmission** — contact, droplet, vehicle, vector
5. **Portal of entry** — same ways into the new host
6. **Susceptible host** — immunity, age, nutrition, illness

Nursing breaks the chain by handwashing, isolation, immunisation, safe water, food hygiene, vector control and nurse’s treatment of cases.

## Steps of outbreak investigation

1. Verify the facts and confirm the diagnosis
2. Define and count cases (person, place, time)
3. Draw the epidemic curve and map
4. Form a hypothesis about source and route
5. Control: isolate, treat, protect contacts, remove the source
6. Report to health authorities and communicate with the community
7. Follow up and evaluate control measures

<Callout type="must" title="Break the chain anywhere and stop the disease">
  You do not need to remove every link. **Handwashing, immunisation, safe water and one reported case can stop an outbreak** — that is why nursing action is so valuable.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Epidemiology: who, where, when and why of disease.
- Incidence new cases; prevalence all cases.
- Sporadic, endemic, epidemic, pandemic.
- Chain: agent, reservoir, portals, transmission, host.
- Outbreak steps: verify, define, control, report, follow up.
