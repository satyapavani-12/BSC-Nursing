## In simple words

Community nutrition assessment finds out how well a whole population is eating. It combines surveys, measurements, clinical examination and food availability, and it leads directly to nutrition education and local programmes.

## Methods of assessment

| Method | What it does |
|---|---|
| 24-hour recall | What the family ate yesterday |
| Food frequency | Which foods are eaten often |
| Weighment survey | Cooked food weighed; nutrient intake calculated |
| Food balance sheet | District-level production and availability |
| Socio-economic survey | Income, family size, food security |

## Anthropometry

| Measure | Tool | Cut-off |
|---|---|---|
| Weight for age | Spring or digital scale | Below 2 SD = underweight |
| Height for age | Stadiometer | Below 3 SD = stunting |
| Weight for height | Scale and board | Below 3 SD = wasting |
| MUAC (mid upper arm circumference) | MUAC tape | Under 11.5 cm = severe acute malnutrition |
| Head and chest circumference | Tape | Head under chest after 1 year = malnutrition |

## Common community deficiencies

| Deficiency | Sign |
|---|---|
| Protein-energy malnutrition | Underweight, wasting, oedema |
| Iron deficiency anaemia | Palmar pallor, tiredness |
| Vitamin A deficiency | Night blindness, Bitot spots, keratomalacia |
| Iodine deficiency | Goitre, cretinism |
| Vitamin D and calcium | Rickets, bowing of legs |

## Nutrition education in practice

- Use locally available food: millets, pulses, green leaves, sprouts, nuts, egg, fish
- Promote breastfeeding and weaning at 6 months with family foods
- Kitchen garden and poultry in every home
- Anaemia: iron and folic acid tablets with deworming
- Work with Anganwadi workers, self-help groups and schools

<Callout type="must" title="MUAC is the field tool">
  In the community, **MUAC tape is the fastest screening tool**. Under 11.5 cm means severe acute malnutrition; between 11.5 and 12.5 cm means moderate — refer and counsel immediately.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Diet surveys: 24-hour recall, weighment, food balance sheet.
- Anthropometry: weight, height, MUAC; MUAC is the field tool.
- Deficiencies: PEM, anaemia, vitamin A, iodine, vitamin D.
- Education: local foods, breastfeeding, kitchen garden, ICDS link.
- Severe malnutrition needs referral plus counselling.
