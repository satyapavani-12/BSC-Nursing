## In simple words

Most communicable diseases in India come from a poor environment — unsafe water, poor sanitation, refuse and vectors. Environmental health means making the home, school and community safe to live in.

## Safe water

- Requirement: about **40 litres per person per day** in rural areas and **125 litres in urban** areas
- Sources: protected well, tube well, tap water with chlorination
- **Purification:** boiling 1 minute, chlorine tablets, bleaching powder, filtration, ORT
- Water-borne diseases: cholera, typhoid, hepatitis A and E, diarrhoea, dysentery

## Sanitation

| Type | Suitable method |
|---|---|
| Excreta disposal | Pour-flush latrine, pit latrine, sanitary latrine; no open field |
| Refuse disposal | Burial, composting, incineration for medical waste |
| Sewage | Underground sewerage or septic tank |
| Kitchen hygiene | Wash utensils, cover food, keep dry |
| Personal hygiene | Handwashing after toilet, bathing, clean clothes |

## Vectors and their diseases

| Vector | Disease | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Anopheles mosquito | Malaria | Nets, repellent, IRS, removing stagnant water |
| Aedes aegypti | Dengue, chikungunya | Prevent containers from holding water; weekly dry day |
| Culex mosquito | Filariasis | Anti-larval measures; nets |
| Sandfly | Kala-azar | IRS, bed nets |
| Housefly | Diarrhoea, trachoma | Cover food; refuse disposal; screens |
| Rat | Plague, leptospirosis | Rat burrows, rodenticides |
| Ticks and lice | Typhus, relapsing fever | Hygiene, insecticides |

## Pollution

| Type | Main sources | Health effects |
|---|---|---|
| Air | Vehicles, industry, indoor smoke | Asthma, COPD, lung cancer |
| Water | Sewage, industry, fertiliser | Diarrhoea, heavy metal poisoning |
| Noise | Traffic, industry | Hearing loss, stress |
| Soil | Pesticides, refuse | Food contamination |

<Callout type="must" title="Weekly dry day">
  Aedes aegypti breeds in **clean, stored water in containers**. Ask every household to empty, scrub and dry all containers once a week to stop dengue.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Safe water 40 L rural and 125 L urban per person; boil 1 minute.
- Sanitation: latrines, refuse, sewage, kitchen and personal hygiene.
- Vectors: Anopheles-malaria, Aedes-dengue, Culex-filariasis, housefly-diarrhoea.
- Pollution: air, water, noise, soil — with their health effects.
- Nurse teaches, supervises and reports environmental hazards.
