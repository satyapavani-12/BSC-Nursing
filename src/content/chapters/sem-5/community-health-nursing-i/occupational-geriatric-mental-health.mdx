## In simple words

Some groups need special community care: **workers** exposed to hazards, **older adults** with age-related problems, and **people with mental illness** in the community. The nurse identifies, teaches, supports and links them to services.

## Occupational health

- Goal: protect workers from hazards and promote their health
- **Hazards:** dust (silicosis), chemicals, noise, heat, radiation, ergonomic strain
- **Health check:** pre-placement and periodic medical checks, PFT and audiometry
- **Safety:** helmets, gloves, goggles, safe machinery, rest breaks
- **Programmes:** ESI (Employees' State Insurance) provides care and cash benefits

## Geriatric health

| Problem | Care |
|---|---|
| Frailty and falls | Home safety, grip rails, exercise, review medicines |
| Cataract and hearing or vision loss | Screening, aids, spectacles |
| Diabetes, hypertension, heart disease | Regular check-up, medicines, diet |
| Dementia | Orientation, routine, safe home, family support |
| Depression and loneliness | Activities, family contact, day care |
| Caregiver burden | Training, respite care, counselling |
| Elder abuse | Report and protect; include in care plans |

## Community mental health

- Promote mental health and prevent relapse
- Identify early signs of illness and suicide risk
- Support adherence to medicines and support groups
- Rehabilitation and social integration, reduce stigma
- Support families and caregiver training
- Refer to DMHP or specialised services when needed

<Callout type="must" title="Keep older people independent">
  The aim of geriatric care is **ability, not dependence**. Encourage activity, safe home, family contact and timely treatment. A fall is not normal ageing — assess it.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Workers: hazard control, PPE, health checks, ESI.
- Older adults: independence, falls, vision, dementia, caregiver support.
- Community mental health: identify early, adherence, rehabilitation, reduce stigma.
- Nurse’s role: identify, teach, support, link, report.
