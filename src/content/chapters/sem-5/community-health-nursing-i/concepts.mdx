## In simple words

Community health nursing is the care of **groups of people in the place where they live, work and learn**, not only of individuals in a hospital. The aim is to prevent disease, promote health and bring care to the doorstep.

## Basic concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Community | A group of people living in a defined area with shared interests |
| Community health | The health of all people living in that community |
| Public health | Science and art of preventing disease and prolonging life |
| Primary health care | Essential care based on practical, scientific and acceptable methods |
| Health for all | Universal, equitable health coverage (Alma-Ata 1978) |

## Primary health care — five elements

1. Health education
2. Locally endemic disease prevention and control
3. Promotion of safe food supply and proper nutrition
4. Adequate safe water and basic sanitation
5. Maternal and child health and family welfare
(plus immunisation, treatment of common illness, essential drugs)

## Levels of prevention

| Level | What is done |
|---|---|
| Health promotion | Nutrition education, immunisation, health education |
| Specific protection | Vaccines, safe water, sanitation, food hygiene |
| Early diagnosis and treatment | Screening, case finding, prompt treatment |
| Disability limitation | Rehabilitation, physiotherapy, aids |
| Rehabilitation | Return the person to a useful life |

## Nurse’s roles in the community

- Direct caregiver to individuals and families
- Teacher of health and health education
- Planner of health programmes and camps
- Supervisor of ASHA, Anganwadi workers and health volunteers
- Epidemiologist — case finding, contact tracing, reporting
- Advocate for the vulnerable and for resources
- Team member in national health programmes

<Callout type="must" title="Primary health care is essential care">
  PHC is **not second-class care**. It is scientifically sound, socially acceptable, universally accessible care provided close to where people live and work, at a cost the community can afford.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Community health nursing = care of populations in their own setting.
- Community, health, public health and primary health care definitions.
- PHC from Alma-Ata 1978: essential, accessible, affordable care.
- Five levels of prevention.
- Nurse’s roles: caregiver, teacher, supervisor, epidemiologist, advocate.
