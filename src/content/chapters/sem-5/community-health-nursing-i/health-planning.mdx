## In simple words

Health planning is deciding in advance **what health services are needed, who will provide them, with what resources and how they will be checked**. In India this plan is carried out through a three-level system built on the Bhore Committee and Five-Year Plans.

## Steps of health planning

1. Survey the community — need and resources
2. Define the problem and objectives
3. Set priorities with available resources
4. Prepare the plan — manpower, money, materials, methods
5. Implement, supervise and monitor
6. Evaluate against objectives and revise

## Health infrastructure

| Level | Facility | Staff |
|---|---|---|
| Village | Sub-centre (1 per 5000 population) | ANM, male health worker |
| PHC (1 per 30000) | Primary health centre | Medical officer, staff nurse, pharmacist |
| CHC (1 per 100000) | Community health centre | Specialists, nurses, laboratory |
| District | District hospital | Full hospital team |
| State | Medical college hospital | Teaching and tertiary care |

## Planning tools

- **Five-Year Plans** — since 1951
- **Morbidity and mortality surveys** — identify real problems
- **Manpower and budget** — 4 Ms: men, money, materials, methods
- **Management tools** — Gantt charts, SWOT analysis, work plans
- **Evaluation** — output, outcome and impact indicators

<Callout type="must" title="Referral is a nursing responsibility">
  Before referring a patient, **stabilise, write the reason, records and referral letter, explain the family doubts, and arrange transport**. After referral, follow up and record the outcome.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Planning: survey, set objectives, priorities, 4 Ms, implement, evaluate.
- Levels: village/sub-centre, PHC, CHC, district hospital, medical college.
- Sub-centre 5000; PHC 30000; CHC 100000.
- Referral: stabilise, documents, letter, transport, follow-up.
- Five-Year Plans and surveys drive planning.
