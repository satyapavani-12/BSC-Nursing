## In simple words

India runs large national programmes for the major causes of illness and death. The nurse is often the person who delivers them, records them and teaches the family.

## Important national programmes

| Programme | Focus |
|---|---|
| National TB Elimination Programme (formerly RNTCP) | Free diagnosis and treatment; DOTS strategy |
| National AIDS Control Programme | Prevention, ART, PPTCT, testing |
| National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme | Malaria, dengue, filariasis, kala-azar |
| Reproductive and Child Health | Maternal, child and family welfare services |
| Universal Immunisation Programme | Free vaccines to all children and pregnant women |
| National Leprosy Eradication Programme | MDT therapy and disability prevention |
| National Programme for Control of Blindness | Cataract surgery and eye care |
| Anemia Mukt Bharat | Iron and folic acid for mothers and children |
| National Programme for Prevention of NCDs | Diabetes, hypertension, cancer, heart |
| National Mental Health Programme and DMHP | Mental health care at district level |

## TB elimination programme (DOTS)

- Diagnosis: sputum smear, CB-NAAT, chest X-ray; free of cost
- Treatment: intensive phase 2 months then continuation phase; daily regimen
- **Nikshay Poshan Yojana** gives nutrition support
- Nurse: supervise daily doses, teach cough hygiene, find contacts, prevent default

## HIV/AIDS programme

- ICTC testing, ART free, PPTCT to prevent mother-to-child transmission
- Nurse: pre- and post-test counselling, confidentiality, adherence, stigma reduction, safe sex teaching, needle safety

## Malaria control (NVBDCP)

- Radical treatment with primaquine for vivax and chloroquine and ACT for falciparum
- Nurse: fever surveillance, early RDT and slide testing, vector control, bed nets

<Callout type="must" title="Supervision is the nurse’s programme skill">
  In TB and malaria control, the nurse’s unique contribution is **direct supervision of medicine intake, default tracing and health education**. A missed dose becomes drug resistance.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- TB: DOTS, free drugs, Nikshay nutrition.
- HIV: ICTC, ART, PPTCT, confidentiality.
- Malaria: NVBDCP, ACT and primaquine, fever surveillance.
- RCH, JSY and UIP for mother and child.
- Leprosy, blindness, anaemia and NCD programmes.
