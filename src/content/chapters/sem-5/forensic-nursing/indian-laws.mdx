## In simple words

The law is a set of rules enforced by the state. Nursing practice operates inside the law — consent, confidentiality, documentation and reporting are all legal acts.

## Judicial system in India

| Court | Role |
|---|---|
| Supreme Court | Highest court; guardian of the Constitution |
| High Courts | State level courts |
| District and Sessions Courts | Trial courts in a district |
| Civil courts | Disputes between persons; compensation cases |
| Consumer commissions | Complaints of deficiency in health service |

## Criminal law

- **Indian Penal Code (IPC)** — defines offences: assault, injury, negligence, culpable homicide, abetment of suicide
- **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023** replaces the IPC from 2024 into effect, with modernised provisions
- **Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC)** — procedure for investigation, arrest, postmortem, courts

## Laws relevant to nursing

| Law | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 | Registration and standards of nursing |
| Consumer Protection Act, 2019 | Patient complaints about negligence |
| POCSO Act, 2012 | Mandatory reporting of child sexual abuse |
| Medical Termination of Pregnancy Act | Records and consent for abortion care |
| Transplantation of Human Organs Act, 1994 | Custody of organs and consent |
| Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 | Care of mentally ill persons |
| Bio-medical Waste Rules | Safe waste handling |
| Prevention of Food Adulteration Act | Hospital food safety |

## Nursing legal acts

- **Consent** — take informed consent before procedures
- **Document** — accurate, timed, signed, never falsified
- **Confidentiality** — protect records and information
- **Report** — child abuse, notifiable diseases, negligence, death
- **Testify** — honest court evidence based on records

<Callout type="must" title="Record everything, falsify nothing">
  If it is not written, it did not happen. **But what is written must be true.** Altering or fabricating records is a serious legal and professional offence.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Courts: Supreme, High, District, Consumer commissions.
- Criminal law: IPC / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita and CrPC.
- Civil and consumer law: negligence and deficiency in service.
- Key statutes: INC Act, POCSO, MTP, THOTA, MHCA, BMW Rules.
- Nursing legal acts: consent, document, confidentiality, report, testify.
