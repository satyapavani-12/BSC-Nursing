## In simple words

Forensic investigation is teamwork. The nurse is a key member who supplies victim care, first-hand observations and preserved evidence.

## The forensic team

| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| Doctor (forensic pathologist) | Examines, certifies, performs postmortem |
| Nurse | First contact, care, observation, evidence support, documentation |
| Police | Investigation, custody of accused, legal orders |
| Investigating officer | Leads case investigation and evidence collection |
| Forensic laboratory | Analyses samples and prepares reports |
| Social worker / counselor | Victim support and rehabilitation |
| Lawyer / public prosecutor | Legal proceedings |

## Types of evidence

| Type | Example | Preservation |
|---|---|---|
| Physical | Weapons, broken glass, bullets | Dry, sealed, labelled, never cleaned |
| Biological | Blood, hair, semen, tissue | Cool and dry, sealed, avoid contamination |
| Chemical | Poison, drugs, alcohol | Sealed bottle, refrigerated if needed |
| Documentation | Records, prescriptions, notes | Photograph and safe custody |

## Do’s and don’ts of evidence handling

**Do:** wear gloves, change gloves between samples, label after sealing, keep wet samples cool, record every step, hand over with signature.
**Don’t:** clean or dry bloody clothes, use the same container for different samples, leave samples unattended, label before collection, hand over without signatures.

## Scene of crime

- Nothing is touched or moved until authorised
- Record what is found, where and by whom
- Photographs and sketches are made before removal
- Samples are removed with proper documentation

<Callout type="must" title="Nursing observation is evidence">
  The nurse who sees **position of the body, spillage, smell, burns pattern or defensive injuries** provides the first and often the strongest observation. Record it immediately and factually.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Forensic team members and their roles.
- Evidence types: physical, biological, chemical, documentary.
- Preserve: seal, label, cool, sign; never clean.
- Record scene findings immediately and factually.
- Handover is signed and timed.
