## In simple words

The Constitution is the supreme law of India. It guarantees the rights of every citizen and sets the duties we owe to one another. Health care works inside that framework.

## Fundamental rights

| Right | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Right to equality | Equal treatment, no discrimination |
| Right to life and dignity | Includes access to health care |
| Right against exploitation | Protection from trafficking and forced labour |
| Right to freedom | Movement, profession, protection of privacy |
| Right to freedom of religion | Share in culture and religion |
| Right to constitutional remedies | Go to court for violation of rights |

## Fundamental duties

Respect the national flag, anthem, Constitution and ideals; protect the sovereignty of the country; promote harmony; safeguard public property; protect the environment; and strive for excellence in work.

## Rights of the patient

- Right to know the diagnosis, treatment and costs
- Right to informed consent
- Right to privacy and confidentiality
- Right to refuse or leave treatment
- Right to complain and seek second opinion
- Right to safety from harm
- Right to equal treatment regardless of status

## Duties of the nurse

- Give competent, safe and ethical care
- Maintain accurate and timely records
- Respect dignity, privacy and confidentiality
- Report hazards, errors and complaints honestly
- Follow orders within competence; clarify when not sure
- Keep knowledge current; be honest in court

<Callout type="must" title="Rights carry duties">
  Every right has a matching duty. The patient has the right to information; **the nurse has the duty to give information in a language the patient understands.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Constitution: fundamental rights and duties.
- Right to life and dignity includes health care.
- Patient rights: information, consent, privacy, refusal, complaint, safety.
- Nurse’s duties: competence, safety, records, confidentiality.
- Every right has a matching duty.
