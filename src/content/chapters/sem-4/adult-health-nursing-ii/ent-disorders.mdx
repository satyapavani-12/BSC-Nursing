## In simple terms

ENT disorders affect the ear, nose and throat — common problems that nurses see frequently in clinical practice. Understanding assessment and care helps nurses provide timely interventions and patient education.

## Common ENT disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Otitis media | Middle ear infection (bacterial/viral) | Ear pain, hearing loss, fever, irritability (children) |
| Tonsillitis | Tonsil inflammation (viral/bacterial) | Sore throat, fever, difficulty swallowing, swollen tonsils |
| Rhinitis | Nasal mucosa inflammation (allergic/infectious) | Runny nose, sneezing, nasal congestion |
| Sinusitis | Sinus cavity inflammation | Facial pain/pressure, nasal congestion, headache |
| Epistaxis | Nosebleed | Bleeding from nose (anterior/posterior) |
| Laryngitis | Larynx inflammation (viral/overuse) | Hoarseness, voice loss, dry cough |

## Nursing care

- **Otitis media** — assess ear pain, discharge, hearing loss; administer prescribed antibiotics/analgesics; teach ear care.
- **Tonsillitis** — assess throat, temperature; encourage fluids, rest; monitor for complications (peritonsillar abscess).
- **Epistaxis** — apply direct pressure to soft part of nose; have patient sit leaning forward; avoid nose blowing.
- **Sinusitis** — encourage fluids, humidification, warm compresses; administer decongestants/antibiotics as ordered.

<Callout type="must" title="Epistaxis first aid">
  **For nosebleed: sit patient leaning forward, apply direct pressure to soft part of nose for 10-15 minutes.** Do not tilt head back — blood can run down the throat causing vomiting/aspiration.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Assessment** — inspect ears, nose, throat; assess hearing, voice, breathing.
- **Medication administration** — ear drops (warm first, pull pinna up/back in adults, down/back in children), nasal sprays, throat lozenges.
- **Post-operative care** — tonsillectomy patients: monitor for bleeding, encourage cold fluids, avoid hot foods.
- **Patient teaching** — ear care (avoid cotton swabs), nasal hygiene, voice rest for laryngitis, smoking cessation.

## 60-second revision

- ENT disorders: otitis media, tonsillitis, rhinitis, sinusitis, epistaxis, laryngitis.
- Epistaxis: sit forward, direct pressure to soft nose (not head back).
- Otitis media: ear pain, hearing loss, fever (common in children).
- Tonsillitis: sore throat, fever, difficulty swallowing.
- Sinusitis: facial pain/pressure, nasal congestion, headache.
- Laryngitis: hoarseness, voice loss, dry cough.
