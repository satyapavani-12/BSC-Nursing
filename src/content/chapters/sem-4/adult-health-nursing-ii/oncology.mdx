## In simple terms

Oncological conditions (cancer) are diseases where abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and can invade nearby tissues or spread to other parts of the body. Nurses play a key role in cancer care — from symptom management to chemotherapy administration and end-of-life support.

## Common cancers

| Cancer | Risk factors | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Breast cancer | Female gender, family history, age, HRT | Breast lump, nipple discharge, skin changes |
| Lung cancer | Smoking, asbestos, radon | Cough, haemoptysis, weight loss, chest pain |
| Colorectal cancer | Age, family history, diet | Change in bowel habit, blood in stool, weight loss |
| Prostate cancer | Age, family history, race | Urinary symptoms, elevated PSA |
| Lung cancer | Smoking, asbestos, radon | Cough, haemoptysis, weight loss, chest pain |
| Leukaemia | Bone marrow cancer | Fatigue, bleeding, infection, bruising |

## Nursing care

- **Chemotherapy** — administer safely (PPE), monitor for side effects (nausea, vomiting, myelosuppression, alopecia), manage side effects
- **Pain management** — assess pain regularly, administer analgesics as ordered, use non-drug methods
- **Infection prevention** — hand hygiene, avoid crowds, monitor for fever (neutropenia = emergency)
- **Nutrition** — encourage high protein, high calorie diet; manage nausea and poor appetite
- **Patient education** — teach about treatment, side effects, when to seek help, self-care

<Callout type="must" title="Febrile neutropenia = emergency">
  **Fever (>38°C) in a chemotherapy patient is a medical emergency** — may indicate neutropenic sepsis; report immediately, start antibiotics as ordered.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Chemotherapy administration** — administer safely (PPE, double gloves), monitor for infusion reactions, manage side effects
- **Infection prevention** — hand hygiene, avoid crowds, monitor temperature (fever = emergency in neutropenic patients)
- **Pain management** — assess pain regularly, administer analgesics as ordered, use non-drug methods
- **Nutrition** — encourage high protein, high calorie diet; manage nausea and poor appetite
- **Patient education** — teach about treatment, side effects, infection prevention, when to seek help

## 60-second revision

- Oncological disorders: cancer types (breast, lung, colorectal, prostate, leukaemia).
- Chemotherapy side effects: nausea, vomiting, myelosuppression, alopecia.
- Febrile neutropenia = fever >38°C in chemotherapy patient = emergency.
- Chemotherapy safety: PPE, double gloves, cytotoxic waste disposal.
- Patient education: treatment, side effects, infection prevention, when to seek help.
