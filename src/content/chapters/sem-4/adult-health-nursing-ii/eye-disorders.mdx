## In simple terms

Eye disorders affect vision and eye health — common problems include conjunctivitis, cataract, glaucoma, retinal detachment and eye injuries. Nurses assess vision, administer eye medications and teach eye care.

## Common eye disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Conjunctivitis | Conjunctiva inflammation (viral/bacterial/allergic) | Red eye, discharge, itching, gritty sensation |
| Cataract | Lens clouding (age-related) | Blurred vision, glare sensitivity, faded colours |
| Glaucoma | Increased intraocular pressure | Gradual vision loss, halos around lights, eye pain (acute) |
| Retinal detachment | Retina separates from underlying tissue | Floaters, flashes, curtain-like vision loss |
| Conjunctivitis (pink eye) | Conjunctiva inflammation | Red eye, discharge, itching, gritty sensation |

## Nursing care

- **Eye drops** — wash hands, tilt head back, pull lower lid down, instill drop into conjunctival sac, press punctum 1 minute.
- **Eye ointment** — instill into lower conjunctival sac from inner to outer canthus.
- **Eye patch** — apply as ordered; do not patch both eyes unless ordered.
- **Post-operative care** (cataract surgery) — no rubbing, no bending over, no heavy lifting, shield at night, report sudden vision loss.

## Nursing care points

- **Visual acuity** — assess with Snellen chart; document changes.
- **Eye hygiene** — clean crusts/discharge with warm water and cotton wool; wipe from inner to outer canthus.
- **Medication administration** — eye drops before ointment; wait 5 minutes between different drops.
- **Patient teaching** — eye drop technique, hand hygiene, avoid rubbing eyes, report sudden vision changes.

<Callout type="must" title="Retinal detachment = emergency">
  **Retinal detachment is an ophthalmic emergency.** Sudden floaters, flashes of light, or curtain-like vision loss — report immediately. Delayed treatment can cause permanent vision loss.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Eye drop administration** — wash hands, check correct eye/drug, instill into conjunctival sac (not directly on cornea), press punctum 1 minute, wait 5 minutes between different drops.
- **Visual acuity assessment** — use Snellen chart; document changes; report sudden vision loss.
- **Post-operative care** (cataract surgery) — no rubbing, no bending over, no heavy lifting, wear eye shield at night, avoid swimming.
- **Patient teaching** — eye drop technique, hand hygiene, avoid rubbing eyes, report sudden vision changes or severe eye pain.
