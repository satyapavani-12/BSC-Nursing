## In simple terms

Geriatric nursing focuses on the care of older adults. As people age, they experience physiological changes and are at higher risk for chronic conditions, falls, cognitive impairment and polypharmacy. Nurses play a key role in promoting healthy ageing, preventing complications and supporting quality of life.

## Ageing changes

| System | Age-related change | Clinical implication |
|---|---|---|
| Cardiovascular | Arterial stiffness, decreased cardiac output | Hypertension, orthostatic hypotension |
| Respiratory | Decreased lung elasticity, reduced cough reflex | Increased infection risk, impaired clearance |
| Renal | Decreased GFR, reduced concentrating ability | Decreased drug clearance, electrolyte imbalance |
| Musculoskeletal | Decreased muscle mass, bone density | Falls, fractures, osteoporosis |
| Neurological | Decreased cognitive processing speed | Slower reaction time, memory changes |
| Sensory | Decreased vision, hearing, taste, smell | Safety risks, malnutrition, social isolation |

## Common geriatric conditions

| Disorder | Key signs | Nursing care |
|---|---|---|
| Delirium | Acute confusion, fluctuating consciousness | Identify cause, reorient, ensure safety |
| Dementia | Progressive cognitive decline | Memory care, safety, support ADLs |
| Falls | Multi-factorial (balance, vision, meds) | Fall prevention, environmental assessment |
| Polypharmacy | Multiple medications | Medication review, adverse effects |
| Elder abuse | Physical, emotional, financial abuse | Recognize signs, report, protect |

## Nursing care

- **Fall prevention** — assess fall risk, assist with mobility, call bell within reach, non-slip footwear, adequate lighting
- **Medication management** — medication review, monitor for adverse effects, educate about polypharmacy risks
- **Cognitive assessment** — assess cognition, orientation, memory; implement reality orientation techniques
- **Skin integrity** — assess skin integrity, prevent pressure ulcers, reposition every 2 hours
- **Nutrition** — assess nutritional status, assist with feeding, monitor weight
