## In simple terms

Male reproductive disorders affect the penis, testes, prostate and associated structures. Common conditions include BPH, prostatitis, erectile dysfunction, testicular cancer and prostate cancer. Nurses provide assessment, education and support for male reproductive health.

## Common male reproductive disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) | Enlarged prostate compressing urethra | Difficulty urinating, weak stream, nocturia, dribbling |
| Prostatitis | Prostate inflammation (bacterial/non-bacterial) | Pelvic pain, urinary frequency, dysuria, fever |
| Erectile dysfunction | Inability to achieve/maintain erection | Difficulty achieving or maintaining erection |
| Testicular cancer | Cancer of testicular tissue | Painless testicular lump, heaviness, change in size |
| Prostate cancer | Cancer of prostate gland | Urinary symptoms, elevated PSA, bone pain (advanced) |

## Nursing care

- **BPH** — encourage regular voiding, avoid decongestants and antihistamines, limit evening fluids, monitor for retention
- **Prostatitis** — administer antibiotics as ordered, encourage fluids, warm sitz baths for comfort
- **Testicular self-examination** — teach monthly self-exam, best after warm shower when scrotum is relaxed
- **Prostate cancer** — support decision-making about screening (PSA testing), provide information about treatment options
- **Post-operative care** — monitor for bleeding, infection, urinary retention after prostate surgery

<Callout type="must" title="Testicular self-examination">
  **Teach all adult males to perform monthly testicular self-examination.** Best done after a warm shower when the scrotum is relaxed. Report any lump, swelling or change immediately.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **BPH care** — monitor urinary output, encourage regular voiding, avoid decongestants and antihistamines which can worsen retention
- **Prostatitis** — administer antibiotics as ordered, encourage fluid intake, suggest warm sitz baths for comfort
- **Testicular self-exam teaching** — teach monthly self-exam, best after warm shower, report any lump or change immediately
- **Prostate cancer support** — provide information about screening and treatment options, support decision-making
- **Post-operative care** — monitor for bleeding, infection, urinary retention after prostate surgery (TURP, prostatectomy)

## 60-second revision

- Male reproductive disorders: BPH, prostatitis, ED, testicular cancer, prostate cancer.
- BPH = enlarged prostate, urinary retention, weak stream, nocturia.
- Prostatitis = prostate inflammation, pelvic pain, dysuria.
- Testicular cancer = painless lump, most common in men 15-35.
- Prostate cancer = urinary symptoms, elevated PSA, bone pain.
- Testicular self-exam: monthly, after warm shower, report any change.
- BPH: avoid decongestants/antihistamines, encourage regular voiding.
