## In simple terms

Neurological disorders affect the brain, spinal cord and nerves. Common conditions include stroke, Parkinson’s disease, multiple sclerosis, epilepsy and Bell’s palsy. Nurses assess neurological function, monitor for deterioration, and support patients with chronic neurological conditions.

## Common neurological disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Stroke (CVA) | Blood vessel blockage/rupture in brain | Facial droop, arm weakness, slurred speech, confusion |
| Parkinson’s disease | Dopamine-producing neuron loss | Tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia, postural instability |
| Multiple sclerosis | Autoimmune demyelination | Fatigue, vision problems, weakness, numbness |
| Epilepsy | Recurrent seizures | Recurrent seizures, loss of consciousness, convulsions |
| Bell’s palsy | Facial nerve inflammation | Facial droop, inability to close eye, dry eye |
| TIA | Temporary blood flow blockage | Temporary stroke-like symptoms, resolves within 24 hours |

## Nursing care

- **Stroke (CVA)** — assess with FAST (Face, Arms, Speech, Time); position head elevated 30°; monitor swallow before oral intake; monitor vital signs
- **Parkinson’s disease** — give levodopa on time; assist with mobility; encourage independence; monitor for dyskinesia
- **Multiple sclerosis** — prevent fatigue (heat intolerance); assist with mobility; monitor for bladder/bowel dysfunction
- **Epilepsy** — administer anticonvulsants as ordered; seizure precautions (padded side rails, suction, oxygen); do not restrain during seizure; do not put anything in mouth
- **Bell’s palsy** — eye care (artificial tears, eye patch at night); facial exercises; monitor for corneal abrasion

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Stroke assessment** — use FAST (Face, Arms, Speech, Time); note time of symptom onset; position head elevated 30°; monitor swallow before oral intake
- **Parkinson’s disease** — give levodopa on time; assist with mobility; encourage independence; monitor for dyskinesia
- **Multiple sclerosis** — prevent fatigue (heat intolerance); assist with mobility; monitor for bladder/bowel dysfunction
- **Epilepsy** — administer anticonvulsants as ordered; seizure precautions (padded side rails, suction, oxygen); do not restrain during seizure; do not put anything in mouth
- **Bell’s palsy** — eye care (artificial tears, eye patch at night); facial exercises; monitor for corneal abrasion
