## In simple terms

Renal, reproductive and breast pathology deals with diseases affecting the kidneys, reproductive organs and breasts. These conditions are common and nurses must understand their presentation, diagnosis and nursing care.

## Renal pathology

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Glomerulonephritis | Inflammation of glomeruli | Hematuria, proteinuria, edema, hypertension |
| Nephrotic syndrome | Heavy proteinuria, hypoalbuminemia | Massive edema, hypoalbuminemia, hyperlipidemia |
| Chronic kidney disease | Progressive loss of kidney function | Fatigue, nausea, edema, hypertension |
| Renal cell carcinoma | Malignant tumor of kidney | Hematuria, flank pain, palpable mass |

## Reproductive pathology

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Cervical cancer | HPV infection, HPV 16/18 | Abnormal vaginal bleeding, postcoital bleeding |
| Cervical dysplasia | Precancerous cervical changes | Often asymptomatic, detected on Pap smear |
| Endometrial cancer | Unopposed estrogen exposure | Postmenopausal bleeding |
| Ovarian cancer | Silent cancer, often late diagnosis | Abdominal bloating, pelvic pain, early satiety |
| Testicular cancer | Young men, cryptorchidism | Painless testicular mass, heaviness |

## Breast pathology

| Condition | Features | Management |
|---|---|---|
| Fibroadenoma | Benign breast lump, young women | Mobile, rubbery lump, excision if symptomatic |
| Fibrocystic changes | Benign breast changes | Cyclical breast pain, lumpiness |
| Breast carcinoma | Malignant tumor of breast | Lump, nipple discharge, skin changes, axillary lymphadenopathy |
| Ductal carcinoma in situ | Non-invasive breast cancer | Microcalcifications on mammogram |

## Nursing care

- **Cancer screening** - encourage mammography, Pap smear, self-breast examination
- **Symptom management** - pain control, nausea management, nutritional support
- **Emotional support** - cancer diagnosis is stressful; provide emotional support and counseling
- **Treatment support** - chemotherapy, radiation therapy, surgery support and monitoring

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Renal assessment** - monitor urine output, edema, blood pressure; assess for signs of kidney failure
- **Reproductive health** - encourage Pap smear screening, HPV vaccination, breast self-examination
- **Symptom management** - pain control, nausea management, nutritional support for cancer patients
- **Emotional support** - cancer diagnosis is stressful; provide emotional support and counseling
- **Treatment support** - chemotherapy, radiation therapy, surgery support and monitoring

## 60-second revision

- Renal disorders: glomerulonephritis, CKD, renal cell carcinoma.
- Reproductive disorders: cervical cancer, ovarian cancer, testicular cancer, BPH.
- Breast disorders: fibroadenoma, fibrocystic changes, breast carcinoma.
- Breast self-exam: monthly, after menstruation.
- Pap smear screening: cervical cancer screening for women.
- Mammography: breast cancer screening for women over 40.
