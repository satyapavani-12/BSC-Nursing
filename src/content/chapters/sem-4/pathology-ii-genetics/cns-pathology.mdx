## In simple terms

Central nervous system (CNS) pathology deals with diseases of the brain and spinal cord. Common conditions include stroke, brain tumors, Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and multiple sclerosis. Nurses care for patients with neurological conditions and must understand the pathology to provide appropriate care.

## Common CNS disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Ischemic stroke | Blood vessel blockage in brain | Sudden weakness, facial droop, slurred speech |
| Hemorrhagic stroke | Blood vessel rupture in brain | Sudden severe headache, vomiting, decreased consciousness |
| Alzheimer's disease | Neurodegenerative, amyloid plaques | Memory loss, cognitive decline, disorientation |
| Parkinson's disease | Dopamine-producing neuron loss | Tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia, postural instability |
| Brain tumor | Abnormal cell growth in brain | Headache, seizures, focal neurological deficits |
| Multiple sclerosis | Autoimmune demyelination | Fatigue, vision problems, weakness, numbness |

## Nursing care

- **Stroke assessment** - use FAST (Face, Arms, Speech, Time); position head elevated 30°; monitor swallow before oral intake
- **Parkinson's disease** - give levodopa on time; assist with mobility; encourage independence; monitor for dyskinesia
- **Multiple sclerosis** - prevent fatigue (heat intolerance); assist with mobility; monitor for bladder/bowel dysfunction
- **Brain tumor** - monitor for increased intracranial pressure; seizure precautions; pain management
- **Patient education** - teach about condition, medications, fall prevention, when to seek help

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Multiple sclerosis** - prevent fatigue (heat intolerance); assist with mobility; monitor for bladder/bowel dysfunction
- **Brain tumor** - monitor for increased intracranial pressure; seizure precautions; pain management
- **Patient education** - teach about condition, medications, fall prevention, when to seek help

## 60-second revision

- Neurological disorders: stroke, Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis, brain tumor, epilepsy.
- Stroke = FAST; medical emergency; time = brain.
- Parkinson's = tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia; levodopa on time.
- MS = fatigue, weakness; prevent heat intolerance.
- Epilepsy = seizures; seizure precautions; do not restrain; do not put anything in mouth.
- Brain tumor = monitor for increased intracranial pressure; seizure precautions.
