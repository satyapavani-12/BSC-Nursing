## In simple terms

Alternative systems of medicine are healthcare practices used instead of or alongside conventional medicine. Common systems include Ayurveda, homeopathy, naturopathy, acupuncture/traditional Chinese medicine, and Unani medicine. Patients often use these alongside conventional medicine without telling healthcare providers, which can lead to interactions and adverse effects.

Nurses should ask patients about alternative medicine use, provide education, and ensure safe integration with conventional care.

## Alternative medicine systems

| System | Origin | Main principles | Common practices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ayurveda | India | Balance of three doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) | Herbal medicines, massage, yoga, diet |
| Homeopathy | Germany | Like cures like; highly diluted substances | Highly diluted remedies |
| Naturopathy | Various | Body’s self-healing ability | Diet, lifestyle, hydrotherapy, herbal medicine |
| Traditional Chinese Medicine | China | Balance of Qi (energy flow) | Acupuncture, herbs, tai chi, qigong |
| Unani | Greece/Middle East | Balance of four humours | Herbal medicines, diet, cupping |

## Safety considerations

- **Herbal-drug interactions** - some herbs interact with conventional medicines (e.g., St. John’s Wort with antidepressants, warfarin)
- **Contamination** - some herbal products contain heavy metals, pesticides, or undeclared pharmaceutical ingredients
- **Lack of regulation** - many alternative products are not strictly regulated for safety and efficacy
- **Delayed conventional care** - patients may delay conventional treatment in favor of alternative therapies

## Nurse’s role

- **Ask about alternative medicine use** - ask non-judgmentally about herbal medicines, supplements, alternative therapies
- **Document** - document all alternative medicine use in the patient record
- **Educate** - inform patients about potential interactions, risks, and benefits
- **Collaborate** - work with the healthcare team to integrate safe complementary therapies with conventional care
- **Advocate** - advocate for patient safety and informed decision-making

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Admission assessment** - ask about alternative medicine use, herbal medicines, supplements, alternative therapies
- **Medication reconciliation** - include alternative medicines in medication reconciliation; check for interactions
- **Patient education** - inform patients about potential interactions, risks, benefits
- **Collaborate** - work with the healthcare team to integrate safe complementary therapies with conventional care
- **Advocate** - advocate for patient safety and informed decision-making

## 60-second revision

- Alternative medicine systems: Ayurveda, homeopathy, naturopathy, TCM, Unani.
- Ask about alternative medicine use - patients often do not volunteer this.
- Herbal-drug interactions: St. John’s Wort with antidepressants/warfarin; ginkgo/garlic/ginseng with anticoagulants.
- Document alternative medicine use in patient record.
- Provide patient education about risks, benefits, interactions.
