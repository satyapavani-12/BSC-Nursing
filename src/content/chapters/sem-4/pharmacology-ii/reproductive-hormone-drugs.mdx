## In simple terms

Hormonal drugs regulate reproduction and endocrine function — oral contraceptives, hormone replacement therapy (HRT), drugs for medical termination of pregnancy (MTP), and uterine stimulants (oxytocin, ergometrine). Nurses administer these, counsel patients and monitor side effects.

## Common hormonal drugs

| Class | Examples | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Combined oral contraceptive | Ethinyl estradiol + levonorgestrel | Contraception |
| Progestin-only pill | Desogestrel | Contraception (breastfeeding women) |
| Emergency contraceptive | Levonorgestrel | Emergency contraception (within 72-120 hours) |
| HRT | Oestrogen ± progesterone | Menopausal symptoms |
| Oxytocin | Syntocinon | Labour induction, postpartum haemorrhage |
| Ergometrine | Ergometrine | Postpartum haemorrhage (uterotonic) |
| Mifepristone + misoprostol | Mifepristone, misoprostol | Medical termination of pregnancy (MTP) |

## Oral contraceptives (OCPs)

- **Combined** (oestrogen + progestin) — taken daily; prevent ovulation.
- **Progestin-only** (mini-pill) — for breastfeeding women; must be taken at same time daily.
- **Emergency contraception** (levonorgestrel) — within 72 hours (up to 120 hours) of unprotected intercourse; more effective the sooner taken.
- **Side effects** — nausea, breast tenderness, breakthrough bleeding, mood changes; rare but serious: thrombosis (DVT, stroke).
- **Contraindications** — age >35 and smoking, history of thrombosis, breast cancer, uncontrolled hypertension.

## Oxytocin and ergometrine

- **Oxytocin** — labour induction, augmentation, postpartum haemorrhage (PPH) — IV infusion; monitor contractions and fetal heart rate; watch for water intoxication.
- **Ergometrine** — PPH (uterotonic) — causes strong uterine contraction; **contraindicated in hypertension, pre-eclampsia, heart disease** (causes vasoconstriction, hypertension).

## Nursing care

- **OCPs** — take at same time daily; missed pill rules; counsel on side effects and thrombosis risk; advise against smoking (especially >35 years).
- **Emergency contraception** — as soon as possible after unprotected intercourse (within 72-120 hours); not for regular use.
- **Oxytocin/ergometrine** — monitor contractions, fetal heart rate, BP; ergometrine contraindicated in hypertension/pre-eclampsia.
- **PPH management** — uterotonics (oxytocin first-line, ergometrine second-line), fundal massage, IV access, blood replacement.

<Callout type="must" title="Ergometrine contraindication">
  **Ergometrine is contraindicated in hypertension, pre-eclampsia and heart disease** — it causes vasoconstriction and can dangerously raise blood pressure. Oxytocin is preferred in these cases.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **OCP counselling** — take at same time daily; missed pill rules; advise against smoking (>35 years); monitor BP; report thrombosis symptoms (leg pain, chest pain, headache, visual changes).
- **Emergency contraception** — as soon as possible after unprotected intercourse (within 72-120 hours); not a regular contraceptive method.
- **Oxytocin/ergometrine** — monitor contractions, fetal heart rate, BP; ergometrine avoided in hypertension/pre-eclampsia.
- **PPH management** — uterotonics (oxytocin first-line), fundal massage, IV access, blood replacement; monitor for shock.
- **Patient teaching** — medication compliance, side effects, warning signs, follow-up.
