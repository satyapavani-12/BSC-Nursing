## In simple words

Professionalism means behaving like a trained, responsible member of a profession. A professional nurse has the right knowledge and skill, follows a code of conduct, takes responsibility for her actions, respects patients, keeps learning, and puts the patient’s safety first.

Nursing is a profession because it has a defined body of knowledge, formal education, a service motive, a code of ethics, self-regulation and its own organisations.

## Why nursing is a profession

| Criterion | How nursing meets it |
|---|---|
| Specialised body of knowledge | Anatomy, physiology, pharmacology, nursing science |
| Formal education | B.Sc. Nursing, GNM, M.Sc. Nursing with supervised practice |
| Service motive | Care of the sick and health promotion of society |
| Code of ethics and conduct | INC Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct |
| Self-regulation | Indian Nursing Council, State Nursing Councils |
| Professional organisation | TNAI, ICN, specialty nursing associations |
| Autonomy | Independent nursing decisions within scope of practice |

## Attributes of a professional nurse

- **Competence** — correct knowledge, skill and judgement
- **Accountability** — answerable for own actions and omissions
- **Responsibility** — completes duties reliably and on time
- **Autonomy** — makes nursing decisions within her scope
- **Assertiveness** — speaks up clearly for the patient and for herself
- **Caring and compassion** — treats every patient with dignity
- **Confidentiality** — protects patient information
- **Ethical behaviour** — follows the code of ethics and conduct
- **Continuing education** — updates knowledge throughout her career
- **Teamwork** — works respectfully with doctors and other staff
- **Professional appearance and communication** — uniform, identity badge, polite language

## Accountability, autonomy and assertiveness

| Term | Meaning | Example in the ward |
|---|---|---|
| Accountability | Being answerable for the outcome of your nursing action | Nurse documents a drug error and reports it honestly |
| Autonomy | Freedom to make nursing decisions within the scope of practice | Nurse decides to reposition a pressure-sore patient every 2 hours |
| Assertiveness | Expressing a concern firmly and respectfully | Nurse politely refuses to give a wrong dose ordered by mistake |

<Callout type="must" title="Accountability cannot be delegated">
  A nurse is **personally accountable** for every nursing action she performs or omits. A doctor’s order does not remove the nurse’s duty to check the five rights before giving a drug.
</Callout>

## Code of conduct — the essentials

- Maintain the **dignity and privacy** of every patient
- Give **safe, competent** care and never work beyond your competence
- Keep **confidentiality** of patient information
- Maintain **professional boundaries** with patients and families
- **Report** errors, negligence and unsafe practice honestly
- Respect **culture, religion and beliefs** of patients and colleagues
- Keep **registration valid** and attend continuing education
- Avoid **substance abuse** and unprofessional behaviour
- Maintain a clean **professional image** in uniform and on social media

## Professional organisations

| Organisation | Role |
|---|---|
| Indian Nursing Council (INC) | Statutory body under the INC Act 1947; sets standards of nursing education, registers qualifications |
| State Nursing Council | Registration and licensing of nurses in the state |
| Trained Nurses Association of India (TNAI) | Largest professional nursing body; supports nurses and standards |
| International Council of Nurses (ICN) | Global federation of nursing associations |
| Specialty associations | e.g. oncology, critical care, paediatric nursing groups |

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Every shift** — punctuality, correct uniform, identity badge, polite communication
- **Medication rounds** — five rights, double-checking, honest reporting of errors
- **Patient interaction** — privacy, consent, dignity, confidentiality
- **Teamwork** — respectful handover, speaking up when care is unsafe
- **Career** — renew registration, attend CNE, join a professional association
- **Social media** — never post patient photos or details

## 60-second revision

- Professionalism = behaving like a trained, responsible member of the profession.
- Seven criteria of a profession: knowledge, education, service, code, self-regulation, organisation, autonomy.
- Attributes: competence, accountability, responsibility, autonomy, assertiveness, caring, confidentiality, ethics, lifelong learning.
- Accountability = answerable; autonomy = decision within scope; assertiveness = speak up respectfully.
- Regulators: INC (statutory) and State Nursing Council (registration).
- Associations: TNAI (India), ICN (international).
- Code of conduct: dignity, privacy, confidentiality, competence, honest reporting, valid registration.
