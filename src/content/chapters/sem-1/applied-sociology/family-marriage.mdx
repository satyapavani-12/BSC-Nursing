## In simple words

The family is the basic unit of society. It is the first place where a person learns values, behaviour and attitudes toward health.

Families come in many forms — nuclear, joint and extended — and each affects how a patient is cared for at home.

Marriage is a socially approved union between two people. Its forms vary across cultures and religions.

## Types of family

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Nuclear family | Parents and their children |
| Joint family | Parents, children, grandparents, uncles, aunts living together |
| Extended family | Nuclear family plus other relatives nearby |
| Single-parent family | One parent with children |

In India, the joint family is common and gives strong support, but it can also mean many people are involved in decisions.

## Types of marriage

- **Monogamy** — one husband, one wife.
- **Polygamy** — one person, many spouses.
- **Endogamy** — marriage within the same group (caste, religion).
- **Exogamy** — marriage outside one's group.

## Family functions and health

Functions: reproduction, care of children, socialisation, emotional support, economic support, care of the sick.

In illness, the family gives care, decides about treatment, provides money and offers emotional support. Nurses should involve the family in care planning and teaching.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Identify the decision-maker** — in some families the eldest male decides; ask who should be told about the illness.
- **Involve the family** — teach them about medicines, diet and warning signs.
- **Respect the joint family** — many relatives may visit; manage visiting and information sharing politely.
- **Assess support** — a patient living alone may need more help after discharge.
- **Respect privacy** — share information only with family members the patient allows.

## 60-second revision

- Types: nuclear, joint, extended, single-parent.
- Marriage: monogamy, polygamy, endogamy, exogamy.
- Family roles: reproduction, socialisation, support, care of sick.
- Involve family in decisions and teaching.
