## In simple words

Personality is the relatively stable pattern of thoughts, feelings and behaviour that makes each person unique. It shapes how a person responds to illness, pain, treatment and nurses.

Examiners often ask about Freud's structural model and about type/trait approaches.

## Freud's structural model

| Part | Role | Operates by |
|---|---|---|
| Id | Instincts and urges | Pleasure principle |
| Ego | Reality testing | Reality principle |
| Superego | Morality and ideals | Morality principle |

The id wants immediate gratification, the superego wants to be good, and the ego balances both with reality.

## Defence mechanisms

These are unconscious ways the ego protects itself from anxiety:

| Mechanism | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Denial | Refusing to accept reality | "I am not ill" |
| Repression | Pushing painful thoughts away | Forgetting trauma |
| Projection | Blaming one's feelings on others | "They dislike me" |
| Displacement | Shifting emotion to a safer target | Shouting at family after a bad day |
| Rationalisation | Making excuses | "I failed because the test was unfair" |

## Other views of personality

- **Trait theory** — personality is made of traits such as the Big Five: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, neuroticism.
- **Humanistic (Rogers)** — people strive for self-actualisation; unconditional positive regard helps growth.
- **Type theory** — introverts vs extraverts (Jung).

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Understand reactions** — denial is common at diagnosis; give information gently and repeat it.
- **Build trust** — consistent, respectful care supports a patient's ego strength.
- **Accept feelings** — a patient projecting anger onto staff is usually afraid; do not take it personally.
- **Individualise care** — introverts may want quiet; extraverts may want more interaction.
- **Support self-esteem** — praise effort and small steps in recovery.

## 60-second revision

- Personality = stable pattern of thoughts, feelings, behaviour.
- Id = pleasure; ego = reality; superego = morals.
- Defence mechanisms: denial, repression, projection, displacement, rationalisation.
- Big Five traits: OCEAN.
- Humanistic = self-actualisation.
- Personality affects illness coping.
