## In simple words

Cognitive processes are the mental activities of knowing — sensing, perceiving, paying attention, remembering, learning, thinking and using language.

Memory has three stages: **sensory memory** (brief), **short-term/working memory** (limited, about 7 items), and **long-term memory** (large, lasting). Rehearsal moves information from short-term to long-term.

## Memory

| Stage | Duration | Capacity | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sensory memory | Seconds | Large | Afterimage of light |
| Short-term memory | Seconds to minutes | About 5–9 items | A phone number |
| Long-term memory | Days to years | Large | Your own name |

Forgetting happens through decay, interference or lack of retrieval cues. Mnemonics and repetition help memory.

## Learning

- **Classical conditioning (Pavlov)** — a neutral stimulus becomes associated with a natural response. Pavlov's dogs salivated at a bell after it was paired with food.
- **Operant conditioning (Skinner)** — behaviour is shaped by consequences: reinforcement increases behaviour, punishment decreases it.
- **Observational learning (Bandura)** — learning by watching and imitating a model.

## Thinking and intelligence

- **Intelligence** is the ability to learn, reason and solve problems.
- **IQ** is one measure; emotional intelligence (EQ) is recognising and managing emotions — vital for nurses.
- **Thinking** includes reasoning, problem-solving and decision-making.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Teach in chunks** — give one piece of information at a time; repeat it.
- **Use repetition and demonstration** — patients remember by doing and by written reminders.
- **Check understanding** — ask the patient to repeat back the instructions.
- **Use emotional intelligence** — recognise your own and the patient's feelings; stay calm.
- **Reinforce good behaviour** — praise self-care efforts (Skinner: reinforcement increases behaviour).

## 60-second revision

- Memory: sensory (seconds) → short-term (5–9 items) → long-term (large).
- Rehearsal moves info to long-term.
- Forgetting: decay, interference, no cue.
- Classical = Pavlov association; Operant = Skinner reinforcement.
- Bandura = observational learning.
- EQ = emotional intelligence.
