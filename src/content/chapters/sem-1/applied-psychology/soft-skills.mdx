## In simple words

Soft skills are the personal and social skills that help a nurse work well with patients, families and the team. Technical knowledge matters, but soft skills make the difference between good and great care.

Key soft skills: communication, empathy, teamwork, time management, stress management and professional behaviour.

## Key soft skills

| Skill | What it means |
|---|---|
| Communication | Clear speaking, listening and body language |
| Empathy | Understanding and sharing the patient's feelings |
| Active listening | Fully focusing on what the patient says |
| Teamwork | Working with doctors, aides and other nurses |
| Time management | Prioritising tasks and using time well |
| Professional behaviour | Punctuality, honesty, boundaries, confidentiality |

## Empathy vs sympathy

- **Empathy** — understand the patient's feeling ("I can see this is hard for you").
- **Sympathy** — feel sorry for the patient ("I feel sorry for you").

Empathy builds trust; sympathy can seem pitying.

## Teamwork and time management

- Ward work is a team effort — nurses, doctors, nursing assistants, therapists and cleaners.
- Handovers pass information between shifts; use SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation).
- Prioritise: urgent and important tasks first (airway, breathing, circulation, safety).

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Handover** — use SBAR to pass clear, complete information.
- **Patient talk** — listen without interrupting; reflect feelings.
- **Team conflict** — speak respectfully, focus on the patient, involve the charge nurse if needed.
- **Prioritise** — do urgent tasks first; delegate appropriately.
- **Boundaries** — be friendly but professional; do not share personal details.

## 60-second revision

- Soft skills = personal and social skills for working with people.
- Communication, empathy, listening, teamwork, time management, professionalism.
- Empathy = understand; sympathy = feel sorry.
- SBAR handover: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation.
- Prioritise urgent and important tasks first.
