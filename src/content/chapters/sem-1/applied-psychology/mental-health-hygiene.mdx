## In simple words

Mental health is a state of well-being in which a person can cope with normal stress, work productively and contribute to the community. It is not just the absence of mental illness.

Mental hygiene means the habits and practices that keep the mind healthy — managing stress, expressing feelings, sleeping well and seeking help early.

Mental illness is a diagnosable condition that disturbs thinking, feeling or behaviour and reduces a person's ability to cope. Common examples are anxiety, depression and schizophrenia.

## Signs of good mental health

- Realistic self-esteem and confidence
- Ability to form and keep relationships
- Can cope with stress and bounce back
- Makes decisions and solves problems
- Feels in control of life
- Shows appropriate emotions

## Factors affecting mental health

- Biological — genes, brain chemistry, hormones
- Psychological — coping style, self-esteem, past trauma
- Social — family support, finances, housing, culture
- Environmental — workplace stress, disasters, violence

Early warning signs of poor mental health: withdrawal, mood swings, sleep change, poor concentration, irritability, hopelessness, substance misuse.

<Callout type="must" title="Key distinction">
  **Mental health is a state of well-being; mental illness is a diagnosable condition.** Poor mental health can be improved; mental illness needs assessment and treatment.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Observe** — notice withdrawal, mood change, poor self-care or hopelessness.
- **Listen** — give time, do not judge, and let the patient express feelings.
- **Encourage help** — refer to the doctor or counsellor early; do not wait for a crisis.
- **Support routine** — regular sleep, meals, activity and social contact help recovery.
- **Safety** — if a patient talks of self-harm or suicide, stay with them and report immediately.

## 60-second revision

- Mental health = well-being, not just absence of illness.
- Mental illness = diagnosable condition.
- Signs of good mental health: confidence, relationships, coping.
- Factors: biological, psychological, social, environmental.
- Warning signs: withdrawal, mood swings, sleep change, hopelessness.
- Mental hygiene: sleep, talk, exercise, seek help early.
