## In simple words

Psychology is the scientific study of behaviour and mental processes. Behaviour is anything a person does that can be observed; mental processes are thoughts, feelings, memories and perceptions that happen inside.

The goals of psychology are to **describe, explain, predict and control** behaviour — to understand why people act as they do and to help them change when needed.

Nursing is about people, so psychology helps nurses understand patients' fears, reactions to illness, pain and stress, and to communicate better.

## Branches of psychology

| Branch | What it studies |
|---|---|
| General psychology | Basic principles of behaviour and mind |
| Developmental psychology | Growth and change across the lifespan |
| Social psychology | How people influence each other |
| Abnormal psychology | Mental illness and unusual behaviour |
| Clinical psychology | Assessment and treatment of mental disorders |
| Educational psychology | Learning and teaching |
| Industrial psychology | Behaviour at work |
| Health psychology | Behaviour and physical health |

## Perspectives on behaviour

- **Biological** — behaviour from genes, brain and hormones.
- **Behavioural** — behaviour learned from the environment.
- **Cognitive** — thoughts, beliefs and perception shape behaviour.
- **Psychoanalytic** — unconscious motives and early experience.
- **Humanistic** — personal growth and free will.

</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Understand reactions** — illness, pain and hospitalisation cause fear, anger and withdrawal; respond with empathy.
- **Communicate better** — listen, observe body language and adapt to each patient.
- **Support coping** — help patients and families adjust to diagnosis and treatment.
- **Health teaching** — use psychology to motivate behaviour change (diet, exercise, stopping smoking).
- **Teamwork** — understand colleagues' behaviour and manage workplace stress.

## 60-second revision

- Psychology = behaviour + mental processes.
- Goals: describe, explain, predict, control.
- Branches: general, developmental, social, abnormal, clinical, educational, health.
- Nursing use: understand fear/pain/stress, communicate, support coping, teach health.
- Perspectives: biological, behavioural, cognitive, psychoanalytic, humanistic.
