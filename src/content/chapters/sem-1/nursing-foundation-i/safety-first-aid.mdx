## In simple words

Patient safety means preventing harm to patients — falls, infections, medication errors and injuries. Nurses are the first line of safety.

First aid is the immediate care given before professional help arrives. It preserves life, prevents worsening and promotes recovery.

## Patient safety

- **Fall prevention** — keep the call bell within reach, non-slip footwear, uncluttered floor, good lighting, assist with walking.
- **Medication safety** — right patient, right drug, right dose, right route, right time (the five rights).
- **Fire safety** — RACE: Rescue, Alarm, Confine, Extinguish. PASS for extinguisher: Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep.
- **Safe environment** — keep beds low with side rails up, wipe spills, store chemicals safely.

## First aid for common emergencies

| Emergency | First aid |
|---|---|
| Bleeding | Direct pressure, elevate, bandage |
| Burns | Cool with clean water, cover, do not break blisters |
| Choking | Encourage coughing; back blows and abdominal thrusts if needed |
| Fainting | Lay the person flat, loosen clothing, fresh air |
| Seizure | Protect from injury, do not put anything in the mouth, turn to side after |
| Choking | Encourage coughing; back blows and abdominal thrusts if needed |

CPR: chest compressions at 100–120 per minute, depth 5–6 cm, ratio 30:2 for adults.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Prevent falls** — assist weak patients to walk, keep floors dry, keep the call bell within reach.
- **Fire** — know the nearest exit, alarm and extinguisher; use RACE and PASS.
- **Bleeding** — apply direct pressure and elevate the limb.
- **Burns** — cool with clean running water; do not apply toothpaste or ice directly.
- **Seizure** — protect the head, do not restrain, do not put anything in the mouth.

## 60-second revision

- Safety = prevent falls, errors, injury, infection.
- Five rights of medication.
- Fire: RACE (Rescue, Alarm, Confine, Extinguish).
- Extinguisher: PASS (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep).
- Bleeding: direct pressure; burns: cool water; seizure: protect, nothing in mouth.
