## In simple words

Comfort means the patient is free from pain, discomfort and anxiety. Rest and sleep help the body heal. Pain is whatever the patient says it is — always believe the patient.

Nurses assess comfort regularly and use both drug and non-drug methods to relieve pain.

## Comfort measures

| Measure | What it does |
|---|---|
| Positioning | Supine, Fowler's, lateral, prone — chosen for comfort and need |
| Back care | Prevents pressure ulcers and improves comfort |
| Temperature control | Keeps the patient neither too hot nor too cold |
| Quiet and rest | Reduces stress and helps sleep |

Fowler's position — sitting up — helps breathing. The lateral (side-lying) position is safe for unconscious patients.

## Rest and sleep

Adults need about 7–8 hours of sleep. Hospital noise, pain, worry and frequent checks can disturb sleep.

To promote sleep: reduce noise, dim lights, offer a warm drink, back massage, and cluster care so the patient is not woken often.

## Pain

Pain types:
- **Acute** — sudden, short-term, e.g. after surgery.
- **Chronic** — long-term, e.g. arthritis or cancer pain.

Pain assessment (PQRST):
- **P**rovocation — what starts it.
- **Q**uality — sharp, dull, throbbing.
- **R**egion — where it is.
- **S**everity — 0–10 scale.
- **T**ime — when it started and how long.

Non-drug relief: repositioning, massage, heat/cold, distraction, repositioning, repositioning, emotional support.

<Callout type="must" title="Pain is what the patient says">
  **Pain is whatever the patient says it is.** Believe the patient, assess with PQRST, and relieve pain promptly.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Position for comfort** — Fowler's for breathing difficulty; lateral for unconscious patients.
- **Promote sleep** — cluster care, reduce noise, dim lights at night.
- **Assess pain** — use PQRST and the 0–10 scale; ask the patient to rate pain.
- **Give analgesia** — as prescribed, and re-check that it worked.
- **Non-drug relief** — repositioning, massage, heat or cold packs, distraction.

## 60-second revision

- Comfort = free from pain, discomfort, anxiety.
- Positions: supine, Fowler’s, lateral, prone.
- Pain = what the patient says; use PQRST and 0–10 scale.
- Sleep: 7–8 hours; cluster care, quiet, dim lights.
- Non-drug relief: repositioning, massage, heat/cold.
