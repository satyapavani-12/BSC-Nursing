## In simple words

Equipment and linen are the tools and fabrics nurses use every day. They must be clean, safe, working and ready when needed.

Linen includes bedsheets, blankets, pillowcases and patient gowns. Equipment ranges from a thermometer to a BP apparatus and an oxygen cylinder.

## Common hospital equipment

| Equipment | Use |
|---|---|
| Thermometer | Measure temperature |
| BP apparatus | Measure blood pressure |
| Stethoscope | Listen to heart and lungs |
| Oxygen cylinder | Give oxygen |
| Suction apparatus | Remove secretions |
| Weighing scale | Measure weight |
| Wheelchair / stretcher | Move patients |

## Linen

Types: bedsheets, draw sheets, blankets, pillowcases, patient gowns, towels, mackintosh (waterproof sheet).

Linen handling rules:
- Never shake linen in the air — it spreads dust and germs.
- Hold soiled linen away from your body.
- Use gloves when handling infected linen.
- Send infected linen for separate washing.

## Care of equipment

- Clean after each use.
- Check that it works before using on a patient.
- Report broken or faulty equipment.
- Store in the right place so others can find it.

<Callout type="must" title="Infected linen">
  **Handle infected linen with gloves and send it for separate washing.** Never shake soiled linen — germs spread through the air.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Check before use** — make sure the thermometer, BP apparatus and oxygen are working.
- **Handle linen safely** — protect yourself and prevent infection spread.
- **Report faults** — broken equipment must be taken out of use and reported.
- **Store correctly** — clean linen and equipment go to their proper place.
- **Count and control** — keep track of equipment and linen on your ward.

- Equipment: thermometer, BP, stethoscope, oxygen, suction, scale, wheelchair.
- Linen: bedsheets, blankets, pillowcases, gowns, mackintosh.

- Gloves + separate wash for infected linen.
- Check equipment before use; report faults.
