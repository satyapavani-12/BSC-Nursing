## In simple words

Modern nursing began with Florence Nightingale during the Crimean War (1854–56). She showed that cleanliness, fresh air and good care reduce death, and she started the first nursing school.

In India, nursing began with missionary and government efforts, and the Indian Nursing Council (INC) was set up in 1947 to regulate nursing education and practice.

Nursing is a profession because it has specialised knowledge, formal education, a code of ethics, a professional organisation and service to society.

## Florence Nightingale

- Known as the founder of modern nursing.
- Improved hygiene in military hospitals during the Crimean War.
- Called "the Lady with the Lamp."
- Started the Nightingale School of Nursing at St. Thomas' Hospital, London (1860).
- Her environmental theory: fresh air, light, warmth, cleanliness, quiet and good food help healing.

## Nursing in India

- The first nursing school in India started in 1868 at St. Stephen's Hospital, Delhi.
- The Trained Nurses' Association of India (TNAI) was formed in 1905.
- The Indian Nursing Council (INC) was constituted in 1947 under the Indian Nursing Council Act.
- INC sets standards for nursing education and registers nurses.

## Nursing as a profession

A profession has:
- Specialised body of knowledge
- Formal education and training
- Code of ethics
- Professional organisation (TNAI, INC)
- Service motive and autonomy

Nurses are accountable for their practice and must keep learning.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Apply Nightingale's principles** — keep the ward clean, well-ventilated, quiet and well-lit.
- **Know your council** — INC sets education standards and registers nurses in India.
- **Follow the code of ethics** — respect patient dignity, confidentiality and rights.
- **Keep learning** — professions require continuing education and updating skills.
- **Be proud of the profession** — nursing is a respected, accountable profession.
