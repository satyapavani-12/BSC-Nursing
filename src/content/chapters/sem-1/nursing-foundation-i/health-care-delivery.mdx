## In simple words

Health care delivery is how health services are organised and given to people. In India, care is delivered through government and private services at primary, secondary and tertiary levels.

A patient usually starts at a primary level (PHC or sub-centre) and is referred higher if needed.

## Levels of care

| Level | Example | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Sub-centre, PHC | First contact; basic care, prevention, immunisation |
| Secondary | CHC, district hospital | Specialist care, referral from primary |
| Tertiary | Medical college hospital | Advanced care, super-specialists, teaching |

## India's rural health system

- **Sub-centre** — serves about 5,000 people; run by an ANM.
- **Primary Health Centre (PHC)** — serves about 30,000 people (plains) or 20,000 (hilly/tribal); run by a medical officer.
- **Community Health Centre (CHC)** — serves about 120,000 people; has specialists and beds.

## National programmes

- **National Health Mission (NHM)** — improves rural and urban health.
- **Ayushman Bharat** — has two parts: Health and Wellness Centres and PM-JAY health insurance.
- **ABHA** — Ayushman Bharat Health Account, a digital health ID.

<Callout type="must" title="First contact is primary care">
  **Most people first meet the health system at a sub-centre or PHC.** Nurses at these centres give immunisation, antenatal care and basic treatment.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Know your place** — a PHC nurse gives primary care and refers upward when needed.
- **Use referral correctly** — send a patient higher only when the centre cannot handle it.
- **Support national programmes** — immunisation, antenatal care and health camps run through PHCs and CHCs.
- **Help with Ayushman Bharat** — guide eligible patients to enrol and use PM-JAY benefits.
- **Work as a team** — ANM, staff nurse, medical officer and specialist each have a role.

## 60-second revision

- Levels: primary, secondary, tertiary.
- Sub-centre (~5,000, ANM) → PHC (~30,000, MO) → CHC (~120,000, specialists).
- Referral chain upward to district hospital and medical college.
- NHM, Ayushman Bharat (HWC + PM-JAY), ABHA digital ID.
- PHC nurse gives primary care and refers.
