## In simple words

Anatomy needs a fixed starting position so that "above" and "left" mean the same thing to everyone. That position is the **anatomical position**: standing upright, face forward, arms by the side, palms facing forward, feet together.

From that position we describe the body using planes, directions and movement words. Learn these first — every later chapter uses them.

## Key points

**Levels of organisation:** cell → tissue → organ → organ system → organism.

**Four tissue types:** epithelial (covering), connective (support), muscular (movement), nervous (control).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Anterior / ventral | Towards the front |
| Posterior / dorsal | Towards the back |
| Superior | Towards the head |
| Inferior | Towards the feet |
| Medial | Towards the midline |
| Lateral | Away from the midline |
| Proximal | Nearer the trunk |
| Distal | Farther from the trunk |
| Superficial | Near the surface |
| Deep | Away from the surface |
| Supine | Lying on the back |
| Prone | Lying on the face |

**Planes**
- **Sagittal** — divides left and right.
- **Coronal (frontal)** — divides front and back.
- **Transverse (horizontal)** — divides upper and lower.

**Movements:** flexion (bending), extension (straightening), abduction (away from midline), adduction (towards midline), rotation, circumduction, supination (palm up), pronation (palm down), inversion/eversion (sole in/out), dorsiflexion/plantar flexion (foot up/down).

**Body cavities:** cranial (brain), vertebral (spinal cord), thoracic (heart, lungs), abdominal (gut, liver, spleen, kidneys), pelvic (bladder, rectum, reproductive organs). The diaphragm separates thoracic from abdominal.

## Memory trick

**Sagittal = “S” for side-to-side split. Coronal = “C” for crown (front/back). Transverse = across.**

For proximal/distal think: **“PROximal = close to home”**.

## 60-second revision

- Anatomical position = palms forward, standing.
- Sagittal / coronal / transverse planes.
- Medial = midline, lateral = outside.
- Supine back, prone face down.
- Four tissue types, five levels of organisation.
