## In simple words

The nervous system controls and coordinates everything the body does. It has two main parts:

- **Central nervous system (CNS)** — the brain and spinal cord. It receives information, makes decisions and sends instructions.
- **Peripheral nervous system (PNS)** — all the nerves that run from the CNS to the body. It carries messages in both directions.

The basic working cell is the **neuron**: a cell body, dendrites that receive signals, and an axon that sends them. Many axons are wrapped in a fatty **myelin sheath**, which speeds up the signal.

## The brain

| Part | Main function |
|---|---|
| Cerebrum | Thinking, memory, speech, movement, senses; the largest part |
| Cerebellum | Balance, posture and coordinated movement |
| Brainstem (midbrain, pons, medulla) | Breathing, heart rate, blood pressure, swallowing |
| Diencephalon (thalamus, hypothalamus) | Relay of sensations; controls body temperature, hunger, thirst |

The brain floats in **cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)**, which cushions it. CSF is made in the choroid plexus and circulates through the ventricles.

The **meninges** are three coverings — dura mater (tough outer), arachnoid mater (middle web-like) and pia mater (thin inner layer closely on the brain).

## The spinal cord

The spinal cord runs inside the vertebral column from the foramen magnum to about L1–L2 in adults. Below that, the nerve roots continue as the **cauda equina**. It has:

- **Grey matter** — butterfly-shaped in the middle; cell bodies.
- **White matter** — outer area; myelinated axons.

31 pairs of **spinal nerves** come off the cord: 8 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral, 1 coccygeal.

**Cranial nerves** — 12 pairs come directly from the brain (I olfactory to XII hypoglossal). They mainly serve the head and neck.

## Reflexes

A **reflex arc** is the shortest nerve path: receptor → sensory neuron → interneuron in the cord → motor neuron → effector (muscle). A knee jerk is a spinal reflex — it happens before the brain even knows.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Level of consciousness** — use AVPU (Alert, responds to Voice, Pain, or Unresponsive) and record it.
- **Pupils** — check size, equality and reaction to light; unequal pupils can mean brain injury.
- **Limb movement** — test power and note any weakness on one side (possible stroke).
- **Spinal injury** — keep the spine still; log-roll the patient; never bend the neck.
- **Seizure** — protect from injury, do not put anything in the mouth, time the seizure, place in recovery position after.

## 60-second revision

- CNS = brain + spinal cord; PNS = nerves.
- Cerebrum = thought, speech, movement; cerebellum = balance.
- Medulla = breathing, heart rate, blood pressure.
- Meninges: dura, arachnoid, pia; CSF cushions.
- 31 spinal nerves, 12 cranial nerves.
- Grey matter = cell bodies; white matter = axons.
- Reflex arc: receptor → sensory → cord → motor → muscle.
