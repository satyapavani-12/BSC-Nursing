## In simple words

Sensory organs turn one kind of energy into nerve impulses. The eye turns light into sight, the ear turns sound waves into hearing and also senses balance, the nose detects chemicals for smell, the tongue detects them for taste, and the skin senses touch, heat, cold and pain.

All sensory messages travel to the brain for interpretation.

## The eye

The eye has three layers:

| Layer | Parts | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Outer (fibrous) | Sclera, cornea | Protection; cornea refracts light |
| Middle (vascular) | Choroid, ciliary body, iris | Nourishment; pupil size; focus |
| Inner (nervous) | Retina | Contains rods and cones |

Light passes: **cornea → aqueous humour → pupil → lens → vitreous humour → retina**.

- **Rods** work in dim light and detect black and white.
- **Cones** work in bright light and detect colour; they are concentrated at the fovea.
- The **optic nerve** carries impulses to the visual cortex.

The lens changes shape to focus — called **accommodation**. In **myopia** (short sight) the image falls in front of the retina; in **hypermetropia** (long sight) it falls behind.

## The ear

The ear has three parts:

- **Outer ear** — pinna and ear canal; collects sound.
- **Middle ear** — tympanic membrane and three ossicles: malleus, incus, stapes. The eustachian tube keeps air pressure equal.
- **Inner ear** — cochlea for hearing and semicircular canals for balance.

Sound path: **pinna → ear canal → tympanic membrane → ossicles → oval window → cochlea → auditory nerve → brain**.

The **organ of Corti** inside the cochlea holds the hair cells that convert sound into impulses.

## Nose, tongue and skin

- **Nose** — olfactory receptors in the roof of the nasal cavity detect smell.
- **Tongue** — taste buds detect sweet, salty, sour, bitter and umami. Taste depends heavily on smell.
- **Skin** — receptors for touch (Meissner), pressure (Pacinian), pain (free nerve endings), heat and cold. The skin is also the first line of defence.

<Callout type="must" title="Two facts that come up every year">
  **Rods see black and white in dim light; cones see colour in bright light.** In the ear, the **cochlea** is for hearing and the **semicircular canals** are for balance.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Eye drops** — instil into the lower conjunctival sac, never touch the dropper to the eye.
- **Eye surgery** — protect the eye, avoid pressure, teach the patient not to rub it.
- **Ear drops** — warm them to room temperature; in adults pull the pinna up and back.
- **Hearing loss** — face the patient, speak clearly, reduce background noise, never shout.
- **Unconscious patient** — corneal reflex and pupil reaction are checked as part of neurological observation.

## 60-second revision

- Eye: sclera/cornea, choroid/iris, retina.
- Rods dim, cones colour.
- Light → cornea → lens → retina → optic nerve.
- Ear: outer, middle (three ossicles), inner (cochlea + canals).
- Cochlea hearing; canals balance.
- Nose smells, tongue tastes, skin feels.
- Myopia concave lens; hypermetropia convex lens.
