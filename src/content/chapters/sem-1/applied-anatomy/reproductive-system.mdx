## In simple words

The reproductive system makes gametes — sperm in males and ova (egg cells) in females. Fertilisation usually happens in the fallopian tube, and the fertilised egg implants in the uterus.

The male system makes sperm and delivers them. The female system produces ova, receives sperm, nurtures the embryo and gives birth.

## Female reproductive organs

| Organ | Function |
|---|---|
| Ovaries | Produce ova and the hormones oestrogen and progesterone |
| Fallopian tubes | Carry the ovum from ovary to uterus; site of fertilisation |
| Uterus | Nurtures the fertilised egg; sheds its lining each month as menstruation |
| Cervix | Lower narrow part of the uterus; opens into the vagina |
| Vagina | Birth canal; receives the penis during intercourse |
| Vulva | External female genital organs |

The **menstrual cycle** averages 28 days. Ovulation (release of an ovum) occurs around day 14, triggered by LH. If pregnancy does not occur, the uterine lining sheds — menstruation.

The ovaries alternate releasing an ovum each month.

## Male reproductive organs

| Organ | Function |
|---|---|
| Testes | Produce sperm and testosterone in the scrotum |
| Epididymis | Stores and matures sperm |
| Vas deferens | Carries sperm from epididymis to the urethra |
| Seminal vesicles and prostate | Add fluid to sperm to form semen |
| Urethra | Carries semen or urine out through the penis |
| Penis | Delivers semen into the vagina |

Spermatogenesis (sperm formation) needs a temperature slightly below body temperature — that is why the testes sit outside the body in the scrotum.

Fertilisation usually occurs in the **ampulla of the fallopian tube**. Implantation occurs about 6–7 days after fertilisation in the **endometrium** of the uterus.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Menstrual history** — ask about cycle length, flow and pain; heavy bleeding needs review.
- **Antenatal care** — confirm pregnancy, calculate the expected date, and encourage folic acid early.
- **Family planning** — explain condom, pill, IUD and implant options without pressure.
- **Self-examination** — teach breast self-exam and testicular self-exam as routine checks.
- **Hygiene** — perineal care from front to back prevents urinary infection.

## 60-second revision

- Ovaries make ova + oestrogen/progesterone; testes make sperm + testosterone.
- Fertilisation in fallopian tube; implantation in uterus.
- Cycle averages 28 days; ovulation around day 14.
- Female: ovary → tube → uterus → cervix → vagina.
- Male: testis → epididymis → vas deferens → urethra → penis.
- Testes outside body for lower temperature.
