## In simple words

The heart pumps blood around the body in a repeating sequence called the **cardiac cycle**. Each heartbeat has two phases:

- **Systole** — the chambers contract and push blood out.
- **Diastole** — the chambers relax and fill with blood.

The two heart sounds ("lub-dub") come from the valves closing: "lub" when the AV valves close at the start of ventricular systole; "dub" when the semilunar valves close at the end.

## Cardiac output

Cardiac output is the volume of blood the heart pumps per minute:

**Cardiac output = heart rate × stroke volume**

Normal values:
- Heart rate: 60–100 beats/minute at rest
- Stroke volume: about 70 mL per beat
- Cardiac output: about 5 litres/minute

**Stroke volume** is the amount of blood ejected by one ventricle in one beat.

## Blood pressure

Blood pressure is the force of blood against artery walls:

- **Systolic** — pressure during ventricular contraction (normally about 120 mmHg).
- **Diastolic** — pressure during relaxation (normally about 80 mmHg).
- **Pulse pressure** — systolic minus diastolic (normally 30–50 mmHg).

Blood pressure is controlled by the heart, blood vessels, kidneys and nervous system. Baroreceptors in the carotid sinus and aorta detect changes and adjust heart rate and vessel tone.

Blood moves through vessels by pressure difference: arteries (high pressure) → arterioles → capillaries (exchange) → venules → veins (low pressure) → heart.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Pulse** — count for a full minute if irregular; note rate, rhythm and strength.
- **Blood pressure** — use the correct cuff size, arm at heart level, deflate 2–3 mmHg per second.
- **Shock** — fast weak pulse, falling BP, cold clammy skin; report immediately.
- **Hypertension** — encourage medication compliance, low salt, regular checks.
- **After cardiac surgery** — monitor ECG, heart rate, BP and urine output hourly as ordered.

  Heart sounds: **“Lub closes AV, Dub closes semilunar.”** The first sound is longer and duller; the second is shorter and sharper.

## 60-second revision

- Systole = contract; diastole = fill.
- Lub = AV valves close; dub = semilunar valves close.
- Cardiac output = HR × SV; normal 5 L/min.
- BP: systolic 120, diastolic 80; pulse pressure 30–50.
- Arteries away, veins back, capillaries exchange.
- SA node = pacemaker.
