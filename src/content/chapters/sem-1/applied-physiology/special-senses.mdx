## In simple words

Special senses turn one kind of energy into nerve impulses that the brain interprets. The eye turns light into sight, the ear turns sound into hearing (and also senses balance), and the nose and tongue detect chemicals for smell and taste.

The eye has a focusing system (cornea and lens) and a sensing layer (retina). The ear has a collecting part, a middle-ear amplifier, and an inner-ear sensory part.

## Vision

Light passes: **cornea → aqueous humour → pupil → lens → vitreous humour → retina**.

- The **cornea** and **lens** refract (bend) light so it focuses on the retina.
- The **iris** changes the pupil size to control how much light enters.
- **Accommodation** is the lens changing shape to focus near or far objects.
- **Rods** work in dim light (black and white); **cones** work in bright light (colour).
- The **optic nerve** carries signals to the visual cortex.

Common errors:
- **Myopia** (short sight): image focuses in front of the retina; corrected with a concave lens.
- **Hypermetropia** (long sight): image focuses behind the retina; corrected with a convex lens.

## Hearing and balance

Sound path: **pinna → ear canal → eardrum → ossicles (malleus, incus, stapes) → oval window → cochlea → auditory nerve**.

- The **eardrum and ossicles** amplify sound vibrations.
- The **cochlea** contains the organ of Corti with hair cells that convert sound to impulses.
- The **semicircular canals** sense head movement and balance.
- The **eustachian tube** equalises pressure between the middle ear and the throat.

## Smell and taste

- **Smell** — olfactory receptors high in the nasal cavity detect airborne chemicals.
- **Taste** — taste buds on the tongue detect sweet, salty, sour, bitter and umami. Flavour is mostly smell.

<Callout type="must" title="Two facts that come up every year">
  **Rods see black and white in dim light; cones see colour in bright light.** In the ear, the **cochlea** is for hearing and the **semicircular canals** are for balance.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Eye drops** — instil into the lower conjunctival sac; never let the dropper touch the eye.
- **Hearing loss** — face the patient, speak clearly, reduce background noise.
- **Eye injury** — cover both eyes (movement in one moves the other) and get urgent help.
- **Dizziness** — help the patient move slowly; fall precautions.
- **Poor appetite** — loss of smell reduces taste; offer strongly flavoured, attractive food.

## 60-second revision

- Light → cornea → lens → retina → optic nerve.
- Rods dim/black-white; cones bright/colour.
- Myopia concave lens; hypermetropia convex lens.
- Sound → eardrum → ossicles → cochlea → nerve.
- Cochlea hearing; canals balance.
- Smell = olfactory; taste = 5 basic tastes.
