## In simple words

This chapter covers two control systems. The **reproductive system** is controlled by hormones from the hypothalamus, pituitary and gonads, producing the menstrual cycle in females and sperm continuously in males.

The **nervous system** uses electrical impulses along neurons and chemical signals across synapses. It senses, integrates and responds — the somatic system for voluntary movement and the autonomic system for involuntary body functions.

## Reproductive physiology

The **menstrual cycle** averages 28 days and has three phases:

| Phase | Days | Main events |
|---|---|---|
| Menstrual | 1–5 | Uterine lining sheds |
| Follicular | 6–14 | Follicle grows; oestrogen rises; ovulation near day 14 |
| Luteal | 15–28 | Progesterone rises; if no pregnancy, lining sheds again |

Key hormones:
- **FSH** — follicle growth
- **LH** — triggers ovulation
- **Oestrogen** — builds the uterine lining
- **Progesterone** — maintains the lining for pregnancy

In males, sperm are made continuously in the testes under FSH and LH, with testosterone from the testes under LH control.

## Nervous system

The neuron has a **cell body**, **dendrites** (receive signals) and an **axon** (sends signals). Many axons have a **myelin sheath** that speeds conduction.

A nerve impulse is an electrical change called an **action potential**. At the synapse, the impulse triggers release of a **neurotransmitter** across the gap to the next cell.

- **Somatic nervous system** — voluntary control of skeletal muscle.
- **Autonomic nervous system** — involuntary control of heart, gut, glands:
  - **Sympathetic** — fight or flight (heart rate up, digestion down).
  - **Parasympathetic** — rest and digest (heart rate down, digestion up).

  **Ovulation occurs around day 14 of a 28-day cycle**, triggered by the LH surge. The fertile window is the few days around ovulation.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Menstrual history** — ask about cycle length, flow, pain and missed periods; pregnancy test if a period is missed.
- **Contraception counselling** — explain options without judgement; support informed choice.
- **Neurological observation** — check consciousness, pupils, limb movement and response regularly.
- **Autonomic signs** — fast heart rate, sweating, high BP can mean pain, fear or shock.
- **Patient safety** — a patient with limb weakness needs fall precautions and help with mobility.

## 60-second revision

- Cycle 28 days; ovulation day 14 by LH surge.
- FSH follicle, LH ovulation, oestrogen builds lining, progesterone maintains.
- Neuron: dendrite → cell body → axon → synapse.
- Action potential = nerve impulse.
- Somatic voluntary; autonomic involuntary.
- Sympathetic fight/flight; parasympathetic rest/digest.
