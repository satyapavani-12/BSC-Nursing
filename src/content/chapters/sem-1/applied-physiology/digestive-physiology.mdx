## In simple words

Digestion turns food into tiny particles that can pass into the blood. It uses both **mechanical digestion** (chewing and churning) and **chemical digestion** (enzymes that split large molecules into small ones).

Each enzyme has a specific job — amylase for starch, protease for protein, lipase for fat. None of them work outside their best pH, which is why stomach acid and intestinal alkali both matter.

## Enzymes and what they digest

| Enzyme | Where it acts | Breaks down | End product |
|---|---|---|---|
| Salivary amylase | Mouth | Starch | Maltose |
| Pepsin | Stomach | Protein | Peptides |
| Pancreatic amylase | Small intestine | Starch | Maltose |
| Pancreatic lipase | Small intestine | Fat | Fatty acids + glycerol |
| Trypsin / peptidases | Small intestine | Protein | Amino acids |

Bile from the liver is stored in the gall bladder and released into the duodenum. It does not contain enzymes — it **emulsifies fat**, breaking big fat droplets into tiny ones so lipase can attack them.

## Absorption

Most absorption happens in the small intestine, especially the duodenum and jejunum. The villi and microvilli give a huge surface area. Each villus contains a blood capillary network and a lymphatic capillary called a **lacteal**, which absorbs fat.

- Glucose and amino acids → blood capillaries.
- Fatty acids and glycerol → lacteals (as chyle).
- Water, salts and some vitamins → large intestine.

The liver processes absorbed nutrients: it converts excess glucose to glycogen, makes plasma proteins, and detoxifies harmful substances.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Tube feeding** — check placement, flush before and after feeds, and give feeds slowly to prevent diarrhoea.
- **Vomiting or diarrhoea** — watch for dehydration and electrolyte loss; record amount and character.
- **Jaundice** — yellow skin and eyes suggest liver or bile-duct problems; check urine colour.
- **Pancreatitis** — severe upper abdominal pain radiating to the back; keep nil by mouth as ordered.
- **Weight and diet** — record intake; refer to dietetics for diabetes, kidney or liver disease.

## 60-second revision

- Mechanical + chemical digestion.
- Amylase → starch; protease → protein; lipase → fat.
- Bile emulsifies fat, no enzymes.
- Villi absorb; lacteals take fat, blood takes sugar and amino acids.
- Liver: glycogen, plasma proteins, detox.
