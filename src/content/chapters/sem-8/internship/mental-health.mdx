## In simple words

The mental health internship trains the student to communicate therapeutically, assess mental state, keep the ward safe, administer medicines and support rehabilitation.

## Objectives

- Apply the nursing process to patients with mental illness
- Perform mental status examination and risk assessment
- Establish a therapeutic relationship
- Give psychiatric medicines and monitor side effects
- Practise observation of mood, behaviour, feeding, sleep and self-care
- Practise de-escalation and safety of the confused or violent patient
- Support families and take part in rehabilitation activities
- Practice ethical and legal responsibilities in psychiatric care

## Skills practised

- Mental status examination and objective documentation
- Therapeutic communication, active listening
- Observation of mood, behaviour, feeding, sleep and self-care
- Medicine administration and monitoring of side effects (tremor, rigidity, weight)
- ECT and electroconvulsive therapy supervision
- Observation recordings, process records, incident reports
- De-escalation, escort and seclusion as last resort
- Rehabilitation and activity therapy
- Team and family work

<Callout type="must" title="Safety first">
  Check for suicide and violence risk at every shift, **remove the means of self-harm, and do not leave a suicidal patient alone**. Confidential and accurate documentation protects the patient and the nurse.
</Callout>
