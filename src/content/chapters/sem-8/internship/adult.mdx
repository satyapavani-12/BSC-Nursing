## In simple words

The adult health internship is six weeks of supervised clinical practice. It converts theory into safe, timely and competent patient care.

## Objectives

- Apply the nursing process to medical and surgical patients
- Practise assessment, monitoring of vitals and comfort
- Give medicines and intravenous therapy safely
- Perform procedures: feeding, wound care, catheters, drains, oxygen therapy
- Practise pre- and post-operative care
- Take part in emergency and critical care
- Communicate with patient, family and team
- Maintain clinical records and participate in handover

## Placement pattern

The student rotates through medical, surgical, ICU, casualty and specialty wards for six weeks. Each day begins with a handover, follows the daily plan, and ends with documentation of activities.

## Skills to be performed

- Vital signs, pain assessment, blood sugar
- Medication administration by the five rights
- Intravenous therapy, cannula, blood transfusion
- Oxygen therapy, nebulisation, suction
- Feeding: nasogastric tube, Ryles tube, care of the tube
- Urinary catheter and ostomy care
- Wound dressing, drain and sutures
- Pre- and post-operative preparation and complication prevention
- CPR support, triage and first aid

<Callout type="must" title="Supervision level by level">
