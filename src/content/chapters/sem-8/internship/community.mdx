## In simple words

The community health internship is four weeks of supervised practice. The student delivers care, teaches families and keeps records in a village or urban community.

## Objectives of the internship

- Apply nursing process to a family
- Assess the community and its health problems
- Provide antenatal, newborn and child care
- Conduct health education and camps
- Work with ASHA, Anganwadi workers and the community
- Maintain the registers and reports

## Activities in the community internship

- Family visits with home assessment and nursing process
- Antenatal and postnatal visits with follow-up
- Immunisation sessions and growth monitoring
- Screening for anaemia, hypertension and diabetes in community camps
- School health programmes, health education and nutrition teaching
- Home-based care of the old, disabled and sick
- Epidemiological investigation and outbreak follow-up work
- Community survey and the village map

## Placement pattern

The student spends the week in the sub-centre and village, does field rounds with supervisors, and prepares records and the weekly report.

<Callout type="must" title="Supervision and safety">
  Work always under supervision, keep the family informed, respect privacy and confidentiality, and **record every activity honestly**. The final evaluation includes competence, attitude and record quality.
</Callout>
