## In simple words

The midwifery internship trains the student to care for mothers during pregnancy, labour, birth and after, and for their newborns, under supervision.

## Objectives

- Apply nursing process to antenatal, labour and postnatal mothers
- Take a booking history and perform antenatal examination
- Monitor labour, use the partograph and assist deliveries
- Give care to the mother after birth and to the newborn
- Support breastfeeding and teach the mother
- Identify danger signs and refer urgently
- Maintain midwifery records and reports

## Skills practised

- Booking history and antenatal examination
- Fundal height, fetal heart and danger sign assessment
- Support and preparation of the mother in labour
- Monitoring contractions, cervical dilatation and fetal heart; using the partograph
- Conducting a normal delivery under supervision
- Care of the placenta, active management of third stage
- Immediate newborn care: warmth, cord, vitamin K, APGAR
- Postnatal observation of mother and baby, breastfeeding support
- Contraception counselling and postnatal visits

<Callout type="must" title="Always escalate abnormal labour">
  Signs such as non-descent, constant pain, fetal distress or heavy bleeding need **urgent obstetric referral**. A student never manages an emergency alone — call, prepare, support and document.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Antenatal: booking, examination, screening, counselling.
- Labour: monitoring, partograph, assisted delivery, placenta.
- Newborn: warmth, cord, vitamin K, breastfeeding within one hour.
- Postnatal: mother and baby checks, danger signs, planning.
- Emergencies: APH, PPH, eclampsia and cord prolapse.
