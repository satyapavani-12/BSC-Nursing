## In simple words

The child health internship trains the student to care for infants and children in the ward, keep their families at the centre, and use growth, feeding and play as tools.

## Objectives

- Apply the nursing process to sick children and their families
- Practise feeding, nutrition and growth assessment
- Give medicines and intravenous therapy for weight-based doses
- Practise IMNCI assessment and care
- Communicate with child and parent; use play therapy
- Practise infection control and isolation for children
- Support parents and prepare the family for discharge

## Skills practised

- Age-based communication and assessment
- Vital signs, weight, height, MUAC, growth chart
- Comfort and positioning
- Paediatric medication by weight; nebulisation; oxygen; IV fluids
- Feeding: breastfeeding support, nasogastric tube, paediatric feeds
- IMNCI classification and danger signs
- Isolation and infection control for children
- Play and activity therapy; preparation for procedures
- Discharge planning and parent counselling

<Callout type="must" title="Care for the child with the family">
  The parent is the child’s constant caregiver. **Keep the parent informed, allow feeding and comfort, and use play and simple words to reduce fear.**
</Callout>
