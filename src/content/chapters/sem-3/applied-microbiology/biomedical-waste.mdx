## In simple words

Biomedical waste is waste generated during diagnosis, treatment or immunisation of humans. It can be infectious, hazardous or sharp. Correct segregation at the point of generation protects staff, patients, waste handlers and the community.

India follows colour-coded segregation under the Biomedical Waste Management Rules.

## Colour-coded bins

| Colour | Waste type | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow | Human/animal waste, soiled waste | Bandages, dressings, cotton, body fluids |
| Red | Contaminated recyclable plastic | Tubing, bottles, syringes (without needle) |
| White/blue translucent | Sharps | Needles, syringes with needle, blades, broken glass |
| Black | General non-infectious waste | Packaging, food waste, paper |

## Sharps safety

- Never recap, bend or break needles.
- Drop sharps directly into a puncture-proof container.
- Do not overfill; seal when three-quarters full.
- Report needlestick injuries immediately.

## Handling rules

- Segregate at the point of generation — never sort later.
- Wear gloves and apron when handling waste.
- Tie bags securely; label with date and ward.
- Store in a secure area; transport to treatment facility on schedule.

<Callout type="must" title="Segregate at the source">
  **Segregate biomedical waste at the point of generation.** Never sort mixed waste later — it puts staff at risk.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Sharps disposal** — drop needles/syringes with needle directly into the white/blue puncture-proof container; never recap.
- **Soiled dressings** — yellow bag.
- **Contaminated plastic** — red bag.
- **General waste** — black bag (paper, packaging, food).
- **Needlestick injury** — wash, report immediately, follow post-exposure protocol.

## 60-second revision

- Biomedical waste = from diagnosis, treatment, immunisation.
- Yellow = soiled/infectious; Red = contaminated plastic; White/Blue = sharps; Black = general.
- Segregate at the source; never sort later.
- Sharps into puncture-proof container; never recap.
- Report needlestick injuries immediately.
