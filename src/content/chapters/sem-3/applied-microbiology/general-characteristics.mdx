## In simple words

Bacteria are single-celled microbes with a simple structure. Unlike human cells, they have no nucleus — their DNA floats freely in the cell. Bacteria come in different shapes and react differently to Gram stain, which helps identify them.

Viruses are even smaller — just genetic material wrapped in protein. They cannot multiply on their own; they need a living cell.

## Bacterial cell structure

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Cell wall | Gives shape and protection |
| Cell membrane | Controls what enters and leaves |
| Cytoplasm | Contains enzymes and DNA |
| DNA | Genetic material (no nucleus) |
| Flagella | Helps some bacteria move |
| Pili | Helps bacteria attach to surfaces |
| Capsule | Protects from immune system |

## Gram stain

The Gram stain divides bacteria into two groups:

- **Gram-positive** — stain purple (thick cell wall). Examples: Staphylococcus, Streptococcus.
- **Gram-negative** — stain pink (thin cell wall, outer membrane). Examples: E. coli, Pseudomonas.

Gram stain helps choose the right antibiotic.

## Bacterial growth and spores

Bacteria multiply by binary fission (one cell splits into two). Given warmth, moisture, food and time, one bacterium can become millions in hours.

Some bacteria form **spores** — tough, dormant forms that survive heat, drying and disinfectants. Examples: Clostridium tetani (tetanus), Clostridium difficile, Bacillus anthracis.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Hand hygiene** — removes bacteria from hands before they spread.
- **Wound care** — clean wounds reduce the chance of bacterial growth.
- **Specimen collection** — collect swabs, urine or sputum correctly so the lab can identify the organism.
- **Isolation** — some Gram-negative bacteria (e.g., Pseudomonas) are hard to treat and need careful isolation.
- **Sterilisation** — spores survive normal cleaning; sterilisation (autoclaving) kills spores.

## 60-second revision

- Bacteria: single-celled, no nucleus.
- Shapes: coccus, bacillus, spirillum.
- Gram-positive = purple; Gram-negative = pink.
- Binary fission = one cell splits into two.
- Spores survive heat and disinfectants.
