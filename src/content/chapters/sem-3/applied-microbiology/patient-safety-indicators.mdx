## In simple words

Patient safety indicators are measures that show how safely care is delivered. They track events like falls, infections, medication errors and pressure ulcers. Hospitals use them to find problems and improve care.

Nurses collect data and act daily to keep indicator rates low.

## Common patient safety indicators

| Indicator | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Fall rate | Patients who fall per 1,000 patient days |
| HAI rate | Hospital-acquired infections (CAUTI, CLABSI, VAP, SSI) |
| Medication error rate | Errors in prescribing, dispensing, administering |
| Pressure ulcer rate | New pressure ulcers during stay |
| Readmission rate | Patients readmitted soon after discharge |

## How nurses improve indicators

- Follow standard precautions and hand hygiene to reduce HAIs.
- Assess fall risk and assist mobility to reduce falls.
- Follow the five rights of medication to reduce errors.
- Turn patients every 2 hours and assess skin to reduce pressure ulcers.
- Document accurately so data reflects real care.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Hand hygiene and asepsis** — reduce HAI rates.
- **Fall risk assessment** — use a fall scale; assist mobility; keep call bell within reach.
- **Medication safety** — five rights; double-check high-risk drugs.
- **Pressure ulcer prevention** — 2-hourly turning, skin assessment, pressure-relieving surfaces.
- **Accurate documentation** — records the true picture for indicator reporting.
