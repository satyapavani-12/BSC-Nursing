## In simple words

Antiseptics and disinfectants are both antimicrobial agents, but they are used differently: antiseptics on living tissue (skin), disinfectants on non-living surfaces (instruments, floors).

Nurses use antiseptics for skin preparation and hand hygiene, and disinfectants for cleaning equipment and the environment.

## Differences

| Feature | Antiseptic | Disinfectant |
|---|---|---|
| Applied to | Living tissue (skin, wounds) | Non-living surfaces (instruments, floors) |
| Strength | Weaker (safe on tissue) | Stronger (too harsh for skin) |
| Examples | Dettol, chlorhexidine, iodine | Bleach, formaldehyde, phenolic compounds |

## Common agents

| Agent | Type | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| Chlorhexidine | Antiseptic | Skin prep, hand hygiene, mouthwash |
| Povidone-iodine | Antiseptic | Skin prep, wound cleaning |
| Ethyl/isopropyl alcohol | Antiseptic/disinfectant | Skin prep, surface disinfection |
| Hydrogen peroxide | Antiseptic/disinfectant | Wound cleaning, surface disinfection |
| Bleach (sodium hypochlorite) | Disinfectant | Floors, surfaces, blood spills |
| Formaldehyde | Disinfectant | Fumigation, instrument sterilisation |

## Safe use

- Follow manufacturer dilution instructions.
- Allow correct contact time (don't wipe off immediately).
- Never mix chemicals (e.g., bleach + ammonia = toxic gas).
- Wear gloves; ensure ventilation.
- Store safely, away from children and heat.

<Callout type="must" title="Antiseptic ≠ disinfectant">
  **Antiseptics go on living skin; disinfectants go on non-living surfaces.** Never put strong disinfectants (e.g., bleach) on skin.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Skin preparation** — apply antiseptic (chlorhexidine or iodine) before injections, IV insertion, surgery.
- **Hand hygiene** — use antiseptic hand rub or antiseptic soap.
- **Wound cleaning** — use mild antiseptic (e.g., diluted hydrogen peroxide or povidone-iodine) as ordered.
- **Environment cleaning** — disinfect beds, floors, equipment between patients.
- **Blood spills** — disinfect with bleach solution as per policy.

## 60-second revision

- Antiseptic = on skin (weaker); Disinfectant = on surfaces (stronger).
- Antiseptics: chlorhexidine, iodine, alcohol.
- Disinfectants: bleach, formaldehyde.
- Follow dilution and contact time.
- Never mix chemicals; wear gloves.
