## In simple words

Pharmacology is the study of drugs and their effects on the body. A drug is any substance (other than food) used to prevent, diagnose, treat or cure disease.

Pharmacology has two main parts: pharmacokinetics (what the body does to the drug) and pharmacodynamics (what the drug does to the body).

## Branches of pharmacology

| Branch | What it studies |
|---|---|
| Pharmacokinetics | Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion (ADME) |
| Pharmacodynamics | Drug effects, mechanism of action, dose-response |
| Pharmacotherapeutics | Use of drugs to treat disease |
| Toxicology | Harmful effects of drugs and poisons |
| Pharmacy | Preparation and dispensing of drugs |

## Pharmacokinetics (ADME)

- **Absorption** — drug enters the bloodstream from the site of administration.
- **Distribution** — drug is carried by blood to tissues.
- **Metabolism** — drug is broken down (mainly in the liver).
- **Excretion** — drug and metabolites leave the body (mainly via kidneys).

## Routes of administration

| Route | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Oral | Tablets, capsules, syrups | Safest, most common, slowest onset |
| Intravenous (IV) | Injection, infusion | Fastest, 100% bioavailability |
| Intramuscular (IM) | Into muscle | Vaccines, some antibiotics |
| Subcutaneous | Under skin | Insulin, heparin |
| Topical | Creams, ointments | Local effect |
| Sublingual | Under tongue | Nitroglycerine — fast absorption |

<Callout type="must" title="Pharmacokinetics vs pharmacodynamics">
  **Pharmacokinetics = what the body does to the drug (ADME). Pharmacodynamics = what the drug does to the body (effect).**
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Route selection** — IV for emergencies (fast); oral for routine; IM/SC for vaccines and some drugs.
- **Timing** — give drugs at the right time to maintain blood levels.
- **Absorption factors** — food, other drugs, pH can change absorption; follow instructions (e.g., "take on empty stomach").
- **Metabolism/excretion** — liver and kidney disease change drug levels; adjust dose as ordered.
- **Patient teaching** — explain how and when to take each drug.

## 60-second revision

- Pharmacology = drugs and their effects.
- Pharmacokinetics (ADME) = body → drug.
- Pharmacodynamics = drug → body.
- Routes: oral, IV, IM, SC, topical, sublingual.
- IV fastest; oral safest; liver metabolises; kidneys excrete.
