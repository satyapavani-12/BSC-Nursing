## In simple words

Cardiovascular disorders affect the heart and blood vessels — hypertension, angina, myocardial infarction (heart attack), heart failure and arrhythmias. Nurses monitor vital signs, give medications, and teach lifestyle changes.

## Common cardiovascular disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Hypertension | Persistently high BP | Often silent; headache, dizziness |
| Angina | Coronary artery narrowing | Chest pain on exertion, relieved by rest/GTN |
| Myocardial infarction (MI) | Coronary artery blockage; heart muscle death | Severe chest pain, sweating, nausea, breathlessness |
| Heart failure | Heart can't pump effectively | Breathlessness, oedema, fatigue, crackles |
| Arrhythmias | Irregular rhythm | Palpitations, dizziness, syncope |

## Nursing care

- **Hypertension** — monitor BP, give antihypertensives as ordered, teach salt restriction, weight control, medication compliance.
- **Angina** — GTN sublingual for chest pain; sit patient down; rest; report unrelieved pain.
- **MI** — emergency; oxygen, ECG, cardiac enzymes, thrombolysis/PCI as ordered; monitor for arrhythmias and complications.
- **Heart failure** — daily weight, fluid/salt restriction, diuretics as ordered, position upright, monitor oedema and crackles.
- **Arrhythmias** — monitor ECG, check pulse, prepare for cardioversion/defibrillation if unstable.

## Nursing care points

- **Chest pain** — treat as MI until proven otherwise; sit patient down, oxygen, GTN as ordered, ECG, inform doctor immediately.
- **Monitoring** — vital signs, ECG, oxygen saturation, urine output, daily weight.
- **Medications** — antihypertensives, antianginals, anticoagulants, diuretics, antiarrhythmics as ordered.
- **Lifestyle teaching** — smoking cessation, low-salt diet, regular exercise, weight control, stress management, medication compliance.

<Callout type="must" title="Chest pain = treat as MI">
  **Treat all chest pain as a possible MI until proven otherwise:** sit patient down, give oxygen, GTN as ordered, obtain ECG, inform doctor immediately.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Chest pain** — sit patient down, loosen tight clothing, oxygen, GTN sublingual as ordered, 12-lead ECG, continuous monitoring.
- **BP monitoring** — regular checks; report high or low readings; give antihypertensives on time.
- **Daily weight** — heart failure patients; sudden weight gain = fluid retention.
- **Oedema assessment** — press over shin/sacrum; record pitting.
- **Patient teaching** — medication compliance, low-salt diet, smoking cessation, regular follow-up, recognise warning signs.

## 60-second revision

- Cardiovascular disorders: hypertension, angina, MI, heart failure, arrhythmias.
- Chest pain = treat as MI until proven otherwise.
- GTN sublingual for angina; repeat up to 3 doses.
- Heart failure: daily weight, fluid/salt restriction, upright position.
- Monitor BP, pulse, SpO2, urine output, oedema, crackles.
- Lifestyle: low-salt, stop smoking, exercise, medication compliance.
