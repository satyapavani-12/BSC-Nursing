## In simple words

Endocrine disorders affect hormone-producing glands — diabetes mellitus (insulin problem), thyroid disorders (too little or too much thyroid hormone), and adrenal disorders (Cushing's and Addison's). Nurses monitor blood glucose, give medications and teach self-care.

## Diabetes mellitus

| Type | Cause/Features |
|---|---|
| Type 1 | Autoimmune destruction of beta cells; little/no insulin; needs insulin |
| Type 2 | Insulin resistance + relative deficiency; common in adults; lifestyle-related |
| Gestational | Diabetes first diagnosed in pregnancy |

**Classic symptoms (3Ps):** polyuria (excess urination), polydipsia (excess thirst), polyphagia (excess hunger), weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision.

**Hypoglycaemia** (low blood glucose): sweating, trembling, hunger, confusion, weakness, sweating — treat with fast-acting sugar.
**Hyperglycaemia** (high blood glucose): thirst, polyuria, blurred vision, fatigue — can lead to DKA (ketones, fruity breath, vomiting, abdominal pain).

## Thyroid disorders

- **Hypothyroidism** (underactive): tiredness, weight gain, cold intolerance, constipation, dry skin, slow pulse — treated with thyroxine.
- **Hyperthyroidism** (overactive): weight loss, heat intolerance, tremor, fast pulse, anxiety, exophthalmos (Graves') — treated with antithyroid drugs, radioiodine, surgery.

## Adrenal disorders

- **Cushing's syndrome** (excess cortisol): moon face, buffalo hump, central obesity, hypertension, diabetes, thin skin, easy bruising.
- **Addison's disease** (adrenal insufficiency): fatigue, weight loss, hyperpigmentation, low BP, salt craving — Addisonian crisis = emergency (vomiting, dehydration, shock).

## Nursing care

- **Diabetes** — blood glucose monitoring, insulin/oral hypoglycaemics as ordered, diet (regular meals, carbohydrate consistency), foot care, exercise, hypoglycaemia treatment.
- **Thyroid** — give thyroxine on empty stomach (morning); monitor for thyroid storm (hyperthyroidism emergency: fever, tachycardia, agitation).
- **Adrenal** — monitor BP (Addison's: hypotension), glucose (Cushing's: hyperglycaemia); never stop steroids suddenly (adrenal crisis).

<Callout type="must" title="Hypoglycaemia treatment">
  **Treat hypoglycaemia immediately with fast-acting sugar** (glucose tablets, juice, sugar water) and recheck blood glucose in 15 minutes; repeat if still low.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Blood glucose monitoring** — before meals and at bedtime; record and report abnormalities.
- **Insulin administration** — check dose and type; rotate sites; check blood glucose before giving.
- **Hypoglycaemia** — treat with fast-acting sugar; recheck in 15 minutes; educate patient/family.
- **Foot care** — daily inspection, moisturise (not between toes), proper footwear, report cuts/infections.
- **Steroid therapy** — never stop suddenly (adrenal crisis); monitor blood glucose, blood pressure, weight, mood.
