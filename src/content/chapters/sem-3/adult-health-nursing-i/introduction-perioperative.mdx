## In simple words

Perioperative nursing care covers the whole surgical journey — before, during and after surgery. The goal is to prepare the patient, keep them safe during surgery and support recovery.

The three phases are preoperative (before surgery), intraoperative (during surgery) and postoperative (after surgery).

## Preoperative care

- **Assessment** — history, physical examination, allergies, medications, smoking/alcohol, previous anaesthesia reactions.
- **Preparation** — consent, fasting (nil by mouth usually 6–8 hours), skin preparation, bowel preparation if ordered, removing jewellery/dentures.
- **Patient teaching** — explain what to expect, deep breathing and coughing, leg exercises, pain control (PCA), early mobilisation.
- **Psychological support** — reduce anxiety, answer questions, involve family.

## Intraoperative care

- **Roles** — scrub nurse (sterile field, instruments), circulating nurse (outside sterile field, supplies, documentation), anaesthetist (anaesthesia and monitoring).
- **Safety** — patient identification, surgical site marking, time-out (correct patient, site, procedure), sterile technique, counts (swabs, instruments, needles).
- **Positioning** — protect pressure points, maintain airway, prevent nerve injury.

## Postoperative care

- **Recovery room (PACU)** — monitor airway, breathing, circulation, vital signs, consciousness, pain, nausea, bleeding, urine output.
- **Pain management** — analgesics as ordered, PCA, non-drug methods (positioning, distraction).
- **Complications** — haemorrhage, shock, infection, DVT, pulmonary embolism, atelectasis, pneumonia.
- **Early mobilisation** — leg exercises, deep breathing, early walking to prevent complications.

<Callout type="must" title="Time-out before surgery">
  **Time-out: pause before the first incision to confirm correct patient, correct site and correct procedure.** This prevents wrong-site surgery.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Preoperative** — verify consent, ensure fasting, complete checklist, relieve anxiety.
- **Intraoperative** — maintain sterile technique, assist with counts, protect patient position and dignity.
- **Postoperative** — monitor vital signs, manage pain, encourage deep breathing and leg exercises, assist early walking.
- **Discharge teaching** — wound care, medication, activity restrictions, follow-up, warning signs to report.

## 60-second revision

- Perioperative = before, during, after surgery.
- Preoperative: assess, consent, fast, prepare, teach.
- Intraoperative: sterile technique, counts, time-out.
- Time-out: correct patient, site, procedure.
- Postoperative: monitor ABCs, manage pain, mobilise early.
- Surgical counts: swabs, instruments, needles.
