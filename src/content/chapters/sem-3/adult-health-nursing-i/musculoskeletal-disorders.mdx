## In simple words

Musculoskeletal disorders affect bones, joints, muscles and connective tissue — fractures, arthritis, osteoporosis, osteomyelitis and amputations. Nurses assess, immobilise, give medications, assist mobilisation and teach self-care.

## Common musculoskeletal disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Fracture | Bone break (trauma, osteoporosis) | Pain, deformity, swelling, immobility, crepitus |
| Osteoarthritis | Joint cartilage degeneration | Joint pain, stiffness, reduced movement |
| Rheumatoid arthritis | Autoimmune joint inflammation | Symmetrical joint pain, swelling, stiffness (morning) |
| Osteoporosis | Bone density loss | Fractures (hip, spine, wrist), height loss |
| Osteomyelitis | Bone infection | Fever, bone pain, swelling, redness |
| Amputation | Limb removal (trauma, vascular disease) | Stump care, phantom pain, rehabilitation |

## Fracture care

- **Immobilise** — splint the joint above and below the fracture; do not move unnecessarily.
- **Check distal circulation** — pulse, colour, warmth, movement, sensation (5 Ps: pain, pallor, pulselessness, paraesthesia, paralysis).
- **Pain control** — analgesics as ordered.
- **After casting** — check for tightness (swelling, pain, numbness, cool fingers/toes); elevate; exercise uninjured joints.

## Arthritis and osteoporosis

- **Osteoarthritis** — weight control, exercise, analgesics, joint protection; joint replacement if severe.
- **Rheumatoid arthritis** — DMARDs, rest during flares, exercise during remission, joint protection.
- **Osteoporosis** — calcium, vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise, fall prevention; bisphosphonates as ordered.

## Amputation care

- **Stump care** — keep clean and dry; inspect for redness/breakdown; position to prevent contracture (do not let limb hang).
- **Phantom pain** — common; reassure; medications as ordered.
- **Rehabilitation** — early mobilisation, prosthesis fitting, independence training.

<Callout type="must" title="Check circulation after fracture/cast">
  **Check the 5 Ps after any fracture or cast:** Pain, Pallor, Pulselessness, Paraesthesia (tingling), Paralysis. Report abnormalities immediately — compartment syndrome risk.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Fracture** — immobilise, check distal circulation, pain control, elevate, monitor for compartment syndrome.
- **Cast care** — keep dry (unless fibreglass), check for tightness/swelling, do not insert objects under cast, exercise uninjured joints.
- **Arthritis** — assist with mobility, joint protection, heat/cold as ordered, medication compliance.
- **Osteoporosis** — fall prevention, calcium/vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise as tolerated.
- **Amputation** — stump care, contracture prevention, phantom pain management, rehabilitation support.

## 60-second revision

- Musculoskeletal disorders: fracture, arthritis, osteoporosis, osteomyelitis, amputation.
- Fracture: immobilise; check 5 Ps; pain control.
- Osteoarthritis = cartilage degeneration; rheumatoid arthritis = autoimmune inflammation.
- Osteoporosis = bone density loss; calcium, vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise.
- Amputation: stump care, phantom pain, rehabilitation.
- 5 Ps: pain, pallor, pulselessness, paraesthesia, paralysis.
