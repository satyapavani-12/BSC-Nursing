## In simple words

Skin disorders affect the skin, hair and nails — dermatitis, eczema, psoriasis, pressure ulcers, skin infections and burns. Nurses assess skin, prevent pressure ulcers, manage wounds and teach skin care.

## Common skin disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| Dermatitis/eczema | Allergy, irritation, genetics | Itching, redness, dryness, rash |
| Psoriasis | Autoimmune; rapid skin cell turnover | Thick silvery scales, plaques |
| Acne | Blocked oil glands, bacteria | Pimples, blackheads, inflammation |
| Fungal infection | Fungi (tinea, candida) | Itching, redness, scaling, ring shape |
| Pressure ulcer | Pressure, shear, friction | Redness (non-blanching), blister, ulcer |
| Burns | Heat, chemicals, electricity, radiation | Redness, blisters, charring, pain |

## Pressure ulcer stages

| Stage | Appearance |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Non-blanchable redness of intact skin |
| Stage 2 | Partial-thickness skin loss (shallow open ulcer, blister) |
| Stage 3 | Full-thickness skin loss (deep crater) |
| Stage 4 | Full-thickness tissue loss (bone, tendon, muscle exposed) |

## Pressure ulcer prevention

- Turn/reposition every 2 hours.
- Use pressure-relieving mattress and cushions.
- Keep skin clean and dry; manage incontinence promptly.
- Assess skin at least daily (pressure points: sacrum, heels, elbows, hips).
- Avoid massaging red areas; ensure adequate nutrition and hydration.

## Burns care

- **First-degree** (superficial): redness, pain — cool water, analgesics.
- **Second-degree** (partial-thickness): blisters, pain — cool, cover, do not break blisters.
- **Third-degree** (full-thickness): white/charred, painless (nerves destroyed) — emergency; cover, IV fluids, referral.
- **Rule of nines** — estimates % body surface area burned (head 9%, each arm 9%, front trunk 18%, back 18%, each leg 18%, perineum 1%).

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Skin assessment** — inspect skin at least daily, especially pressure points (sacrum, heels, elbows, hips, back of head).
- **Pressure ulcer care** — clean wound, appropriate dressings, relieve pressure, nutrition, pain control.
- **Burn care** — cool running water for minor burns; cover; do not break blisters; refer serious burns.
- **Skin hygiene** — keep skin clean and dry; moisturise dry skin; avoid harsh soaps.
- **Patient teaching** — skin inspection, pressure relief, sun protection, medication adherence (topical steroids).

## 60-second revision

- Skin disorders: dermatitis, eczema, psoriasis, pressure ulcers, burns.
- Pressure ulcer stages 1–4; pressure points: sacrum, heels, elbows, hips, head.
- Turn every 2 hours; keep skin clean and dry; no massage over red areas.
- Burns: cool water; do not break blisters; rule of nines for % BSA.
- Topical steroids: not on face/long term without review.
