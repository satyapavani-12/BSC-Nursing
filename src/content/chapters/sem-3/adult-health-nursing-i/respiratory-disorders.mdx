## In simple words

Respiratory disorders affect the airways and lungs — making breathing difficult. Nurses assess breathing, give oxygen, teach breathing exercises and monitor for deterioration.

Common disorders: COPD, asthma, pneumonia, tuberculosis.

## Common respiratory disorders

| Disorder | Cause/Features | Key signs |
|---|---|---|
| COPD | Chronic bronchitis + emphysema; smoking | Breathlessness, chronic cough, wheeze |
| Asthma | Airway inflammation, reversible narrowing | Wheeze, cough, chest tightness, triggered by allergens |
| Pneumonia | Lung infection (bacterial/viral) | Fever, cough, breathlessness, chest pain, consolidation |
| Tuberculosis (TB) | Mycobacterium tuberculosis | Chronic cough >2 weeks, weight loss, night sweats, haemoptysis |

## Nursing care

- **Position upright** — improves lung expansion.
- **Oxygen therapy** — as prescribed; monitor SpO2 (target 88–92% in COPD, 94–98% otherwise).
- **Breathing exercises** — deep breathing, pursed-lip breathing, incentive spirometer.
- **Coughing and clearance** — encourage effective coughing; suction if needed.
- **Hydration** — encourages thin secretions (unless fluid-restricted).

## Tuberculosis care

- **Airborne precautions** — mask, isolation.
- **Sputum collection** — early morning, before antibiotics.
- **DOTS** — observe treatment; 6–9 months; adherence critical.
- **Patient teaching** — cover mouth when coughing, dispose of sputum safely, complete treatment.

<Callout type="must" title="Oxygen in COPD">
  **In COPD, give controlled oxygen — target SpO2 88–92% (not 100%).** High-flow oxygen can remove the hypoxic drive to breathe in some COPD patients.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Assess breathing** — rate, depth, pattern, use of accessory muscles, colour, SpO2.
- **Position upright** — Fowler's position improves breathing.
- **Oxygen** — as prescribed; never adjust flow without orders; humidify.
- **Breathing exercises** — teach deep breathing, pursed-lip breathing, incentive spirometer use.
- **TB infection control** — airborne precautions, mask, sputum precautions, DOTS adherence.

- TB: airborne precautions, DOTS (6–9 months).
