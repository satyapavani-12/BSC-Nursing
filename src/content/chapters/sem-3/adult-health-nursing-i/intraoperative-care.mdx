## In simple words

Intraoperative care is care during surgery. The circulating nurse (outside the sterile field) and scrub nurse (within the sterile field) work together to keep the patient safe and the operation running smoothly.

Key principles: sterile technique, correct patient positioning, accurate counts, and constant monitoring.

## Roles in the operating theatre

- **Scrub nurse** — scrubs in, wears sterile gown and gloves, passes instruments and supplies to the surgeon, maintains the sterile field.
- **Circulating nurse** — does not scrub in; manages supplies, documents, obtains specimens, communicates with outside, supports the team.
- **Anaesthetist** — administers and monitors anaesthesia, monitors vital signs throughout.
- **Surgeon** — performs the operation.

## Sterile technique

- Sterile team members touch only sterile items.
- Non-sterile team members stay away from the sterile field.
- Sterile packages are opened carefully; contents are not touched by non-sterile hands.
- If sterility is broken (e.g., a sterile item falls), discard and replace.

## Patient positioning

- Protect pressure points (heels, elbows, sacrum, occiput).
- Maintain neutral alignment.
- Protect nerves (avoid pressure on ulnar, peroneal nerves).
- Ensure airway is visible and unobstructed.
- Prevent pressure injury.

## Surgical counts

- Count sponges (swabs), sharps (needles, blades) and instruments before the procedure, before closure and at the end.
- Any discrepancy must be reported and resolved before closing.

## Anaesthesia basics

- **General** — patient unconscious (inhaled or IV agents).
- **Regional** — epidural, spinal, nerve block (region numb, patient awake or sedated).
- **Local** — small area numb (patient awake).
- **Monitored anaesthesia care** — sedation with monitoring.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Scrub role** — pass instruments efficiently, maintain sterility, assist with counts.
- **Circulating role** — document, obtain supplies and specimens, support the team, communicate with family.
- **Positioning** — assess and protect pressure points before and during positioning.
- **Counts** — participate in sponge, sharp and instrument counts; speak up if a count is wrong.
- **Monitoring** — observe the patient's condition and report changes to the anaesthetist.

## 60-second revision

- Intraoperative = care during surgery.
- Roles: scrub (sterile), circulating (outside), anaesthetist, surgeon.
- Sterile technique: sterile touches sterile; broken = discard.
- Positioning: protect pressure points, alignment, nerves, airway.
- Counts: swabs, sharps, instruments — before, before closure, at end.
- Anaesthesia: general, regional, local, MAC.
