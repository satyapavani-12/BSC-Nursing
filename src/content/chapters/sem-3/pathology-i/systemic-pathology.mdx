## In simple words

Systemic pathology studies diseases of specific body systems. Nurses care for patients with heart, lung, liver and kidney disease every day — understanding the pathology helps with assessment, care and patient teaching.

## Heart and vessels

- **Atherosclerosis** — fatty plaques in artery walls → narrowing → heart attack, stroke.
- **Myocardial infarction (MI)** — heart muscle death due to blocked coronary artery.
- **Hypertension** — persistently high blood pressure → damages heart, kidneys, vessels.

## Lungs

- **Pneumonia** — lung infection → consolidation (fluid/pus in alveoli) → fever, cough, difficulty breathing.
- **COPD** — chronic bronchitis + emphysema → airflow limitation → breathlessness.
- **Tuberculosis** — bacterial lung infection → cavitation → chronic cough, weight loss.

## Liver

- **Hepatitis** — liver inflammation → jaundice (yellow skin/eyes) → viral (A, B, C), alcohol, drugs.
- **Cirrhosis** — liver scarring → liver failure → jaundice, ascites, bleeding.

## Kidneys

- **Glomerulonephritis** — inflammation of glomeruli → blood/protein in urine → kidney failure.
- **Nephritis** — kidney inflammation → reduced filtration → fluid retention, hypertension.

<Callout type="must" title="Jaundice = liver problem">
  **Jaundice (yellow skin and eyes) signals liver or bile-duct disease.** Report it — it indicates bilirubin buildup from hepatitis, cirrhosis or obstruction.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Cardiac patients** — monitor chest pain, BP, pulse; give medications as ordered; position comfortably.
- **Respiratory patients** — monitor oxygen saturation, breathing pattern, sputum; position upright; encourage deep breathing and coughing.
- **Liver patients** — monitor for jaundice, bleeding, confusion (hepatic encephalopathy); avoid alcohol.
- **Kidney patients** — monitor urine output, fluid balance, oedema; restrict fluids/salt as ordered.
- **Patient teaching** — explain the disease, medications, diet and lifestyle changes (smoking cessation, salt restriction, medication compliance).

## 60-second revision

- Systemic pathology = diseases of specific body systems.
- Heart: atherosclerosis, MI, hypertension.
- Lungs: pneumonia, COPD, TB.
- Liver: hepatitis, cirrhosis (jaundice, ascites).
- Kidneys: glomerulonephritis, nephritis.
- Monitor: BP, pulse, SpO2, urine output, jaundice, bleeding.
