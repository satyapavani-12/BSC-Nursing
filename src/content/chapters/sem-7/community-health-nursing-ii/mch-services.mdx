## In simple words

Maternal and child health services protect the two most vulnerable groups. The aims are: safe pregnancy, safe birth, healthy newborn, and healthy child growth.

## Components of MCH services

| Component | What is delivered |
|---|---|
| Antenatal care | Regular visits, screening, nutrition, TT, iron and folic acid, birth planning |
| Intrapartum care | Trained birth attendant, clean delivery, referral for complications |
| Postnatal care | Check mother and baby, breastfeeding, family planning counselling |
| Newborn care | Warmth, cord and eye care, exclusive breastfeeding, danger signs |
| Child health | Growth monitoring, immunisation, nutrition, ORS, IMNCI |
| Adolescent health | Anaemia screening, nutrition, counselling |

## Reproductive and Child Health programme

- ANC with **four to eight visits**: registration, weight, BP, Hb, blood group, HIV and sugar
- **Tetanus toxoid:** two doses in the first pregnancy
- **Iron and folic acid** tablets and calcium
- **Birth planning:** place of delivery, transport, funds, blood
- **JSY:** cash support for institutional delivery
- **High-risk pregnancy:** age below 18 or above 35, height under 145 cm, anaemia, diabetes, hypertension, malpresentation, previous caesarean
- **Postnatal visits:** 6 visits — day 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 28 and 42

## Child health services

- **Growth monitoring:** weigh monthly, plot on the growth chart
- **Immunisation** and **Mission Indradhanush** for missed children
- **Nutrition:** ICDS supplementary feeding, vitamin A, IFA
- **Diarrhoea and pneumonia** management with ORS, zinc and amoxycillin
- **Early detection** of disability, congenital anomaly and development delay — RBSK
- **Protection** of girls from early marriage and teenage pregnancy

## Indicators

- **Maternal mortality ratio (MMR)** — deaths per 10 000 live births
- **Infant mortality rate (IMR)** — deaths under 1 year per 1000 live births
- **Institutional delivery rate** and antenatal registration
- **Full immunisation coverage**
- **Low birth weight** — under 2.5 kg
- **Adolescent anaemia** rate

<Callout type="must" title="High-risk mothers need more care">
  Mothers under 18 or over 35 years, under 145 cm, anaemic, with hypertension, diabetes, malpresentation or a previous scar need **specialist antenatal care and hospital delivery**. A missed high-risk pregnancy causes preventable deaths.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- ANC four to eight visits with TT, IFA and birth planning.
- Safe institutional birth with skilled attendance and JSY support.
- Postnatal checks on days 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 28 and 42.
- Child health: growth monitoring, immunisation, ORS, IMNCI, RBSK.
- Indicators: MMR, IMR, institutional delivery, full immunisation.
