## In simple words

Occupational health protects the health of workers and promotes their productivity. It looks at hazards, prevention, early detection, treatment, rehabilitation and compensation.

## Objectives of occupational health

- Prevent work-related injury and disease
- Detect and treat occupational disease early
- Adjust workers to their work where possible
- Improve the work environment
- Provide first aid, emergency plans and rehabilitation
- Advocate for laws, compensation and welfare services

## Workplace hazards

| Hazard | Examples | Diseases |
|---|---|---|
| Dust | Silica, coal, cotton | Silicosis, coal worker’s pneumoconiosis, byssinosis |
| Chemicals | Lead, mercury, solvents, asbestos | Anaemia, dermatitis, asbestosis |
| Noise | Machinery, blasting | Noise-induced hearing loss |
| Heat | Furnaces, mining | Heat stress |
| Radiation | Nuclear, X-ray | Cancer, genetic damage |
| Physical | Falls, machinery | Injuries |

## Workers' health services

- **Pre-placement examination** — protects workers from unsuitable jobs
- **Periodic medical examinations** — early detection
- **Emergency and first aid services** — first aid boxes, trained staff
- **Health education** — nutrition, hygiene, HIV and tobacco
- **Welfare measures** — drinking water, toilets, canteen, housing, rest, crèche
- **Mental health** — stress, work-life balance, counselling

## Compensation and legislation

- **Employee’s State Insurance (ESI) Act, 1948** — medical care and cash benefit for workers
- **Factories Act, 1948** — working conditions, safety, welfare
- **Workmen’s / Employees' Compensation Act** — payment for injury or death
- **Mines Act** — working conditions in mines
- **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020** — consolidated safety rules

## 60-second revision

- Occupational health: prevent, protect, detect, treat, rehabilitate.
- Hazards: dust, chemicals, noise, heat, radiation.
- Diseases: silicosis, pneumoconiosis, asbestosis, byssinosis.
- Worker services: pre-placement checks, periodic exams, first aid, welfare.
- Laws: ESI 1948, Factories Act 1948, Employees' Compensation Act.
