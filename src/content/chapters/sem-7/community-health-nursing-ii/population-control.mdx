## In simple words

India’s population grows, but the rate is slowing. Family welfare is the programme that helps couples choose their family size, spacing and methods — a rights-based service, never coercion.

## Population trends in India

- Population over **1.4 billion** (second largest after China)
- Growth rate declining but still positive
- **Density** of population: about 480 persons per square kilometre
- Sex ratio about **1088 males per 1000 females**
- **Literacy rate** about 74 per cent
- **Young population** — a large proportion is under 25

## Demographic transition

1. High birth and high death rates — stable but low growth
2. Falling death rate, birth rate still high — rapid growth
3. Falling birth rate — growth slows
4. Low birth and low death rates — stable (developed phase)

## National Population Policy 2000

- **Aim:** the second-phase goal is to take the total fertility rate to replacement level by 2010, and a stable population by 2045
- Voluntary, couple-centred planning with free choice of methods
- Reduces infant and maternal mortality by improving MCH
- Addresses adolescent health, gender education, sex ratio and postponed marriage
- Population stabilisation only through voluntary participation

## Family welfare services in the community

- Contraceptive services: condoms, OCP, IUD, injectable, sterilisation
- Emergency contraception, MTP counselling
- Prevention and treatment of infertility and RTI
- Counselling on spacing and small family size
- Adolescent health services and counselling
- Postpartum family planning

<Callout type="must" title="Voluntary, informed and respectful">
  Family planning is a **right of the couple**. No target, pressure, incentive conditioning or coercion is acceptable. Nurses must inform, counsel and support the couple’s chosen method.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Population: large, growing but slowing; young and urbanising.
- Demographic transition moves to low birth and death rates.
- NPP 2000: voluntary, couple-centred; stable population by 2045.
- Services: contraception, RTI, infertility, MTP, adolescent care.
- Nurse’s role: inform, counsel, refer, review.
