## In simple words

An information system is the nervous system of health care: it records who is ill, what care was given and what resources are used.

## Registers used in community health

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Eligible couple register | Family planning eligible couples |
| Antenatal register | Pregnant women registered and visits |
| Delivery register | Conducted deliveries |
| Immunisation register | Children vaccinated, due and missed |
| Under-five register | Births, weight, illness |
| Mortality register | Births and deaths |
| Family folder | Complete family health record |

## Reporting chain

- **Daily:** outpatient register, treatment and drug records
- **Weekly:** births, deaths, immunisation, communicable disease reports
- **Monthly:** review and analysis, achievements and gaps
- **Quarterly and yearly:** performance and programme review

## Digital health systems

- **Electronic health records and hospital information system** for fast access
- **ABDM — Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission** creates a national health ID and links records
- **Telemedicine** consultations through video call
- **Reproductive and child health portals** for tracking
- **eSanjeevani** is a telemedicine platform of the Government of India

<Callout type="must" title="Record keeping is a nursing duty">
  Incomplete or delayed records cause missed vaccination, missed referral and missed births. **Record immediately, accurately and truthfully, and check the register against the actual visit.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- HMIS records, reports and analyses health data.
- Key registers: antenatal, delivery, immunisation, under-five, mortality.
- Reports are daily, weekly, monthly and annually.
- Information guides planning, monitoring and improving.
- Digital health: electronic records, ABDM, eSanjeevani telemedicine.
