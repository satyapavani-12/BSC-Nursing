## In simple words

School health and disaster preparedness are community nursing activities. Both require planning, teaching and rapid response.

## School health programme

- **Components:** annual health check-up, treatment of minor ailments, referral for major problems
- **School lunch** and nutrition support
- **Deworming** and **iron and folic acid** supplementation
- **Immunisation** of school children
- **Health education:** hygiene, sanitation, oral health, sex education, substance and tobacco avoidance
- **Environment:** safe drinking water, toilets, hygiene and safe school infrastructure
- **Vision, hearing and dental screening** and referrals
- **Counselling** for adolescence, learning problems and stress

## Disaster management

| Phase | Actions |
|---|---|
| Preparedness | Risk map, stockpile, drills, community volunteers |
| Early warning | Identify and monitor signals, evacuate on instruction |
| Response | First aid, search and rescue, referral, safe water, food, shelter |
| Recovery | Psychosocial support, follow-up, records, rehabilitation |

- **Nurse’s roles:** emergency response and care, triage and first aid, referral, coordination, education, psychosocial support, surveillance, rehabilitation, support for care givers
- **Common disasters:** earthquakes, floods, landslides, cyclones, drought, epidemics, industrial accidents and armed conflict

<Callout type="must" title="Nurses are part of the emergency response team">
  In disasters, nurses triage, manage mass casualties, maintain surveillance, support families and protect the vulnerable. **You may often be the first to answer, so planning and practice make the difference.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- School health: check-up, nutrition, hygiene, immunisation, education.
- Disaster cycle: preparedness, warning, response, recovery.
- Nurse’s role: triage, first aid, referral, coordination, support.
- Safe water, food and shelter are essentials in disasters.
- New initiatives: ABDM, telemedicine and digital programmes.
