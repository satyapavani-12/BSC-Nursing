## In simple words

National health programmes turn policy into doorstep care. The nurse delivers, records and supervises most of them, so she must know each programme’s aim, drugs and target.

## Communicable disease programmes

| Programme | Strategy |
|---|---|
| National TB Elimination Programme | DOTS, free drugs, Nikshay nutrition support |
| National AIDS Control (NACP) | ICTC, free ART, PPTCT, condom programming |
| National Vector Borne Disease Control | Malaria, dengue, filariasis, kala-azar, Japanese encephalitis |
| National Leprosy Eradication | MDT, deformity prevention, ASHA referral |
| National Programme for Control of Blindness | Cataract surgery, school eye camps |
| National Iodine Deficiency Disorders Control | Iodised salt, goitre survey |
| National Viral Hepatitis Control Programme | Screening, vaccination, treatment |

## Non-communicable disease programmes

- **National Programme for Prevention and Control of Cancer, Diabetes, Cardiovascular Disease and Stroke (NPCDCS)** — screening at CHC and PHC, referral to district hospital
- **National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly (NPHCE)** — geriatric clinics and day care
- **National Programme for Prevention and Control of Deafness (NPPCD)**
- **National Programme for Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases** — tobacco control, healthy lifestyle
- **National Tobacco Control Programme** — COTPA 2003, smoke-free public places, cessation services
- **National Mental Health Programme** and **National Iodine Deficiency Disorders Control Programme**

## Programme monitoring indicators

| Programme | Key indicators |
|---|---|
| TB | Case detection, treatment completion, cure rate |
| HIV | Testing numbers, ART linkage, PPTCT coverage |
| Immunisation (UIP, Mission Indradhanush) | Full immunisation coverage per year |
| Blindness | Cataract surgical rate per million |
| Anaemia (Anemia Mukt Bharat) | Hb improvement in mothers and adolescents |
| NCD | Screening numbers, new cases, referral follow-up |

<Callout type="must" title="Nurses make programmes succeed">
  The programme drugs only work if they reach patients. **Supervising medicine intake, tracing defaulters, keeping records and reporting data are nursing responsibilities that decide whether a programme meets its targets.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- TB: DOTS and Nikshay; HIV: NACP with ART and PPTCT.
- Vector borne: NVBDCP for malaria, dengue, filariasis, kala-azar.
- NCD: NPCDCS screening and lifestyle; tobacco control by COTPA.
- Nutrition: Anemia Mukt Bharat with IFA, vitamin A, iodised salt.
- Nurse: supervise, trace defaulters, record, report.
