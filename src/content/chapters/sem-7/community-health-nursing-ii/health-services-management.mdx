## In simple words

Managing community health services means organising a team, supplies and transport so that care reaches people on time — with clear goals, supervision and evaluation.

## Team building

| Member | Role |
|---|---|
| Medical officer | Clinical and administrative lead |
| Health supervisor and nursing staff | Community practice and supervision |
| Male and female health workers, ASHA | Home visits and follow-up |
| Health assistants and laboratory technicians | Support services |
| Community leaders | Mobilisation, trust, local resources |

**Steps of team building:** identify and group activities, assign people, define authority and communication, arrange resources.

## Supervising community health workers

- Fix duties and the monthly work plan
- Observe visits, technique and records
- Verify stock, medicine and vaccine availability
- Coach on skill gaps; correct errors positively
- Review performance and update workers
- Provide supportive supervision, not only finding faults

## Planning community activities

- **Map the area:** population, villages, households, distance, transport
- **Identify priorities** from records: pregnant women, infants, high-risk groups
- Monthly plan: clinic days, immunisation days, outreach sessions
- Budget: staff, medicines, transport, supplies
- **Contingency planning** for emergencies, epidemics and festivals

## Logistics

- Medicine and vaccine availability with no stock-out
- Cold chain maintenance for vaccines
- Transport plan for referrals
- Communication with the referral hospital and blood bank

<Callout type="must" title="Empowerment, not overload">
  Duty that is allocated must be **supported with knowledge, information and equipment**. Unclear authority causes overload, error and complaints.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Team building: group work, assign, define authority.
- Monthly work plan with routine clinics and visits.
- Supervision: observe, coach, correct, appraise.
- Logistics: medicines, vaccines, cold chain, transport.
- Evaluate coverage and quality; correct gaps.
