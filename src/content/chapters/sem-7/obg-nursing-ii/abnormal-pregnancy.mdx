## In simple words

High-risk means the pregnancy carries danger to the mother or baby. Most problems can be prevented from becoming emergencies with early detection, specialist care and hospital delivery.

## Bleeding in early pregnancy

| Condition | Features | Management |
|---|---|---|
| Abortion | Bleeding, cramping, expulsion of products | Rest, anti-D, emptying the uterus |
| Ectopic pregnancy | Amenorrhoea, unilateral pain, bleeding, dizziness | Emergency surgical management |
| Molar pregnancy | Large uterus, high hCG, vomiting, bleeding | Evacuation and follow-up of hCG |

## Bleeding in late pregnancy (APH)

| Type | Features |
|---|---|
| Placenta praevia | Painless, bright red, recurrent bleeding; placenta covers the cervix; NO vaginal examination |
| Abruptio placentae | Painful, dark blood, tense tender uterus, fetal distress |
| Others | Vasa praevia, local cervical lesions |

**Management:** admit, IV line, cross-match, ultrasound, expectant care or surgical delivery. **Never do a vaginal examination before excluding placenta praevia.**

## Hypertensive disorders

- **Gestational hypertension** — BP above 140/90 after 20 weeks without proteinuria
- **Pre-eclampsia** — hypertension with proteinuria, oedema, headache and reduced fetal growth
- **Eclampsia** — convulsions; airway, magnesium sulfate is first-line, delivery when stable
- **HELLP** — haemolysis, raised liver enzymes, low platelets

<Callout type="must" title="Magnesium sulfate for fits">
  **In eclampsia, give magnesium sulfate as ordered** — watch for toxicity: absent knee jerk, respiratory depression, urine output under 25 mL per hour; keep calcium gluconate ready as the antidote.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Early bleeding: abortion, ectopic, molar.
- Late bleeding: placenta praevia (painless), abruptio (painful).
- Pre-eclampsia and eclampsia with magnesium sulfate.
- Anaemia, diabetes, heart disease need specialist care.
- Multiple pregnancy and malpresentation need extra monitoring.
