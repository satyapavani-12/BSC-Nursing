## In simple words

Abnormal labour is labour where the baby cannot be delivered naturally without danger. Early recognition and timely referral save both lives.

## Abnormal labour — causes

| Cause | Example |
|---|---|
| Passenger | Malpresentation (breech, transverse, face), macrosomia, fetal abnormality |
| Passage | Contracted pelvis, full bladder, cervical fibrosis |
| Power | Weak or incoordinate contractions |
| Psyche | Fear, exhaustion |

## Signs of obstructed labour

- Slow progress of cervical dilatation despite good contractions
- Head does not descend; moulding and caput
- Bandl’s ring, continuous pain, fetal distress
- Maternal exhaustion, tachycardia, dehydration
- Uterus may rupture if unrelieved

## Cord prolapse and shoulder dystocia

**Cord prolapse:** sudden fetal distress with a palpable pulseless cord. **Kneel-elbow position, lift the presenting part by hand, give oxygen, and deliver immediately by the fastest route.**

**Shoulder dystocia:** head delivers but shoulders are stuck. Deliver the anterior arm, use McRoberts manoeuvre and apply suprapubic pressure.

## Rupture of the uterus

- Sudden severe abdominal pain, haemorrhage, shock, absent fetal heart, easily palpable fetal parts
- **Immediate laparotomy and resuscitation**

## Postpartum haemorrhage

**Causes (4 Ts):** Tone (atony — commonest), Tissue (retained products), Trauma (tears), Thrombin (clotting failure).

**Management:** call for help, rub the uterus, give oxytocic, check for tears and retained products, compress the aorta, fluids and blood.

<Callout type="must" title="Obstructed labour must not be allowed to become rupture">
  Signs such as non-descent, constant pain and maternal exhaustion demand **urgent obstetric referral**. A timely caesarean or operative delivery is the only safe plan.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Abnormal labour is due to the passenger, passage, power or psyche.
- Obstructed labour: slow progress, non-descent, constant pain, exhaustion.
- Cord prolapse: relieve the pressure and deliver fast.
- Rupture: sudden pain, shock, absent fetal heart — laparotomy.
- PPH causes: tone, tissue, trauma, thrombin; treat with massage, oxytocics, fluids.
