## In simple words

Gynaecological nursing covers problems of the uterus, ovaries, tubes and breasts, from menstrual disorders to cancer care and surgery.

## Common gynaecological conditions

| Condition | Features |
|---|---|
| Amenorrhoea | Absent periods; pregnancy, thyroid, PCOS, menopause |
| Menorrhagia | Heavy periods; fibroid, hypothyroidism, anaemia |
| Dysmenorrhoea | Painful periods |
| Pelvic inflammatory disease | Lower abdominal pain, fever, discharge; infertility risk |
| Prolapse | Uterus descends, bearing-down sensation, urine retention |
| Fibroid | Heavy periods, mass, pressure symptoms |
| Cervical cancer | Postcoital bleeding; HPV 16, 18; PAP and HPV testing |
| Breast cancer | Lump, nipple discharge, skin changes |
| Ovarian cyst / cancer | Bloating, pain, late diagnosis |
| Endometriosis | Painful periods, painful intercourse, infertility |

## Gynaecological operations

| Operation | Care |
|---|---|
| Dilatation and curettage | Bleeding and pain check |
| Myomectomy, hysterectomy | Early walking, deep breathing, catheter care, avoid heavy lifting |
| Mastoidectomy abdominal / vaginal hysterectomy | Recovery, counselling |
| Laparoscopy | Gas pain from carbon dioxide; early ambulation |

## Infertility and assisted reproduction

- Causes are male, tubal, ovarian (PCOS, premature ovarian failure), uterine (fibroid, septum), endometriosis, immunological, unexplained
- Treatment: ovulation induction, tubal surgery, intrauterine insemination, IVF, ICSI
- **Nursing:** history, teach the cycle, give emotional support, follow up

## Menopause

- Natural menopause between 45 and 55 years; ovary stops producing eggs and hormones
- **Symptoms:** hot flushes, night sweats, mood change, insomnia, vaginal dryness, weight gain, bone thinning
- **Care:** reassurance, hormone therapy where indicated, calcium and vitamin D, exercise, bone density, screening for breast cancer and cervical cancer

<Callout type="must" title="Screening for cervical cancer saves lives">
  A **PAP smear and HPV test detect precancer early.** Encourage screening for sexually active women, vaccination coverage and early referral of abnormal bleeding.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Menstrual disorders: amenorrhoea, menorrhagia, dysmenorrhoea.
- Infections: PID, vaginitis, STI and HIV care.
- Masses: fibroid, ovarian cyst, prolapse.
- Cervical and breast cancers: screen and refer early.
- Post-operative nursing: walking, breathing, catheter, wound.
