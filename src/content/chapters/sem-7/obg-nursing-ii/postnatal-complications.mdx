## In simple words

The first days after birth carry risk. Haemorrhage and infection kill most quickly, and emotional problems follow soon after.

## Postnatal complications

| Complication | Signs |
|---|---|
| Postpartum haemorrhage | Heavy bleeding, soft uterus, bleeding over 500 mL |
| Puerperal sepsis | Fever above 24 hours after birth, foul lochia, tender uterus |
| Endometritis | Fever, lower abdominal pain, offensive discharge |
| Subinvolution | Slow uterine descent and prolonged lochia |
| Urinary retention | Inability to void, painful distended bladder |
| Mastitis and breast abscess | Red painful breast, fever, cracked nipple |
| Thrombophlebitis or pulmonary embolism | Sudden breathlessness, chest pain, calf pain |
| Postnatal depression | Low mood, tearfulness, poor care, guilt |
| Postnatal psychosis | Delusions, confusion, refusal to feed the baby |

## Nursing actions by complication

- **PPH:** call for help, rub the uterus, give oxytocic, check for tears and retained products, compress the aorta, fluids and blood
- **Sepsis:** cultures, antibiotics, perineal care, isolation if infectious
- **Subinvolution:** treat infection and anaemia; review for retained products
- **Urinary retention:** privacy, warm water, catheterise if needed
- **Mastitis:** continue feeding, correct attachment, warm compress, antibiotics as prescribed
- **Thromboembolism:** report breathlessness and calf pain; anticoagulants as prescribed
- **Postnatal depression:** screen with EPDS, reassure, refer; protect mother and baby

<Callout type="must" title="Fever after delivery is never normal">
  **Any fever after 24 hours from delivery needs urgent assessment** for endometritis, wound or breast infection, urinary infection or deep vein thrombosis. Early antibiotics prevent septic shock.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- PPH: massage, oxytocics, fluids, blood.
- Sepsis: fever, foul lochia; cultures and antibiotics.
- Subinvolution, urinary retention, mastitis, thromboembolism.
- Postnatal blues, depression and psychosis need screening and referral.
- Fever after 24 hours is never normal.
