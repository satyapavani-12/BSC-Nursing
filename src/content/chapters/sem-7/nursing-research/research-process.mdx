## In simple words

Research is a careful, controlled way of finding new answers. Nursing research asks questions about nursing so that care is based on evidence, not habit.

## Purposes of nursing research

- Describe and clarify nursing practice
- Explain and predict patient outcomes
- Discover new nursing knowledge
- Test nursing interventions
- Improve the standard and quality of care
- Build the professional body of knowledge

## Steps of the research process

1. Formulate the research problem
2. Review the literature
3. Formulate objectives and hypotheses
4. Design the study and select the sample
5. Develop data collection tools
6. Collect data ethically
7. Analyse data
8. Discuss, conclude and make recommendations
9. Write the report and disseminate

## Types of research

| Type | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Quantitative | How many, how much, what effect |
| Qualitative | What does it mean to the person |
| Experimental | Does the intervention cause the change |
| Non-experimental | What is the relationship between variables |
| Descriptive | What is the situation |
| Correlational | Is one variable related to another |

<Callout type="must" title="Nursing research is a nursing responsibility">
  Evaluating and using evidence is part of nursing practice. **A nurse who reads, questions and applies evidence improves the patient’s outcome, not only her own knowledge.**
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Research is organised, purposeful and search for new knowledge.
- Nine steps from problem to report.
- Quantitative vs qualitative; experimental vs non-experimental.
- Ethics before data collection.
- Dissemination and application complete the research.
