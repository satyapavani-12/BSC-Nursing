## In simple words

The design is the plan for answerng the research question. It decides how data are collected and how trustworthy the conclusions are.

## Quantitative designs

| Design | What it does |
|---|---|
| Descriptive | Describes what exists |
| Correlational | Tests relationships between variables |
| Cross-sectional | Data at one point of time |
| Longitudinal | Data over time |
| Case study | Deep study of one case |
| Comparative | Compares two or more groups |

## Experimental designs

| Design | Description |
|---|---|
| True experimental | Randomisation, control group, intervention, measurement |
| Quasi-experimental | Intervention without randomisation |
| Pre-test / post-test control group | Measures before and after the intervention |
| Solomon four-group | Controls for the testing effect |

**Elements:** manipulation, control, randomisation, measurement.

## Qualitative designs

- **Phenomenology** — lived experience
- **Grounded theory** — builds theory from data
- **Ethnography** — culture of a group
- **Case study** — a case in depth
- **Historical research** — past records

## Threats to validity

| Threat | Effect |
|---|---|
| History | Outside events change the result |
| Maturation | Natural change over time |
| Testing effect | Repeating the test changes results |
| Instrument change | Tool or observer changes |
| Selection bias | Groups differ at the start |
| Attrition | Participants dropping out |
| Maturation | Participants grow tired |

<Callout type="must" title="Design decides how much you can claim">
  A descriptive study can say "this is the situation". Only an experimental study with control **can say the intervention caused the change**.
</Callout>

## 60-second revision

- Designs: descriptive, correlational, experimental, quasi-experimental.
- True experiments randomise and control; quasi-experiments do not.
- Pre-test and post-test with control group is common in nursing.
- Qualitative designs explore meaning.
- History, maturation, testing, selection and attrition threaten validity.
