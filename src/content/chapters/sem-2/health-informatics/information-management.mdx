## In simple words

Health information management is the organisation, storage and use of health data and records. Good information management means data is accurate, complete and available when needed.

Managers use information to make decisions about staffing, budgets, quality and services.

## Elements of data quality

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Data is correct |
| Completeness | All required data is present |
| Timeliness | Data is available when needed |
| Consistency | Data is the same across systems |
| Relevance | Data is useful for the purpose |

## Uses of health information

- Staffing and rostering.
- Budget planning.
- Quality monitoring and improvement.
- Reporting to regulators.
- Service planning.

## Nurse’s role

- Document accurately and completely.
- Record in real time.
- Use standard terms and abbreviations.
- Report data errors.
- Follow retention and confidentiality rules.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Documentation** — accurate, complete, timely records support good decisions.
- **Standard terms** — use approved abbreviations and terminology.
- **Data entry** — enter data correctly in the electronic record.
- **Reporting** — contribute to ward reports and quality data.
- **Confidentiality** — protect health information at all times.
