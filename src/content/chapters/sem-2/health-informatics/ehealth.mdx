## In simple words

eHealth uses the internet and digital technologies for health care and health information. Telehealth is care delivered at a distance using technology.

Nurses should guide patients to reliable online health information and understand telehealth basics.

## eHealth tools

| Tool | Example |
|---|---|
| Telehealth | Video consultation with a doctor |
| mHealth | Health apps on a phone |
| Patient portal | Online access to records and messages |
| eLearning | Online nursing education |
| Social media | Health education and support groups |

## Evaluating online health information

- Who runs the site? (government, university, hospital — more trustworthy)
- Is the information current? (check the date)
- Is it evidence-based? (references to research)
- Is it trying to sell something? (be cautious)

## Nurse’s role

- Teach patients to use patient portals.
- Support telehealth visits (help with technology).
- Guide patients to reliable websites.
- Warn against unreliable or commercial health claims.
- Protect privacy during telehealth visits.

  **eHealth uses digital technology for health care and information — telehealth, apps and patient portals support, but do not replace, in-person care.**
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Telehealth visits** — help patients connect; ensure privacy; assist with the technology.
- **Patient portal** — show patients how to view results and message the team.
- **Health education** — recommend reliable websites; warn against commercial claims.
- **mHealth apps** — suggest reputable apps for medication reminders or tracking.
- **Continuing education** — use eLearning for your own learning.
