## In simple words

Health information systems (HIS) are computer systems that manage health data in hospitals and clinics. They connect departments and improve care coordination.

Key systems: hospital information system (HIS), electronic health record (EHR), picture archiving (PACS), radiology information (RIS), laboratory information (LIS).

## HIS modules

| Module | Function |
|---|---|
| Patient registration | Admission, demographics |
| Electronic health record | Clinical notes, results, orders |
| Pharmacy | Medication orders, dispensing |
| Laboratory | Test orders and results |
| Radiology (PACS/RIS) | Images and reports |
| Billing | Charges and payments |

## Electronic health record (EHR)

An EHR is a digital version of a patient’s chart. It includes history, diagnoses, medicines, allergies, immunisations, results and notes. It is accessible to authorised providers.

## Benefits

- Legible, complete records.
- Quick access to results and history.
- Fewer duplicate tests.
- Better coordination between departments.
- Data for reporting and quality improvement.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Document in the EHR** — chart notes, vital signs and care in real time.
- **View results** — check lab and imaging results as soon as available.
- **Medication orders** — receive and process electronic orders and alerts.
- **Coordinate care** — share information with other departments and providers.
- **Protect access** — log in with your own ID; view only what you need for care.
