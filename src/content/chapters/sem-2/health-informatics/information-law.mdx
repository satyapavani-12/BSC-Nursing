## In simple terms

Information law and governance protect patient information — who can see it, how it is used and how long it is kept. Confidentiality is a core duty of nurses.

Key laws and principles: consent, data protection, confidentiality and records retention.

## Confidentiality and consent

- Confidentiality: do not share patient information without permission or legal authority.
- Consent: get permission before sharing information or using it for purposes other than care.
- Minimum necessary: share only what is needed for the purpose.

## Data protection and governance

- Store records securely (locked cabinets, password-protected computers).
- Keep records for the required retention period.
- Dispose of records securely (shredding, secure deletion).
- Report breaches immediately.
- Follow hospital policy and applicable data-protection law.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Documentation** — record only relevant information; store records securely.
- **Communication** — do not discuss patients in public areas; share only with the care team.
- **Consent** — get permission before sharing information outside the care team.
- **Records** — keep records for the required time; dispose securely.
- **Breaches** — report lost, stolen or improperly accessed records immediately.

- Confidentiality, consent, minimum necessary.

- Report breaches immediately.
- No discussing patients in public.
