## In simple words

Health informatics is the use of information and technology to manage health data and improve patient care. It turns raw data into useful knowledge that supports decisions.

The DIKW hierarchy shows how data becomes wisdom: data → information → knowledge → wisdom.

## DIKW hierarchy

| Level | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Data | Raw facts | Blood pressure 150/90 |
| Information | Data with meaning | “The patient’s BP is high” |
| Knowledge | Applied understanding | “High BP needs treatment” |
| Wisdom | Judicious action | “Start this patient on medication and monitor” |

## Benefits of health IT

- Legible, complete records.
- Quick access to patient information.
- Decision support (alerts, reminders).
- Better coordination between teams.
- Data for quality improvement and research.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Document accurately** — accurate data becomes useful information.
- **Use decision support** — heed alerts and reminders in the electronic record.
- **Access evidence** — use electronic resources for best practice.
- **Protect data** — keep patient information confidential and secure.
- **Participate in quality projects** — use data to improve care.
