## In simple words

Computers are now part of everyday nursing — documentation, medication records, test results and communication. Nurses need basic computer skills to work safely and efficiently.

A computer has hardware (physical parts) and software (programs). Nurses use both to access and record patient information.

## Basic computer parts

| Part | Function |
|---|---|
| Monitor | Shows information on screen |
| Keyboard | Enters text and commands |
| Mouse | Moves the cursor and selects |
| CPU | Processes information |
| Storage | Saves files and data |

## Basic skills for nurses

- Turn the computer on/off correctly.
- Log in with your own username and password — never share passwords.
- Open, save and close files.
- Use the electronic health record to chart and view results.
- Log out when finished — never leave a computer unlocked.

## Computer use in nursing

- Document nursing notes and vital signs.
- View lab results and imaging reports.
- Check medication administration records.
- Access policies, procedures and drug references.
- Communicate with the health team.

  **Never share your login password.** Log out of the computer when you step away — patient information is confidential.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Documentation** — chart nursing notes, vital signs and care given.
- **View results** — check lab, imaging and medication orders.
- **Medication safety** — scan and check medications against the electronic record.
- **Information access** — look up policies, drug references and procedures.
- **Communication** — send secure messages to the health team.
