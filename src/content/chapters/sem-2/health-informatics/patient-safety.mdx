## In simple words

Patient safety means preventing harm to patients during care. Errors can happen — the goal is to build systems that catch them before they reach the patient.

A culture of safety encourages reporting and learning from errors rather than blaming individuals.

## International Patient Safety Goals (key ones)

| Goal | What to do |
|---|---|
| Identify patients correctly | Use two identifiers (name + ID band) |
| Improve communication | Clear handover, read-back orders |
| Improve medication safety | Five rights, high-alert drugs |
| Ensure correct surgery | Mark the site, time-out |
| Reduce infection | Hand hygiene, aseptic technique |
| Reduce falls | Assess risk, assist mobility |

## Error prevention

- Use two patient identifiers before any procedure or medication.
- Follow the five rights of medication administration.
- Do time-out before surgery (correct patient, site, procedure).
- Report near misses and incidents — they reveal system weaknesses.
- Use checklists and standard protocols.

## Culture of safety

- Report errors without fear of blame.
- Learn from near misses.
- Focus on system fixes, not individual blame.
- Support open communication.

</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Identify the patient** — check name and ID band before every medication, procedure or blood transfusion.
- **Hand over clearly** — use SBAR; read back verbal and telephone orders.
- **Medication safety** — five rights; extra care with high-alert drugs.
- **Report incidents** — report errors and near misses through the incident system.
- **Fall prevention** — assess risk, use call bells, assist mobility.
