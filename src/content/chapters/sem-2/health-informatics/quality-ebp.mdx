## In simple words

Quality improvement (QI) is the ongoing effort to make care safer, more effective and more patient-centred. Evidence-based practice (EBP) uses the best research evidence, clinical expertise and patient preferences to make decisions.

Together, QI and EBP improve patient outcomes.

## Steps of EBP

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| Ask | Form a clinical question (PICO) |
| Acquire | Search for the best evidence |
| Appraise | Critically appraise the evidence |
| Apply | Integrate with expertise and patient preference |
| Assess | Evaluate the outcome |

## Nursing sensitive outcomes

- Patient falls.
- Pressure ulcers.
- Hospital-acquired infections.
- Medication errors.
- Patient satisfaction.

## Quality improvement

- Audits — check care against standards.
- Standards — expected levels of care.
- Indicators — measurable outcomes.
- PDCA cycle — Plan, Do, Check, Act.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Ask clinical questions** — use PICO (Patient, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome).
- **Use best evidence** — read guidelines and research before changing practice.
- **Join audits** — help check care against standards.
- **Monitor outcomes** — track falls, infections, medication errors on your ward.
- **Suggest improvements** — use PDCA to test small changes.

## 60-second revision

- QI improves care safety, effectiveness, patient-centred care.
- EBP = evidence + expertise + patient preferences.
- Steps: Ask, Acquire, Appraise, Apply, Assess.
- Nursing sensitive outcomes: falls, pressure ulcers, infections, med errors, satisfaction.
- QI tools: audits, standards, indicators, PDCA.
