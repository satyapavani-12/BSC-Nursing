## In simple words

Medication administration is giving prescribed drugs safely and correctly. Errors can harm or even kill patients, so strict checking is essential.

The nurse checks the prescription, prepares the drug, gives it by the right route, observes the patient and documents everything.

## Rights of medication administration

| Right | What to check |
|---|---|
| Right patient | Name and ID band |
| Right drug | Name matches prescription |
| Right dose | Correct amount and calculation |
| Right route | Oral, IV, IM, subcutaneous, etc. |
| Right time | Correct time and frequency |
| Right reason | Why the drug is given |
| Right documentation | Record after giving |

## Common routes

| Route | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Oral | Tablets, syrups | Safest, most common |
| Intravenous (IV) | Injection, infusion | Fast action; monitor closely |
| Intramuscular (IM) | Into muscle | Vaccines, some antibiotics |
| Subcutaneous | Under skin | Insulin, heparin |
| Topical | Creams, ointments | Local effect |

## Medication safety

- Check the label three times: when taking from storage, before pouring, before giving.
- Do not leave medications unattended.
- Calculate doses carefully; have another nurse check high-risk doses.
- Report errors immediately — do not hide them.
- Watch for allergic reactions and side effects.

<Callout type="must" title="Check three times">
  **Check the medication label three times — when taking it from storage, before preparing and before giving.** Never give a drug you have not checked yourself.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Before giving** — check all rights; know the drug’s action, dose and side effects.
- **During administration** — stay with the patient; ensure the drug is swallowed or given correctly.
- **After giving** — observe for effect and adverse reactions; document immediately.
- **Errors** — report immediately; follow hospital policy; do not blame or hide.
- **High-risk drugs** — insulin, heparin, opioids, chemotherapy — double-check with another nurse.
