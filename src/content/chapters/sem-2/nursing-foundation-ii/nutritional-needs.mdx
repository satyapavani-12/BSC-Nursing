## In simple words

Nutrition is essential for health, growth, healing and immunity. Nurses assess nutritional status, plan diets and help patients eat.

A balanced diet gives all nutrients in correct amounts. Patients who cannot eat normally may need tube feeding or intravenous fluids.

## Components of a balanced diet

| Food group | Examples | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Cereals | Rice, wheat, ragi | Energy |
| Pulses | Dal, beans, soya | Protein |
| Milk and products | Milk, curd, paneer | Calcium, protein |
| Vegetables and fruits | Seasonal produce | Vitamins, minerals, fibre |
| Fats and oils | Oil, ghee, nuts | Energy, fat-soluble vitamins |
| Water | Drinking water | Transport, temperature |

## Feeding methods

- **Oral feeding** — normal eating; assist weak patients.
- **Spoon feeding** — for patients who cannot hold a spoon.
- **Tube feeding** — nasogastric or gastrostomy tube; as prescribed.
- **Intravenous fluids** — when the gut cannot be used; strictly as prescribed.

## Assessing nutritional status

- Weight and height; BMI.
- Mid-upper arm circumference.
- Signs: pallor, oedema, muscle wasting, poor wound healing.
- Dietary history — what the patient usually eats.

<Callout type="must" title="Watch intake and output">
  **Record everything the patient eats and drinks.** Poor intake over days leads to malnutrition and slow healing.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Assess on admission** — weight, appetite, chewing/swallowing ability, special diets.
- **Assist with feeding** — position upright, feed slowly, check swallowing.
- **Tube feeding** — check tube position, flush before and after, give as prescribed.
- **Diabetic diet** — serve meals on time; never skip if on insulin.
- **Report poor intake** — tell the doctor/dietitian if the patient is not eating.

## 60-second revision

- Balanced diet = all nutrients in correct amounts.
- Feeding: oral, spoon, tube, IV fluids.
- Assess: weight, height, BMI, MUAC, deficiency signs.
- Position upright for feeding; check tube position.
- Record intake and output; report poor intake.
