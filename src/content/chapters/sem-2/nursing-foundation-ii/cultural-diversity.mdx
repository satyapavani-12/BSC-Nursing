## In simple words

Cultural diversity means people differ in culture, religion, language, food and beliefs. Culturally competent care respects these differences and adapts care to each patient’s culture.

Culture affects health beliefs, diet, modesty, family roles, pain expression and decision-making.

## Culturally sensitive care

- Ask about cultural and religious beliefs.
- Respect dietary restrictions (fasting, vegetarian, halal, kosher).
- Respect modesty — offer same-gender care when possible.
- Use interpreters when language differs; do not use children.
- Involve the family if the patient’s culture makes decisions as a family.

## Common cultural effects on health

| Area | Cultural effect |
|---|---|
| Diet | Fasting, food restrictions, vegetarianism |
| Modesty | Same-gender preference, covering the body |
| Decision-making | Individual or family-based |
| Pain | Open expression or silent endurance |
| Illness beliefs | Natural, supernatural or biomedical causes |

</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Admission assessment** — ask about culture, religion, language and food preferences.
- **Diet** — arrange culturally appropriate meals; respect fasting.
- **Modesty** — offer same-gender caregivers for intimate care.
- **Communication** — use trained interpreters; translate key documents.
- **Family involvement** — ask who makes decisions and involve them.

## 60-second revision

- Cultural diversity = differences in culture, religion, language, beliefs.
- Culturally competent care respects and adapts to culture.
- Culture affects diet, modesty, family roles, pain, decisions.
- Use interpreters; respect fasting and modesty.
- Ask who makes decisions; involve family.
