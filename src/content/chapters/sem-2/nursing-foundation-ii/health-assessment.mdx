## In simple words

Health assessment is the systematic collection of information about a patient’s health. It is the first step of the nursing process and guides all nursing care.

Assessment gives a baseline, identifies health problems and helps plan care. It is continuous throughout hospitalization.

## Methods of assessment

| Method | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Inspection | Looking | Skin colour, wounds |
| Palpation | Feeling | Pulse, tenderness |
| Percussion | Tapping | Lung density |
| Auscultation | Listening | Heart and bowel sounds |

## Components

- **Health history** — present illness, past history, family history, lifestyle.
- **Physical examination** — head to toe, systematic.
- **Vital signs** — temperature, pulse, respiration, blood pressure, SpO2.
- **Psychosocial** — mood, support, coping, culture.

  **Health assessment is the foundation of the nursing process.** It identifies problems and guides planning, implementation and evaluation.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Admission** — do a full head-to-toe assessment and record baseline findings.
- **Every shift** — assess vital signs and any change in condition.
- **Before procedures** — assess to plan and to compare afterwards.
- **Deteriorating patient** — reassess frequently and report changes early.
- **Documentation** — record findings factually, with date, time and signature.
