## In simple words

Diagnostic tests help diagnose disease, plan treatment and monitor progress. Nurses prepare patients, explain procedures, collect samples and monitor after the test.

Common tests: blood tests, urine tests, ECG, X-ray, CT scan, MRI, endoscopy.

## Common diagnostic tests

| Test | Purpose | Key preparation |
|---|---|---|
| Blood glucose | Sugar level | Fasting for fasting glucose |
| ECG | Heart activity | Lie still; remove metal |
| X-ray | Bones, chest | Remove jewellery; pregnancy check |
| CT scan | Cross-sectional images | Contrast allergy check |
| MRI | Detailed soft tissue | Remove metal; claustrophobia check |
| Urine routine | Infection, kidney | Clean-catch sample |

## Nurse’s role

- **Before** — explain the test, get consent, prepare the patient (fasting, contrast consent).
- **During** — assist, monitor, comfort the patient.
- **After** — monitor for reactions, remove IV, encourage fluids after contrast, report results.

## Special precautions

- **Contrast allergy** — ask about previous reactions; have emergency drugs ready.
- **Pregnancy** — avoid X-ray/CT unless essential; shield the abdomen.
- **Claustrophobia** — reassure; sedation may be needed for MRI.
- **Kidney function** — check creatinine before contrast dye.

<Callout type="must" title="Informed consent">
  **Get informed consent before invasive tests and contrast studies.** Explain what will happen and why.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Explain clearly** — tell the patient what to expect in simple words.
- **Check preparation** — fasting, contrast consent, medication holding as ordered.
- **Monitor after contrast** — watch for allergic reactions; encourage fluids.
- **Safety** — remove metal for MRI; pregnancy check before X-ray/CT.
- **Report results** — inform the doctor of abnormal or critical results.

## 60-second revision

- Diagnostic tests diagnose and monitor disease.
- Tests: blood, urine, ECG, X-ray, CT, MRI.
- Nurse: before (explain, consent, prepare), during (assist, monitor), after (monitor, report).
- Consent before invasive/contrast tests.
- Pregnancy check before X-ray/CT; remove metal for MRI.
