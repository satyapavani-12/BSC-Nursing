## In simple words

Food safety means handling, preparing and storing food so that it does not cause illness. Unsafe food can carry germs and cause diarrhoea, vomiting and food poisoning.

The key principles are: keep clean, separate raw and cooked food, cook thoroughly, keep food at safe temperatures, and use safe water and raw materials.

## Five keys to safer food (WHO)

| Key | What to do |
|---|---|
| Keep clean | Wash hands, surfaces and utensils |
| Separate raw and cooked | Use separate boards and knives |
| Cook thoroughly | Heat food to kill germs |
| Keep at safe temperatures | Refrigerate perishables; serve hot food hot |
| Use safe water and raw materials | Use clean water; wash fruits and vegetables |

## Food-borne illness

Common causes: bacteria (Salmonella, E. coli), viruses, parasites, toxins. Symptoms: nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, abdominal pain, fever.

Prevention: hand hygiene, safe cooking, safe storage, clean water, excluding sick food handlers.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Patient food** — ensure hospital food is freshly cooked, covered and served at safe temperatures.
- **Health teaching** — teach hand washing before eating and after toilet; safe food storage at home.
- **Food-borne illness** — suspect it when vomiting and diarrhoea occur after a common meal; report outbreaks.
- **Vulnerable groups** — children, pregnant women and elderly are at higher risk; be extra careful.
- **Kitchen hygiene** — support clean kitchens, safe water and healthy food handlers.
