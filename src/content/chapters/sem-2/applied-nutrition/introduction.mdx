## In simple words

Nutrition is the science of food, nutrients and how the body uses them. Nutrients are the chemical components of food that the body needs for energy, growth and repair.

Good nutrition keeps people healthy and helps them recover from illness. Poor nutrition leads to deficiency diseases and slower healing.

## Classes of nutrients

| Nutrient | Examples | Main function |
|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrates | Rice, wheat, sugar | Energy |
| Proteins | Dal, eggs, milk, meat | Growth and repair |
| Fats | Oil, ghee, nuts | Energy, insulation |
| Vitamins | Fruits, vegetables | Regulation and protection |
| Minerals | Salt, leafy greens | Bones, blood, nerves |
| Water | Drinking water, fluids | Transport, temperature |
| Fibre | Whole grains, fruits | Bowel movement |

## Nutrition vs nutrients

- **Nutrition** — the process of taking in and using food.
- **Nutrients** — the individual chemical components in food.

## Why nutrition matters in nursing

- **Healing** — protein and vitamin C help wound healing.
- **Immunity** — good nutrition protects against infection.
- **Energy** — carbohydrates and fats give energy for activity.
- **Growth** — children and pregnant women need extra nutrition.
- **Disease management** — therapeutic diets help in diabetes, kidney and heart disease.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Screen for malnutrition** — check weight, appetite and eating ability on admission.
- **Encourage good intake** — offer appealing, culturally acceptable food; assist patients who need help eating.
- **Therapeutic diets** — follow the prescribed diet (diabetic, renal, low-salt) and explain it to the patient.
- **Fluid balance** — record intake and output; encourage fluids unless restricted.
- **Refer to dietetics** — refer patients with diabetes, kidney disease or malnutrition.

## 60-second revision

- Nutrition = science of food and nutrients.
- Six nutrients: carbs, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water.
- Carbs/fats = energy; proteins = growth and repair.
- Good nutrition = healing, immunity, energy, growth.
- Therapeutic diets for diabetes, kidney, heart disease.
