## In simple words

India runs several national nutrition programmes to fight malnutrition, especially among children, pregnant women and mothers. Nurses help implement, monitor and educate about these programmes at the community level.

## Major national programmes

| Programme | Target group | Main features |
|---|---|---|
| ICDS | Children 0–6 years, pregnant/lactating mothers | Supplementary nutrition, health check-ups, preschool education |
| Mid-Day Meal | School children | Free cooked meal in government schools |
| Poshan Abhiyaan | All, focus on women and children | Reduce stunting, wasting, anaemia; behaviour change |
| Anaemia Mukt Bharat | Women, children, adolescents | Iron-folic acid, deworming, testing |
| MDM / school health | School-age children | Nutrition + health education |

## Nurse’s role

- Identify malnourished children and refer them.
- Distribute or supervise iron-folic acid supplements.
- Conduct growth monitoring and nutrition education.
- Participate in village health and nutrition days.
- Maintain records and report coverage.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Village health and nutrition day** — weigh children, give supplements, counsel mothers.
- **School health** — check children’s nutrition, give iron-folic acid, teach hygiene.
- **Antenatal visits** — counsel pregnant women on diet and supplements.
- **Growth monitoring** — plot weights and refer faltering children.
- **Record keeping** — maintain programme records and report to supervisors.
