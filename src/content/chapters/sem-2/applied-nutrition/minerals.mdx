## In simple words

Minerals are inorganic elements needed in small amounts for body processes — building bones, making blood, nerve impulses and fluid balance.

Major minerals (needed in larger amounts): calcium, phosphorus, magnesium, sodium, potassium, chlorine, sulphur. Trace minerals (needed in tiny amounts): iron, iodine, zinc, fluorine, selenium.

## Key minerals

| Mineral | Main sources | Main function | Deficiency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calcium | Milk, milk products, green leafy vegetables | Bones, teeth, muscle contraction | Osteoporosis, rickets |
| Iron | Liver, meat, leafy vegetables, dates | Haemoglobin formation | Anaemia |
| Iodine | Iodised salt, seafood | Thyroid hormone | Goitre, hypothyroidism |
| Sodium | Salt, processed foods | Fluid balance, nerve impulses | Hypertension if excess |
| Potassium | Fruits, vegetables, coconut water | Nerve and muscle function | Weakness, irregular heartbeat |

## Important points

- **Calcium** needs vitamin D for absorption. Milk is the best source.
- **Iron** is absorbed better with vitamin C (citrus fruits) and worse with tea/coffee.
- **Iodine** deficiency causes goitre — use iodised salt.
- **Sodium** excess raises blood pressure; restrict in hypertension.

<Callout type="must" title="Iron absorption">
  **Iron is absorbed better with vitamin C (citrus fruits) and worse with tea or coffee.** Give iron tablets with vitamin C-rich juice, not tea.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Anaemia** — encourage iron-rich foods (liver, meat, leafy vegetables, dates) and iron supplements as prescribed.
- **Goitre prevention** — advise use of iodised salt.
- **Hypertension** — advise salt restriction as prescribed.
- **Bone health** — encourage milk and milk products; vitamin D for calcium absorption.
- **Patient teaching** — explain not to take iron with tea/coffee; take with vitamin C juice.

## 60-second revision

- Minerals: inorganic elements for body processes.
- Calcium = bones; Iron = blood; Iodine = thyroid.
- Iron + vitamin C = better absorption; iron - tea/coffee.
- Sodium excess = hypertension; iodised salt prevents goitre.
- Deficiencies: Ca osteoporosis, Fe anaemia, I goitre.
