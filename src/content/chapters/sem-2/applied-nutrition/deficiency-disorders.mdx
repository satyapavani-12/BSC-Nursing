## In simple words

Nutritional deficiency disorders happen when the body does not get enough of one or more nutrients over time. Common ones in India are protein-energy malnutrition, anaemia, goitre, night blindness and rickets.

Early detection and correct diet prevent most of these disorders.

## Common deficiency disorders

| Disorder | Nutrient lacking | Main signs |
|---|---|---|
| Marasmus | Energy + protein | Severe wasting, no oedema, “old man” face |
| Kwashiorkor | Protein | Oedema, skin/hair changes, apathy |
| Anaemia | Iron, folate, B12 | Pallor, fatigue, breathlessness |
| Goitre | Iodine | Swelling in the neck |
| Night blindness | Vitamin A | Cannot see in dim light |
| Rickets | Vitamin D | Bow legs, delayed teething |
| Scurvy | Vitamin C | Bleeding gums, poor wound healing |

## Protein-energy malnutrition (PEM)

- **Marasmus** — deficiency of both energy and protein; common under 1 year; marked weight loss and wasting.
- **Kwashiorkor** — protein deficiency with adequate energy; common 1–3 years; oedema, “moon face”, skin and hair changes.

## Prevention

- **Anaemia** — iron-rich foods, iron-folic acid tablets, treat worms, vitamin C with meals.
- **Goitre** — use iodised salt.
- **Night blindness** — vitamin A-rich foods (carrot, green leafy vegetables, liver); vitamin A dose to children as per schedule.
- **Rickets** — vitamin D (sunlight), calcium-rich foods.

<Callout type="must" title="Marasmus vs kwashiorkor">
  **Marasmus = energy + protein deficiency, severe wasting, no oedema. Kwashiorkor = protein deficiency with oedema.** Both need careful feeding.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Screen children** — weigh regularly, plot on growth charts, look for wasting and oedema.
- **Anaemia** — check for pallor (palms, nails, conjunctiva); give iron-folic acid as prescribed.
- **Vitamin A** — give high-dose vitamin A to children as per national schedule.
- **Goitre** — advise iodised salt; refer if neck swelling is seen.
- **Health education** — teach families about balanced diet and hygiene.

## 60-second revision

- Deficiency disorders = lack of nutrients over time.
- Marasmus = wasting, no oedema; Kwashiorkor = oedema, moon face.
- Anaemia = pallor, fatigue (iron/folate/B12).
- Goitre = iodine; neck swelling.
- Prevent: iodised salt, iron-rich food, vitamin A, sunlight.
