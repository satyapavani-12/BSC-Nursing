## In simple words

Vitamins are organic compounds needed in small amounts to regulate body processes and protect health. They do not give energy directly.

Vitamins are divided into fat-soluble (A, D, E, K) and water-soluble (B complex and C). Fat-soluble vitamins are stored in the body; water-soluble ones are not stored much and need regular intake.

## Classification and key facts

| Vitamin | Type | Main sources | Deficiency disease |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vitamin A | Fat | Liver, fish oil, carrot, green leafy vegetables | Night blindness, xerophthalmia |
| Vitamin D | Fat | Sunlight, fish oil, fortified milk | Rickets, osteomalacia |
| Vitamin E | Fat | Vegetable oils, nuts, seeds | Rare; nerve problems |
| Vitamin K | Fat | Green leafy vegetables, liver | Bleeding disorders |
| Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) | Water | Whole grains, pork, legumes | Beriberi |
| Vitamin B3 (Niacin) | Water | Meat, fish, groundnuts | Pellagra |
| Vitamin C | Water | Citrus fruits, amla, guava | Scurvy |
| Vitamin B12 | Water | Meat, fish, eggs, milk | Pernicious anaemia |
| Folic acid | Water | Leafy vegetables, liver | Megaloblastic anaemia |

## Functions

- Vitamin A: vision, immunity, skin health.
- Vitamin D: calcium absorption, bone health.
- Vitamin C: wound healing, immunity, iron absorption.
- B complex: energy metabolism, nerve function, blood formation.
- Vitamin K: blood clotting.
- Folate and B12: red blood cell formation, prevent neural tube defects.

<Callout type="must" title="Fat vs water soluble">
  **Fat-soluble vitamins: A, D, E, K — remembered as “ADEK”.** Water-soluble: B complex and C. Fat-soluble are stored; water-soluble need regular intake.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Diet counselling** — advise fruits, vegetables, whole grains and fortified foods.
- **Deficiency signs** — recognize night blindness (vitamin A), bleeding gums (vitamin C), beriberi (B1).
- **Supplements** — give iron-folic acid in pregnancy; vitamin D as prescribed.
- **Newborns** — vitamin K at birth to prevent bleeding.
- **Patient teaching** — explain that excess fat-soluble vitamins can be toxic; do not self-supplement heavily.

## 60-second revision

- Vitamins regulate processes; no energy directly.
- Fat-soluble ADEK; water-soluble B complex, C.
- A = night blindness; D = rickets; C = scurvy; B1 = beriberi.
- K = bleeding; given to newborns.
- C helps iron absorption and wound healing.
