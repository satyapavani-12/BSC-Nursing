## In simple words

Lipids are fats and fat-like substances. They provide energy, form cell membranes, carry fat-soluble vitamins and make hormones. Excess lipid in the blood (hyperlipidaemia) increases the risk of atherosclerosis and heart disease.

Lipids are digested slowly and need bile for emulsification and lipase for breakdown.

## Types of lipids

| Type | Examples | Main feature |
|---|---|---|
| Simple lipids | Triglycerides, fats, oils | Glycerol plus fatty acids |
| Compound lipids | Phospholipids, lipoproteins | Contain phosphorus or protein |
| Derived lipids | Cholesterol, steroid hormones | From breakdown of other lipids |

Fatty acids:
- **Saturated** — animal fats, solid at room temperature; raise blood cholesterol.
- **Unsaturated** — vegetable oils, fish; generally healthier.
- **Essential fatty acids** — linoleic and alpha-linolenic acid; must come from food.

## Digestion and absorption

Fat digestion is minimal in the stomach. In the small intestine, bile emulsifies fat into tiny droplets, and pancreatic lipase breaks triglycerides into fatty acids and monoglycerides.

These form **micelles**, are absorbed into intestinal cells, reassembled into triglycerides, packaged into **chylomicrons**, and enter the lymph before reaching the blood.

## Lipoproteins

Lipoproteins carry lipids in the blood:

| Lipoprotein | Role |
|---|---|
| Chylomicrons | Carry dietary fat from gut |
| VLDL | Carry triglycerides from liver |
| LDL | Carries cholesterol to tissues — “bad cholesterol” |
| HDL | Carries cholesterol back to liver — “good cholesterol” |

Desirable total cholesterol: below **200 mg/dL**. HDL should be high; LDL should be low.

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Diet counselling** — advise less saturated fat (ghee, butter, red meat) and more unsaturated fat (vegetable oils, fish).
- **Hyperlipidaemia** — encourage medication compliance, diet and exercise as prescribed.
- **Weight management** — excess fat intake leads to obesity; counsel on balanced diet.
- **Fat-soluble vitamins** — vitamins A, D, E and K need fat for absorption.
- **Patient teaching** — explain that not all fat is bad; the body needs some for health.

## 60-second revision

- Lipids = fats; energy, membranes, vitamins, hormones.
- Types: simple, compound, derived.
- Bile emulsifies; lipase digests; chylomicrons carry fat.
- LDL bad; HDL good cholesterol.
- Total cholesterol below 200 mg/dL.
- Fat-soluble vitamins: A, D, E, K.
