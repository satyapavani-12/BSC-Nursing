## In simple words

When red blood cells die, their haemoglobin is broken down. The haem part becomes bilirubin, which the liver processes and excretes in bile. If this pathway is blocked or overloaded, bilirubin builds up and causes jaundice (yellow skin and eyes).

Normal serum bilirubin is about **0.3–1.2 mg/dL**.

## Heme breakdown pathway

1. **Red blood cell breakdown** — haemoglobin splits into globin and haem.
2. **Haem to biliverdin** — haem is converted to biliverdin.
3. **Biliverdin to bilirubin** — unconjugated bilirubin forms.
4. **Transport** — unconjugated bilirubin binds albumin and travels to the liver.
5. **Conjugation** — liver adds glucuronic acid to make conjugated (direct) bilirubin.
6. **Excretion** — conjugated bilirubin goes into bile → intestine → faeces (urobilinogen, stercobilin).

## Types of jaundice

| Type | Where the problem is | Main cause | Urine colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-hepatic | Before liver | Excess RBC breakdown (haemolysis) | Dark |
| Hepatic | In the liver | Hepatitis, cirrhosis | Dark |
| Post-hepatic | After liver | Bile duct obstruction (stone, tumour) | Dark, pale stools |

- **Unconjugated (indirect) bilirubin** — not water-soluble; bound to albumin; cannot pass in urine.
- **Conjugated (direct) bilirubin** — water-soluble; can pass in urine (dark urine).

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Jaundice assessment** — check the sclera (eyes), skin and urine colour; report yellowing.
- **Newborn jaundice** — common in neonates; phototherapy may be needed; monitor bilirubin levels.
- **Hepatitis** — watch for fatigue, nausea, dark urine and right upper abdominal pain.
- **Bile duct obstruction** — pale stools and dark urine suggest blockage; report.
- **Patient teaching** — avoid alcohol in liver disease; follow prescribed diet and medicines.

## 60-second revision

- Haemoglobin → biliverdin → unconjugated bilirubin → liver → conjugated → bile → faeces.
- Normal bilirubin: 0.3–1.2 mg/dL.
- Unconjugated = indirect, not in urine; Conjugated = direct, in urine.
- Pre-hepatic = breakdown; Hepatic = liver cell; Post-hepatic = blockage.
- Dark urine + pale stools = obstructive jaundice.
