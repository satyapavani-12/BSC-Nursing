## In simple words

Proteins are made of amino acids joined by peptide bonds. They build and repair tissues, make enzymes, hormones and antibodies, and maintain fluid balance.

Protein is digested in the stomach by pepsin and in the small intestine by pancreatic proteases and brush-border enzymes. Amino acids are absorbed into the blood.

## Types of amino acids

| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | Must come from food | Lysine, methionine, valine, leucine, isoleucine, threonine, tryptophan, phenylalanine, histidine |
| Non-essential | Body can make them | Alanine, glycine, serine |

## Protein digestion and absorption

- **Stomach**: pepsin breaks proteins into smaller peptides.
- **Small intestine**: trypsin, chymotrypsin and carboxypeptidase (from pancreas) plus aminopeptidases (brush border) split peptides into amino acids.
- **Absorption**: amino acids are absorbed through villi into blood capillaries and go to the liver via the portal vein.

## Protein-energy malnutrition

| Condition | Main feature |
|---|---|
| Marasmus | Severe deficiency of both energy and protein; marked wasting |
| Kwashiorkor | Protein deficiency with relatively adequate energy; oedema, skin changes |

Signs: wasting, oedema, poor growth, apathy, hair changes, skin lesions.

<Callout type="must" title="Protein-energy malnutrition">
  **Marasmus = deficiency of both energy and protein (wasting). Kwashiorkor = protein deficiency with oedema.** Both need careful nutritional rehabilitation.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **High-protein diet** — encourage for wound healing, burns, surgery and infection, unless restricted.
- **Malnutrition screening** — assess weight, muscle wasting and oedema; refer to dietetics.
- **Tube feeding** — protein-containing feeds are given as prescribed; monitor tolerance.
- **Oedema** — low protein (hypoalbuminaemia) can cause oedema; report and review.
- **Patient teaching** — explain protein-rich foods: dal, pulses, milk, eggs, fish, meat, soya.

## 60-second revision

- Proteins = amino acids + peptide bonds.
- Essential vs non-essential amino acids.
- Digestion: pepsin → proteases → amino acids.
- Absorbed via villi to liver.
- Marasmus = energy + protein deficiency; Kwashiorkor = protein deficiency with oedema.
- Protein foods: dal, pulses, milk, eggs, fish, meat.
