## In simple words

Immunoglobulins (antibodies) are Y-shaped proteins made by plasma cells. They bind to foreign substances (antigens) and help destroy them. They are the basis of humoral immunity.

There are five main classes: IgG, IgA, IgM, IgE and IgD. Each has a different role and location in the body.

## Classes of immunoglobulins

| Type | Main location | Main function |
|---|---|---|
| IgG | Blood, tissues | Most abundant; crosses placenta; long-term protection |
| IgA | Secretions — saliva, tears, breast milk, gut | Protects mucosal surfaces |
| IgM | Blood | First antibody in response; largest |
| IgE | Skin, lungs, gut | Allergy and parasite defence |
| IgD | B cell surface | B cell receptor |

## Structure and function

An antibody has:
- Two heavy chains and two light chains.
- Two antigen-binding sites (the tips of the Y).
- A constant region (the stem) that determines the class and effector function.

Functions:
- Neutralise toxins and viruses.
- Opsonise bacteria for phagocytosis.
- Activate complement.
- Cross placenta (IgG only) to protect the newborn.

## Clinical significance

- **IgG** — indicates past infection or vaccination; crosses placenta giving newborn passive immunity.
- **IgM** — indicates recent or current infection (first responder).
- **IgA** — protects respiratory and gut mucosa; low IgA causes recurrent infections.
- **IgE** — raised in allergy and parasitic infection.

  **IgG is the only antibody that crosses the placenta**, giving the newborn passive immunity for the first months of life.
</Callout>

## Where the nurse uses this

- **Vaccination** — vaccines stimulate antibody production; explain the schedule to parents.
- **Newborn care** — breastfeeding transfers IgA and IgG, protecting the infant.
- **Allergy** — raised IgE is linked to allergy; watch for reactions.
- **Infection** — IgM suggests recent infection; IgG suggests past infection or immunity.
- **Immunodeficiency** — recurrent infections may suggest low immunoglobulins; report.

- IgG = most abundant, crosses placenta.
- IgA = mucosal protection.
- IgM = first response.
- IgE = allergy and parasites.
